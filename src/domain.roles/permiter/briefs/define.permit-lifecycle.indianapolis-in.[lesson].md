# define.permit-lifecycle.indianapolis-in

## .what

in Indianapolis, work cannot legally begin until a permit is **issued** — not just filed or approved.

## .why this matters

- contractors who say "I'll start work and pull the permit later" propose unpermitted work
- even a filed application does not authorize commencement of work
- homeowners bear liability for work that starts before permit issuance

---

## permit lifecycle

| stage | what it means | work can begin? |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **application filed** | submitted to DBNS, in queue for review | ❌ NO |
| **under review** | divisions check plans for code compliance | ❌ NO |
| **approved** | all divisions have signed off | ❌ NO |
| **issued** | permit document ready, fees paid | ✅ YES |

---

## verbatim from Indianapolis Homeowner's Permit Guide

> "The permits will be issued when approved by each division (Infrastructure, Permit Issuance)."
>
> "Once a permit is issued, a copy of the permit must be displayed in a way that it is visible from the street."
>
> "Construction cannot begin until the Structural permit is issued."

source: City of Indianapolis Homeowner's Permit Guide, Department of Business and Neighborhood Services

## timeline expectations

| permit type | typical issuance time |
|-------------|-----------------------|
| simple electrical (outlet, fixture) | same day to 5 business days |
| panel upgrade | ~1 week |
| whole-house rewire | 1-2 weeks (plans required) |
| structural work | 1-2 weeks |

note: complex projects that require plan review take longer, but **to file is quick** — often same-day.

---

## license verification

the electrical permit application (form dated 7/14/23) requires:

| field | section |
|-------|---------|
| Business License Number | P |
| Applicant License Number | P |

the form includes:

> "I AFFIRM, UNDER PENALTIES OF PERJURY, THAT THE FOREGOING REPRESENTATIONS ARE TRUE."

**license is collected at application** — not at approval. whether DBNS validates in real-time at submission vs. during review is not documented, but:
- they maintain a licensed contractor database
- the perjury clause means false info = criminal liability
- an invalid license would block issuance

**practical effect**: a contractor cannot file for someone else without a valid license number on record.

---

## the "permit delay" excuse

some contractors claim permit delays justify work before approval. this is false for Indianapolis because:

1. **to file is fast** — application can be submitted same-day online or in-person
2. **simple electrical permits** are often issued within 1-5 business days
3. **no provisional permit** exists in Indianapolis for residential electrical work
4. **contractor bears liability** for work started before issuance per Sec. 536-201

---

## emergency exception

Indianapolis does not have a documented emergency permit exception for residential electrical work. compare with:

- **Oregon**: emergency electrical work in industrial plants allowed if permit filed within 5 days
- **Washington**: emergency like-in-kind repairs allowed if permit obtained next business day

**Indianapolis has no such provision** — all electrical work requires permit issuance before commencement.

---

## consequences of work before issuance

| consequence | description |
|-------------|-------------|
| stop-work order | inspector can halt all work |
| fines | per Sec. 536-201 violation |
| permit denial | application may be rejected |
| insurance issues | unpermitted work may void coverage |
| resale problems | disclosure required, buyer may demand retrofit |

---

## red flags for homeowners

| what contractor says | what it means |
|----------------------|---------------|
| "I'll pull the permit later" | unpermitted work |
| "I'll start while we wait for approval" | unpermitted work (Indianapolis) |
| "Permits take too long, we'll sort it out" | unpermitted work |
| "Homeowner pulls permit, I do work" | may be legal if you pass exam, but shifts liability to you |
