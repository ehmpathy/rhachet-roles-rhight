=== correspondence, 2026-08-31 — the Franchise Tax Section, on SEQUENCE and CHANNEL ===

⚠️ not an agency publication. a private email, reproduced in full because two
published briefs turn on its exact words: it states the serial order of the
amended reports, and it draws the channel boundary that a reader can otherwise
misread as a registered-agent requirement. the sender is the same Division
clerk and is NOT identified here, nor is the entity or its file number. the
double spaces are hers and are preserved.

⭐ read the last sentence carefully. it is conditioned on "If you need
assistance" and offers the information center and the registered agent as
ALTERNATIVES. it is a statement about what the Franchise Tax Section does not
do, never a requirement that an agent file for you.

The wording looks good.  Please have notarized and resent.   Once receives you
may go online and file an amended 2022 report to list the verified assets and
issued shares.  Then, once filed proceed with filing an amended 2023 report with
the correct total issued shares.

After all taxes are paid you can file a Certificate of Renewal.  If you need
assistance please contact the information center or your registered agent as I
do not process filings.

⚠️ "Certificate of Renewal" is HER word. the statute and the Division's own
form both call it a Certificate of Revival (8 Del. C. § 312). the two name the
same instrument; a search for "renewal" in the corporate forms list returns
none.
