# howto.reinstate-void-delaware-corp

## .what

step-by-step guide to reinstate a Delaware corporation after it has been voided for tax delinquency.

⚠️ the secondary sources below are registered-agent and formation vendors, each of which sells the cure. where a vendor and the statute disagree, the statute governs — and on the void trigger they do disagree (see below).

---

## .why corporations get voided

| trigger | status | statute |
|---------|--------|---------|
| a missed due date | $200 penalty + 1.5%/month interest | §502(c), §504(c) |
| **1 year** unpaid, **or** a complete annual report not filed | charter **VOID**, powers inoperative | **§510** |
| the same 1-year delinquency, reported to the Governor on or before **June 30** | charter **REPEALED by proclamation** | ⭐ **§511** |

- ⛔ the vendor line *"two years in a row"* is wrong — §510 is **1 year**, and ch. 5 holds no 3-year rule
- ⛔ §511's **June 30** report to the Governor runs **before** §510's November 30 notice, and carries no cure window in its own text
- ✅ §312(b) revives a §511 repeal too, **"at any time"**; only a §284 forfeiture is carved out

the §510 / §511 / §312(b) / §513 quotes, the two-date sequence, and what void
does and does not end: `delaware.franchise.tax.delinquency.md` § *.void status*.

---

## .the good news

1. **the revival price is fixed at the VOID-DATE balance** — ⚠️ not because the tax stops. see below · **8 Del. C. §312(g)**
2. ⭐ **you can recalculate, and it is a STATUTORY RIGHT** — §502(c) lets the corporation *"thereafter elect to compute the franchise tax in the manner prescribed by § 503(a)(2) … by filing the annual franchise tax report."* ⚠️ **the report is the instrument, not the phone call** · **8 Del. C. §502(c)**, quoted whole under *the root cause* below
3. **full restoration** — once revived, "it is as if the company was never voided"

> "Once approved, the business entity is restored—it is as if the company was never voided or cancelled."
>
> — [Harvard Business Services](https://www.delawareinc.com/blog/my-delaware-corporation-was-voided-now-what/)

the statute says it more strongly — see §312(e) in `delaware.franchise.tax.delinquency.md`.

### ⛔ the tax does NOT stop while void — the revival price is what is capped

the tax is imposed on a **class of corporations**, and a void does not remove a corporation from it:

> "…and **every corporation now existing or hereafter to be incorporated** under
> the laws of this State, **shall pay an annual tax**, for the use of the State,
> by way of license for the corporate franchise"
>
> — [8 Del. C. §501(a)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html)

> "**Annually on or before March 1, every corporation now existing or hereafter
> incorporated** under Chapter 1 of this title or which has accepted the
> Constitution of this State, **shall make an annual franchise tax report**"
>
> — [8 Del. C. §502(a)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html)

neither carries a carve-out for a void charter, and a §510 void is **not a dissolution** (`accountant/…/define.void-charter-vs-federal-nol.md`, from §275). what caps your cost is §312(g), which prices the **revival** at the sum *"due **at the time** … became forfeited or void"* — the balance **as of the void date**. §312(g) is quoted whole at step 3, § *⭐ .what must be paid is BOUNDED*.

| the claim | verdict |
|---|---|
| "you don't write a check for the void years" | ✅ **true** — §312(g) fixes the sum at the void-date balance |
| "the tax stops accruing" | ⛔ **false** — §501 and §502 impose on the class throughout |

§312(g) itself supplies three reasons the distinction matters:

1. **the revival payment does NOT cover the revival year.** §312(g) closes with *"**No payment made pursuant to this subsection shall reduce the amount of franchise tax due under Chapter 5 of this title for the year in which the revival is effected**."* you owe the revival sum **plus** the current year.
2. **a §204 certificate of validation re-opens the whole window.** §312(g): if one *"relates to a time during which the corporation's certificate of incorporation had been forfeited or void, **the annual reports and annual franchise taxes that would have been required to be filed and paid during the period** … including interest thereon, **are required to be filed and paid**."* the void-period liability is dormant, not extinguished.
3. **good standing needs the void-period reports on file.** §502(f): the Secretary of State *"shall not issue certificates of good standing that pertain to any corporation that has an unpaid franchise tax balance … or does not have on file a completed annual franchise tax report **for the relevant time period**."*

### ⭐ §312(g)'s 5-YEAR ALTERNATIVE

void **more than 5 years**, and §312(g) substitutes **3 times** the annual tax for the revival year, computed at the then current rate (quoted at step 3). which way it cuts depends **entirely** on whether the annual tax was recalculated first:

| the annual tax at revival | back-tax path | the 5-year alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **$85,165** (Authorized Shares default) | 2 yrs ≈ $170,330 | 3 × $85,165 = **$255,495** — ⛔ far worse |
| **$400** (Assumed Par Value floor) | 2 yrs = $800 | 3 × $400 = **$1,200** — worse, but bounded |

⚠️ the alternative is a **ceiling that rises with your current-year rate**, not a discount. it is never a reason to delay, because the recalculation lowers **both** columns at once.

### 🚩 after a §511 proclamation, to operate is a crime

8 Del. C. §513: up to **$1,000** or **1 year**, or both, for whoever exercises powers under a proclaimed charter (quoted in `delaware.franchise.tax.delinquency.md` § *⭐ §511 — a HARSHER sanction, on the SAME trigger*). that reaches the founder personally — exactly the reader whose charter lapsed and who kept the business alive.

## ⭐ .the root cause, in the statute — §502(c)

**the recalculation is not a favor the clerk might grant. it is a statutory election, and the statute names the instrument.**

when a corporation does not file its annual report, the Secretary of State **computes the tax for you**, on the one method that needs no data from you — §503(a)(1), Authorized Shares — and bills it:

> "In case any corporation shall **fail to file** the annual franchise tax
> report … **the Secretary of State shall ascertain and fix the amount of the
> franchise tax as determined in the manner prescribed by § 503(a) of this
> title, and the amount so fixed** by the Secretary of State **shall stand as
> the basis of taxation** under the provisions of this chapter …
> **unless the corporation shall thereafter elect to compute the franchise tax
> in the manner prescribed by § 503(a)(2) of this title by filing the annual
> franchise tax report** and complying with the provisions of § 503(b) of this
> title."
>
> — [8 Del. C. § 502(c)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html)

| the clause | what it settles |
|---|---|
| "shall **fail to file** … the Secretary of State shall ascertain and fix" | the bill is an **assessment by default**, not a determination about you. no error was made |
| "as determined in the manner prescribed by § 503(a)" | that default is **method A**, the only method computable from the charter alone |
| "shall stand as the **basis of taxation**" | ⛔ it is legally operative and it **accrues**. silence does not shrink it |
| "**unless** the corporation shall **thereafter elect** … **by filing the annual franchise tax report**" | ⭐ the escape exists, it is **retroactive** ("thereafter"), and the **report IS the instrument** |

### ⭐ .§503(b) — the election has a CONDITION, and it names what the report must carry

§502(c) grants the election "by filing the annual franchise tax report **and complying with the provisions of § 503(b) of this title**." §503(b) is a **second, independent route back to method A**:

> "(b) **Unless** a corporation shall submit to the Secretary of State, at the
> time of filing its annual franchise tax report, **a statement setting forth
> the number of shares of each class of stock actually issued, if any, and the
> amount of the total gross assets** of the corporation, **as of the nearest
> date on which the amount is obtainable**, including in the statement its
> goodwill valued at the same amount at which it is valued in the books of
> account of the corporation, **it shall pay a franchise tax for such year
> computed in the manner prescribed by paragraph (a)(1) of this section**."
>
> — [8 Del. C. § 503(b)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html)

⛔ an incomplete statement produces no error message — it produces **method A**, silently. §502(c) defaults you to method A for a **missing report**; §503(b) for a **report missing its statement**.

| §503(b) demands, per year | why the detail matters |
|---|---|
| shares of each class **actually issued** | ⚠️ **issued**, never authorized. a report that restates the authorized count has not met (b) |
| "**if any**" | ⭐ the statute contemplates **zero** issued shares. a zero-shares corporation is inside (b), not outside it |
| **total gross assets** | ⚠️ **both** figures are required. shares alone does not satisfy (b) |
| "as of the **nearest date on which the amount is obtainable**" | 🚩 **read this with §503(i) below — (i) governs (b) and supplies a stricter date rule** |
| goodwill, "**valued at the same amount at which it is valued in the books**" | book value, never a fresh appraisal. the books govern |

⇒ this is why the clerk asks for a shares figure and an assets figure: the statement is what carries the election.

### ⛔ §503(i) — what "total gross assets" MEANS, and why a delinquent 1120 blocks the report

> "(i) As used in **subsections (a) and (b) of this section**, the term 'total
> assets' and the term 'total gross assets' are identical terms and **mean all
> assets of the corporation, net only of allowances for bad debts, accumulated
> depreciation, accumulated depletion, accumulated amortization of land and
> accumulated amortization of intangible assets.**
>
> Such total assets and total gross assets **shall be those 'total assets'
> reported to the United States on U.S. Form 1120 Schedule L, relative to the
> company's fiscal year ending in the calendar year prior to filing** with the
> Secretary of State pursuant to this section. ... **The Secretary of State may
> at any time require a true and correct copy of such schedule to be filed** with
> the Secretary of State's office."
>
> — [8 Del. C. § 503(i)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html)

**1. the figure is GROSS, and the netting list is closed.** **liabilities are not netted.** net assets or book equity is the wrong number.

**2. the date rule keys on the FILING date.** (i) governs (b). in the ordinary case a 2025 report filed in March 2026 uses the FY2025 schedule. for a delinquent filer an amended 2022 report filed in 2026 reads, literally, as FY2025. ⚠️ that literal reading is unlikely to be how it is administered, and no source here says how it is. **ask the Division which fiscal year each amended report should carry.**

**3. ⛔ for a year with no 1120 on file, the definition has NO referent.** *"reported to the United States"* is past tense — a filed return.

> 🎯 **so any delinquent 1120s are a PRECONDITION on the amended annual reports,
> not a parallel task.** the returns are owed anyway — 26 CFR §1.6012-2(a)(1)
> requires a 1120 *"regardless of whether it has taxable income"* — so the order
> only moves mandatory work to the front.

⚠️ **the report is sworn.** §502(b) puts the officer under penalty of perjury, §504(d) gives the Secretary inquiry power over *"the truth or falsity or accuracy of every report,"* and (i) lets the Secretary demand the schedule. a gross-assets figure filed ahead of its return is a sworn number with no document behind it.

⚠️ the Schedule L sentence of (i) is long enough that `rg` suppresses it as `[Omitted long matching line]`; assert decisive strings positively.

### ⛔ an amended report is owed per year — a call alone moves no balance

| act | what it actually does |
|---|---|
| the **call** | coordination. confirms the balance, the years, and the order of operations. **grants no election** |
| the **report, per year** | ⭐ **the election itself.** this is the statutory instrument §502(c) names |
| the **payment** | discharges the amount the election produced. worthless before it |

for a year where none was ever filed, the "amendment" is the **first** report that year ever had.

🚩 **open:** Delaware's portal and clerks say *amended*; §502(c) says only "the annual franchise tax report." whether a never-filed year is entered as **amended** or **original** is unsettled here — confirm on the step 1 call, per year. the **order** is settled either way: report before payment.

### 🎯 the skeptic's check — four doubts, each closed

| doubt | what closes it |
|---|---|
| "the calculator and the bill agree, so the bill is right" | ✅ **both are right.** method A *is* the correct default under §502(c). agreement confirms the default fired, never that the default is the final answer |
| "surely this is a data-entry error on their side" | ⛔ no. the statute **directs** the Secretary of State to fix the amount this way when a report is absent. it is designed behavior |
| "a phone call will fix it" | ⛔ the election is granted by the annual franchise tax report. a call cannot substitute for the named instrument |
| "it is too late — the years are closed" | ⛔ **"thereafter"** carries no deadline in the clause. and §312(e) revives the charter "as if the certificate of incorporation had at all times remained in full force" |

⭐ at a typical startup charter the gap is often **two orders of magnitude** — **$85,165.00** against **$400.00** per year below — and §502(c) crosses it for one report per delinquent year.

## .step 1: call Delaware to recalculate taxes

⚠️ the call is coordination; the **amended report is the statutory election.** a call that ends with no report filed has moved no balance.

**phone:** (302) 739-3073, option 3
**email:** DOSDOC_Ftax@delaware.gov

> "Contact the Franchise Tax Section prior to submitting the document for filing to determine the amount owed."
>
> — [Northwest Registered Agent](https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/corporation/delaware/reinstate)

**what to tell them:**
- your file number
- you want to recalculate back taxes via Assumed Par Value method
- your gross assets for each delinquent year
- your issued shares (not authorized)

**example recalculation:**

| item | Authorized Shares Method | Assumed Par Value Method |
|------|-------------------------|-------------------------|
| authorized shares | 10,000,000 | 10,000,000 |
| **stated par value** | — (not an input) | **$0.0001** |
| issued shares | — (not an input) | 1,000,000 |
| gross assets | — (not an input) | $50,000 |
| **annual tax** | **$85,165.00** | **$400.00** |

```sh
rhx franchise.tax.delaware --authorized 10000000 --par 0.0001 \
  --issued 1000000 --assets 50000
#   ├─ governs: assumed-par-value  →  $400.00 / year
#   ├─ vs method A = $84,765.00 avoided
#   └─ the tax holds at $400.00 for gross assets up to $100,000.00
```

- method A takes **only** the authorized share count — a revised assets figure cannot move it
- method B needs the **stated par**; without it there is no determinate answer (see `delaware.franchise.tax.purpose.md` for the branch)
- the sensitivity line: this corporation stays at $400.00 for **any** gross assets up to $100,000.00, so a late-surfacing balance under that line does not reopen the calculation

> "In most early-stage startup cases with millions of authorized shares but very limited assets, the Assumed Par Value Capital Method provides the lowest possible tax."
>
> — [Clerky](https://help.clerky.com/article/2796-calculate-delaware-franchise-tax)

## .step 3: pay all back taxes

### ⛔ .the amended reports come FIRST

the reflex is to clear the balance and sort the paperwork after. that discharges the **method A** amount — the number §502(c) says stands "**unless the corporation shall thereafter elect**."

| order | what you pay |
|---|---|
| amended report per year → **then** pay | the **method B** amount the election produced |
| pay → then amend | ⛔ the **method A** default. you funded the number you came to displace |

```
1. call            -> confirm the balance, the years, and the order   (coordination)
2. amended report  -> ⭐ THE ELECTION, one per delinquent year        (§502(c))
3. pay             -> discharge what the election produced
4. Certificate of Revival
```

⚠️ the numbered steps on this page run call → calculate → pay → revival; the amended reports belong between the call and the payment. field by field: `howto.fill-amended-annual-report.state=delaware`.

pay via:
- [corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)
- or by phone with Delaware

**payment requirements:**

| transaction amount | payment method |
|-------------------|----------------|
| under $5,000 | credit card or ACH |
| over $5,000 | ACH only |

### ⭐ .what must be paid is BOUNDED — §312(g), in full

> "Any corporation that revives its certificate of incorporation under this
> chapter shall pay to this State a sum equal to all franchise taxes, penalties
> and interest thereon **due at the time its certificate of incorporation became
> forfeited or void** pursuant to this title; **provided, however, that any
> corporation … whose certificate of incorporation has been forfeited or void
> for more than 5 years** shall, in lieu of the payment of the franchise taxes
> and penalties otherwise required by this subsection, pay a sum equal to **3
> times** the amount of the annual franchise tax that would be due … for the
> year in which the revival is effected … provided further, that the payment
> required by this subsection shall **not reduce** any annual franchise tax due
> for the year in which the revival is effected."
>
> — [8 Del. C. § 312(g)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc12/index.html)

| clause | effect |
|---|---|
| "due **at the time** … became … void" | ⭐ the sum is a **snapshot at the void date**, never a running total. years that fell due **after** the void are not named by this subsection |
| "**more than 5 years**" | the 3× substitution applies **only** past 5 years void. under that, you pay the ordinary sum — which for a method-B charter is far less than 3× |
| "shall **not reduce** … the year in which the revival is effected" | ⚠️ the **current** year's franchise tax is owed **separately**, on top |

⚠️ vendor paraphrases (*"All franchise taxes, penalties and interest that were due to the State at the time the corporation became void must be paid"*) carry the first clause only and drop the 5-year/3× substitution and the revival-year carve-out.

### ⛔ and if that sum is WRONG, § 505(b) is your only door — it shuts at step 4

§ 505 offers two paths, **bounded in different units**:

> "(a) If any corporation claims that the annual franchise tax or any penalties
> or interest were **erroneously or illegally fixed or paid** with respect to a
> calendar year, the corporation may, **not later than March 1 of the second
> calendar year following the close of such calendar year**, petition the
> Secretary of State for a reduction or refund of such tax, penalties or
> interest."
>
> "(b) **Prior to the filing of a certificate required by § 312(c)** of this
> title, a corporation may petition the Secretary of State for a reduction of
> taxes, penalties or interest which the State claims are due it **pursuant to
> § 312(g)** of this title and which the corporation claims have been
> erroneously or illegally fixed."
>
> — [8 Del. C. § 505(a)–(b)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html)

(b) names **§ 312(g) by number** — the purpose-built remedy for a void corporation that disputes its revival price.

| path | bounded by | on a multi-year delinquency |
|---|---|---|
| § 505(a) | a **calendar date** | ⛔ expired for every year older than ~2 |
| **§ 505(b)** | an **event** — the § 312(c) certificate | ✅ open until you file at **step 4** |

⚠️ **§ 312(c) is the certificate of revival** — *"executing, acknowledging and filing a certificate of revival in accordance with § 103"*. so step 4 is the act that closes (b). a delinquent corporation is normally past (a) before it learns (b) exists.

#### the remission power is NOT the Secretary's, for a taxable corporation

> "(c) … In the case of any corporation **which is not required to pay an annual
> tax under § 501(a)** of this title, the Secretary of State **may remit** all or
> part of the penalties and interest provided in this chapter."
>
> "(d) Any corporation, **within a period of 60 days** after the determination by
> the Secretary of State on a petition filed pursuant to subsections (a) and (b)
> of this section, may petition the **Court of Chancery** … for a **review de
> novo** of the determination of the Secretary of State."
>
> "(e) … **The Court of Chancery may remit all or part of the penalties and
> interest provided in § 502** of this title."
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 505(c)–(e)

| who | may remit penalties + interest | condition |
|---|---|---|
| Secretary of State — § 505(c) | ✅ | ⛔ **only** a § 501(a)-exempt corporation |
| **Court of Chancery — § 505(e)** | ✅ | **none stated** |

§ 501 settles who is exempt by a closed list:

> "(a) **Every corporation now existing or hereafter to be incorporated under the
> laws of this State shall pay an annual tax**, for the use of the State, by way
> of license for the corporate franchise…"
>
> "(b) **The following corporations are exempt** … (1) Corporations organized
> under Chapter 3 of Title 12; (2) Corporations **not organized for profit** and
> no part of the net earnings of which inures to the benefit of any private
> stockholder or individual; (3) Religious corporations; (4) Corporations for
> charitable purposes; (5) Corporations for educational purposes; (6) Civic
> organizations…"
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 501(a)–(b), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html

- a for-profit stock corporation matches none of the six, so **the Secretary has no power to waive its penalties** — a clerk who declines is correct
- budget the penalties as payable; § 505(e) via a § 505(d) appeal (**60 days**, **de novo**) is the only waiver route
- ⚠️ § 505(d) opens only *"on a petition filed pursuant to subsections (a) and (b)."* no petition, no appeal — so step 4 closes the Chancery route too
- what § 505(e) reaches: § 502(c)'s **$200** per year and § 504(c)'s interest at **1½% per month or portion thereof** — the terms that grow on a multi-year lapse

## ⛔ § 505(a)'s LAST SENTENCE names two instruments — both are traps, on different steps

> "**No refund shall be granted in connection with the filing of a certificate of
> correction under § 103(f) of this title or the filing of a certificate of
> validation under § 204 of this title.**"
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 505(a)

### § 103(f) — the certificate of correction, and why it lands on STEP 3

> "(f) Whenever any instrument authorized to be filed with the Secretary of State
> … **is an inaccurate record of the corporate action therein referred to**, or
> was defectively or erroneously executed … the instrument may be corrected by
> the filing … of a certificate of correction … **The corrected instrument shall
> be effective as of the date the original instrument was filed**"
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 103(f), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc01/index.html

an annual franchise tax report **is** such an instrument. read against § 505(a):

| order | outcome |
|---|---|
| correct → **then** pay | you pay the corrected figure. no refund is owed |
| pay → then correct | ⛔ the correction is retroactive **and the refund is barred.** the overpayment is gone |

⭐ **§ 103(f) is what makes a premature payment permanent.** § 505(a) bars a *refund*; § 505(b) petitions for a *reduction*. reduce before the money moves.

### § 204 — the certificate of validation, and why it lands on STEP 4

§ 204 ratifies a **defective corporate act** — one void or voidable for a *failure of authorization*, which § 204(h)(2)(ii) defines to reach

> "**the failure of the board of directors or any officer of the corporation to
> authorize or approve any act or transaction** taken by or on behalf of the
> corporation that would have required for its due authorization the approval of
> the board of directors or such officer"
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 204(h)(2), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc06/index.html

a sole-founder corporation with a thin minute book sits squarely inside that clause. and § 312(g)'s closing sentence makes § 204 the most expensive filing in the chapter — **keyed on the date of the act**:

> "**If the filing of a certificate of validation under § 204 of this title
> relates to a time during which** the corporation's certificate of incorporation
> had been forfeited or void, the annual reports and annual franchise taxes that
> would have been required to be filed and paid during the period in which the
> corporation's certificate of incorporation had been forfeited or void,
> including interest thereon, **are required to be filed and paid at the time of
> the filing of such certificate of validation**."
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 312(g)

| the ratified act happened… | what § 312(g) does |
|---|---|
| **before** the void | the re-opener does not fire — ⚠️ but see § 503(e) below |
| **while** void | ⛔ **the § 312(g) cap is surrendered** — every void-year report, every void-year tax, plus interest, all due at the filing |

§ 503(e) reaches the same certificate **keyed on the date you file**:

> "The **filing of a certificate of validation under § 204** of this title
> **shall not reduce** the annual franchise tax and interest due for **any
> period prior to the filing of such certificate of validation**, and **shall
> not entitle the corporation to a refund**, and any calculation of
> **additional** annual franchise tax and interest due for any period prior to
> the filing of such certificate of validation shall be calculated **at the
> current rates in effect under this section**."
>
> — 8 Del. C. § 503(e), https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c005/index.html

| provision | keys on | reach |
|---|---|---|
| **§ 312(g)** | the **act's** date | the void window only |
| **§ 503(e)** | the **certificate's** date | ⛔ **every prior period.** void or not |

- a validation of a pre-void act clears § 312(g) and is still barred by § 503(e): no reduction, no refund, and any *additional* tax repriced to **today's rates**
- ⛔ **no version of § 204 lowers a back-year franchise tax** — § 503(e), § 312(g), and § 505(a) each close a different door
- ⚠️ § 503(e) does not bar an **increase**; it expressly contemplates *"additional ... tax"*

for a void-window act you probably do not need § 204 at all, because § 312(e) validates it on revival at no charge:

```
an act done while void
  ├─ cured by § 312(e) on revival     -> $0
  └─ cured by a § 204 certificate     -> the ENTIRE void window, + interest
```

🚩 **open:** § 312(e) validates acts *"within the scope of its certificate of incorporation"* — language about corporate **power**. whether it also reaches a **failure of authorization** is not settled by the text pulled here. put that to counsel; do not derive it.

## .step 5: verify restoration

once processed — and at Priority 7 there is ⛔ **no published interval**, so call for the current processed date rather than a count of weeks (see `## .timeline summary`):

1. check status at [icis.corp.delaware.gov/ecorp/entitysearch](https://icis.corp.delaware.gov/ecorp/entitysearch/namesearch.aspx)
2. status should show "Good Standing" (not "Void")
3. obtain a good-standing certificate if needed — ⚠️ **short form $50.00, long form $175.00.** fresh out of a void, the **long** form is usually what a bank or investor wants, because it carries the history of prior filings. a **special certificate** ($50.00) that references the void specifically is a third, cheaper option

### ⭐ .and restore your OWN record — the § 211(c) duty nobody bills you for

> "(c) A failure to hold the annual meeting at the designated time or to elect
> a sufficient number of directors to conduct the business of the corporation
> **shall not affect otherwise valid corporate acts or work a forfeiture or
> dissolution of the corporation** except as may be otherwise specifically
> provided in this chapter. If the annual meeting for election of directors is
> not held on the date designated therefor **or action by written consent to
> elect directors in lieu of an annual meeting has not been taken, the directors
> shall cause the meeting to be held as soon as is convenient.** If there be a
> failure ... for a period of **30 days** after the date designated for the
> annual meeting, or if no date has been designated, for a period of **13
> months** after the latest to occur of the organization of the corporation, its
> last annual meeting or the last action by written consent ..., **the Court of
> Chancery may summarily order a meeting to be held upon the application of any
> stockholder or director.**"
>
> — [8 Del. C. § 211(c)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc07/index.html),
> pulled as the subchapter VII container and sliced

| clause | what it does |
|---|---|
| *"shall not ... work a forfeiture or dissolution"* | ⭐ **a savings clause.** the annual meetings missed while void did **not** compound the void |
| *"the directors **shall** cause the meeting to be held as soon as is convenient"* | ⛔ **a live, mandatory duty** that survives the revival |

- ⚠️ **§ 312(i) makes the void period COUNT inside those windows**, so a corporation revived after a multi-year void is already past 13 months
- for a sole founder the risk is inert because only a stockholder or director may apply — one person. admit a second stockholder and that changes the same day
- **the discharge is free:** *"action by written consent to elect directors **in lieu of** an annual meeting"* — for a sole stockholder, one signature. no meeting, no notice, no quorum

| item | value |
|---|---|
| fee | **none** |
| filed with Delaware | ⛔ **no** — an internal record |
| where it lives | the minute book |
| when | after the revival lands |

§ 312(h) deems the board to be whoever *would* be directors but for the void, so a signer exists either way — but a post-revival date is the clean record.

⛔ skip it and the corporation is current on tax and restored on the register, with no record that a board was ever elected — a gap that surfaces in diligence, mid-raise, mid-sale, or mid-loan.

## .step 6: prevent future void (optional but recommended)

| action | benefit |
|--------|---------|
| reduce authorized shares to ≤5,000 | future tax = $175/year |

### ⚠️ these rows are COUPLED

March 1 is the whole answer for the tax only while the year's **estimated** liability stays under **$5,000** — **8 Del. ## .case study: $191K → ~$1.5K

the full record, pseudonymized: `corp.franchise.reinstate/.demo/v2020_05_21.sandpine/route/1.calculate.owed.yield.md`.

**before recalculation:**
- 10M authorized shares, 8M issued
- voided 2024-03-01
- Delaware's bill: $191,652.43
- annual assessment: $85,165 (Authorized Shares Method)

**after recalculation (Assumed Par Value):**
- gross assets $0 per Form 1120 Schedule L → **$400/yr**, the §503(a)(2) floor
- **§312(g)** fixes the revival sum at the **void-date** balance: the reports past due at void, tax years **2022** (due 2023-03-01) and **2023** (due 2024-03-01). years after void are not in the revival price. ⚠️ a cap on the *revival price*, not a suspension of the tax; see `.the good news` above

✅ **computed, not hand-tallied** — `rule.forbid.hand-computed-figures`. the open question is whether §504(c) interest stops at void, so both ends are shown:

```
rhx franchise.tax.delaware.accrue --repo rhight --role accountant \
  --tax 400 --years 2022,2023 --asof 2024-03-01   # stops at void
rhx franchise.tax.delaware.accrue --repo rhight --role accountant \
  --tax 400 --years 2022,2023 --asof 2026-03-23   # runs to as-of
```

| item | stops at void | runs to 2026-03-23 | source |
|------|--------------:|-------------------:|--------|
| tax, 2022 + 2023 (recalculated) | **$800.00** | **$800.00** | §503(a)(2) APV floor |
| §502(c) late-report penalty, $200 × 2 | **$400.00** | **$400.00** | ✅ skill |
| §504(c) interest | **$72.00** | **$372.00** | ✅ skill |
| subtotal | **$1,272.00** | **$1,572.00** | ✅ skill |
| Certificate of Revival | **$189.00** | **$189.00** | ⭐ DE fee schedule, rev. 2026-08-01 |
| **total** | **$1,461.00** | **$1,761.00** | |

⚠️ interest runs from each year's **due date**, not the void month: §504(a) puts a below-$5,000 liability due *"on March 1 **following the close of** the calendar year"*. at $400 there are no §504(a) installments, so the $85,165 method's largest interest line (the June 1 installment) has no counterpart.

⚠️ **the total is incomplete by design.** §312(g) closes with *"No payment made pursuant to this subsection shall reduce the amount of franchise tax due under Chapter 5 of this title for the year in which the revival is effected"* — so a revival in 2026 owes **2026's tax as well**, on top of the total above.

**avoided: ~$190,000 (~99%)** — against the **$191,652.43 bill**. the State fixes the final number; §505(b) petitions reduce *"taxes, penalties or interest"* together.
