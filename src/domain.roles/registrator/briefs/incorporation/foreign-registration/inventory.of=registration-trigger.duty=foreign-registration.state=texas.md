# inventory.of=registration-trigger.duty=foreign-registration.state=texas

> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md) · depth: [`hazard.foreign-registration.state=texas.md`](./hazard.foreign-registration.state=texas.md)

## .answer

**before** it transacts business in Texas, a foreign corporation must register with the Secretary of State. the duty attaches at day one; a **90-day grace** applies to the late fee only — after it, $750 per whole or partial calendar year unregistered.

## .the trigger

> "(a) To transact business in this state, a foreign entity must register under this chapter if the entity:"
>
> — BOC §9.001(a), `refs/effectiveAt=2007-09-01.tx-boc-9.txt`

> "(b) A foreign entity described by Subsection (a) must maintain the entity's registration while transacting business in this state."
>
> — BOC §9.001(b)

"transact business" has no affirmative definition; the analysis runs through the §9.251 list (hazard brief).

## .what does NOT trigger it

| BOC §9.251 | activity (condensed) |
|---|---|
| (1)–(2) | a suit or claim; meetings and internal affairs |
| (3) | a bank account |
| (4) | an office **only for** securities transfer |
| (5) | vote an interest it holds |
| (6) | a sale through an independent contractor |
| (7)–(8), (12) | debt, mortgages, security interests; collect a debt |
| (9) | interstate commerce |
| (10) | an isolated transaction done within 30 days, not one of a series |
| (11), (13)–(14), (16) | estate powers; passive mineral interests; a director or officer of a registered entity |
| (15) | own property **without more** |

§9.252 makes the list non-exclusive — in both directions.

## .the deadline once triggered

> "A foreign entity that has transacted business in Texas for more than ninety days without registering is subject to a late filing fee. The late filing fee is equal to the registration fee for each full or partial calendar year that the foreign entity transacted business in Texas without being registered. $750*"
>
> — Tex. SoS fee schedule, `../refs/pulledAt=2026-09-01.tx-sos-fee-schedule.txt`

> "All foreign entities are given a 90 day grace period to register with the secretary of state after initially transacting business in Texas. An entity that registers during the grace period will not be charged late filing fees."
>
> — Tex. SoS, *Foreign Entities FAQs*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-08-30.tx-sos-foreign-faq.txt`

⚠️ compute a late fee with the SoS calculator, never by hand (`rule.forbid.hand-computed-figures`).

## .if it was missed

> "The entity cannot maintain an action, suit, or proceeding in a Texas court until it registers."
>
> — Tex. SoS, *Foreign Entities FAQs*

- BOC §9.052: civil penalty = all fees and taxes owed had it registered and filed all reports, plus penalties and interest — the attorney general's to recover, uncapped
- BOC §9.051(c): contracts stay valid; the shield holds
- the five-year late-fee cap needs a clean Comptroller status first — see the hazard brief
