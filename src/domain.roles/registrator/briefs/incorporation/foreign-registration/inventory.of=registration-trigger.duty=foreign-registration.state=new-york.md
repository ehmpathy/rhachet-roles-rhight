> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md)

## .answer

**before** it does business in New York, a foreign corporation must be authorized by the Department of State (an application for authority, BCL §1304). no grace period and no late-registration fee appear in Article 13; the cost of delay is a court bar until authority **and** all back taxes are paid.

## .the trigger

> "(a) A foreign corporation shall not do business in this state until it has been authorized to do so as provided in this article."
>
> — N.Y. BCL §1301(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-bsc-1301.txt`

"do business" has no affirmative definition in the BCL; §1301(b) lists only what does not count, and the list is short.

## .what does NOT trigger it

| BCL §1301(b) | activity |
|---|---|
| (1) | maintain or defend an action, or settle claims or disputes |
| (2) | hold meetings of directors or shareholders |
| (3) | maintain bank accounts |
| (4) | offices or agencies **only** for securities transfer; trustees or depositaries |

> "(b) Without excluding other activities which may not constitute doing business in this state"
>
> — N.Y. BCL §1301(b)

⚠️ four rows, against eleven to sixteen in the other four states. no isolated-transaction, independent-contractor, interstate-commerce, or property-without-more row is in the statute; whether a court treats them as excluded is case law, not pulled.

## .the deadline once triggered

> "Application for Authority (Foreign) $225"
>
> — NY Department of State fee schedule, BCL table (Rev. 3/16), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-dos-fee-schedule-bcl.txt`

## .if it was missed

> "(a) A foreign corporation doing business in this state without authority shall not maintain any action or special proceeding in this state unless and until such corporation has been authorized to do business in this state and it has paid to the state all fees and taxes imposed under the tax law or any related statute, as defined in section eighteen hundred of such law, as well as penalties and interest charges related thereto, accrued against the corporation."
>
> — N.Y. BCL §1312(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-bsc-1312.txt`

- §1312(b): contracts stay valid; it may still defend
- §1303: the attorney general may sue to restrain it from business done without authority (`refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-bsc-1303.txt`)
