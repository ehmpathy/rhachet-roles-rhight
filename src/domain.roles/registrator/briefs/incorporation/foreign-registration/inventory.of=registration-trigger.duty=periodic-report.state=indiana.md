# inventory.of=registration-trigger.duty=periodic-report.state=indiana

> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md)

## .answer

once registered: a **business entity report every two years**, the first due two years after registration, in the month of registration (to the end of that month). $20 online, $50 on paper. 60 days past due → grounds to revoke.

## .the trigger

> "(a) A domestic filing entity or registered foreign entity shall deliver to the secretary of state for filing a biennial report"
>
> — IC 23-0.5-2-13(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ic-23-0.5-2-13.txt`

> "(c) The biennial report must be delivered to the secretary of state for filing every two (2) calendar years on a schedule determined by the secretary of state. The secretary of state may accept biennial reports during the ninety (90) days before the month in which the biennial report is due."
>
> — IC 23-0.5-2-13(c)

the Secretary of State's schedule:

> "First Report: The first report is due two years after the business is formed or registered."
>
> "Due Date: The month and day the business was formed or registered. You have until the end of the month before the report is considered past due."
>
> — INBiz, *Business Entity Reports*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.in-inbiz-business-entity-report.txt`

## .when none is owed

| condition | cite |
|---|---|
| not a "registered foreign entity" | IC 23-0.5-2-13(a) |
| the first two years after registration | INBiz, above |

## .the deadline once triggered

> "(1) Twenty dollars ($20), in the case of a for-profit entity."
>
> — IC 23-0.5-9-34(a), electronic; $50 on paper per (b), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ic-23-0.5-9-34.txt`

no statutory late fee was found; the sanction is revocation.

## .if it was missed

> "(2) the entity does not deliver to the secretary of state for filing, not later than sixty (60) days after the due date, a biennial report;"
>
> — IC 23-0.5-5-11(a)(2), a ground to revoke a foreign registration, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ic-23-0.5-5-11.txt`

- (c): the notice sets a revocation date at least 60 days out; (d): authority ceases on that date unless each ground is cured
- once revoked, the court bar of IC 23-0.5-5-2(b) applies again
- INBiz: reinstatement first needs a Department of Revenue tax clearance, which "can take 4-6 weeks to process"
