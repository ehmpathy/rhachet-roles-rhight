# inventory.of=registration-trigger.duty=periodic-report.state=florida

> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md)

## .answer

once it holds a certificate of authority: an **annual report every year, Jan 1 – May 1**, the first one in the calendar year **after** the certificate. $150; after May 1 a $400 late charge applies; no report by the third Friday in September → revocation.

## .the trigger

> "(1) Each domestic corporation and each foreign corporation authorized to transact business in this state shall deliver to the department for filing an annual report"
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.1622(1), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-04.fl-stat-607.1622.txt`

> "(4) The first annual report must be delivered to the department between January 1 and May 1 of the year following the calendar year in which a domestic corporation's articles of incorporation became effective or a foreign corporation obtained its certificate of authority to transact business in this state. Subsequent annual reports must be delivered to the department between January 1 and May 1 of each calendar year thereafter."
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.1622(4)

## .when none is owed

| condition | cite |
|---|---|
| no certificate of authority held — the duty attaches to a corporation "authorized to transact business" | §607.1622(1) |
| the calendar year the certificate was obtained — the first report is the year after | §607.1622(4) |
| the business has closed and withdrawn | Division of Corporations, below |

> "If my business has closed, do I still need to file the annual report? No. You do not need to file the annual report if the business has closed. File to dissolve or withdraw a Florida or Foreign Corporation or LLC."
>
> — Division of Corporations, *File Annual Report*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-04.fl-sunbiz-annual-report.txt`

## .the deadline once triggered

> "Profit Annual Report (& Supplemental Fee) $ 150.00 Profit Annual Report (Received after May 1) $ 550.00"
>
> — Division of Corporations, *Corporate Fees*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-04.fl-sunbiz-corporate-fees.txt`

> "a late charge of $400 shall be imposed if the supplemental corporate fee is remitted after May 1"
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.193(2)(b), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-04.fl-stat-607.193.txt`

the $150 is §607.0122(17) $61.25 + §607.193 $88.75, per the statute's own figures.

## .if it was missed

> "(a) The foreign corporation does not deliver its annual report to the department by 5 p.m. Eastern Time on the third Friday in September of each year"
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.1530(1)(a), a ground to revoke the certificate, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-04.fl-stat-607.1530.txt`

- §607.1622(6): no action may be maintained in a Florida court until the report is filed and all fees and penalties are paid
- the Division revokes at close of business on the fourth Friday of September; reinstatement costs the reinstatement fee plus each year's report fee (`fl-sunbiz-annual-report.txt`)
- the Division page states no waiver of the $400; it is a statutory charge
