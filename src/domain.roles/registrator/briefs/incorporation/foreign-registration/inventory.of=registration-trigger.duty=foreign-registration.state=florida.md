# inventory.of=registration-trigger.duty=foreign-registration.state=florida

> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md) · depth: [`hazard.foreign-registration.state=florida.md`](./hazard.foreign-registration.state=florida.md)

## .answer

**before** a foreign corporation transacts business in Florida, it must hold a certificate of authority. no grace period appears in the text; the duty attaches at the first act of business.

## .the trigger

> "(1) A foreign corporation may not transact business in this state until it obtains a certificate of authority from the department."
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.1501(1), `refs/effectiveAt=2024-01-01.fl-stat-607.1501.txt`

"transact business" is defined only negatively, by the (2) list below. the list is non-exhaustive (§607.1501(3)), and it does not decide taxation (§607.1501(4)).

## .what does NOT trigger it

| §607.1501(2) | activity (condensed) |
|---|---|
| (a) | maintain, defend, mediate, arbitrate, or settle a suit or claim |
| (b) | internal affairs — shareholder or board meetings |
| (c) | accounts in financial institutions |
| (d) | an office or agency **for securities transfer** only |
| (e) | sales through independent contractors |
| (f) | solicit orders that require acceptance **outside** Florida |
| (g)–(h) | create debt or security interests; collect debts |
| (i) | business in interstate commerce |
| (j) | an isolated transaction done within 30 days, not one of a series |
| (k)–(l) | own a subsidiary; a passive LP interest |
| (m) | own property **without more** |

⛔ no row covers an office the corporation runs its business from — see the hazard brief.

## .the deadline once triggered

no statutory late fee for the application itself. the fees:

> "(14) Application for certificate of authority to transact business in this state by a foreign corporation: $35."
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.0122(14), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-04.fl-stat-607.0122.txt`

plus a registered agent designation, §607.0122(5), $35.

## .if it was missed

> "A foreign corporation transacting business in this state or its successors may not prosecute or maintain an action or proceeding in this state until it has obtained a certificate of authority"
>
> — Fla. Stat. §607.1502(1), `refs/effectiveAt=2024-01-01.fl-stat-607.1502.txt`

- §607.1502(4): liable for all fees and penalties that would have been imposed for each year or part of a year, plus a court-ordered civil penalty of $500–$1,000 per year or part
- §607.1502(5)–(6): contracts stay valid; the shareholder/officer shield holds
- ⚠️ never compute the exposure by hand (`rule.forbid.hand-computed-figures`); the part-year round-up is the trap
