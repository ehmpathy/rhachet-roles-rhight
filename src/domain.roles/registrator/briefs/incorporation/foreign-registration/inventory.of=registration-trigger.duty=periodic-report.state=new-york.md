# inventory.of=registration-trigger.duty=periodic-report.state=new-york

> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md)

## .answer

once authorized: a **biennial statement every two years**, in the calendar month the application for authority was filed. $9. past due after 30 days; delinquent two years on, cured only by the statement plus a $250 fine.

## .the trigger

> "1. Except as provided in paragraph eight of this section, each domestic corporation, and each foreign corporation authorized to do business in this state, shall, during the applicable filing period as determined by subdivision three of this section, file a statement"
>
> — N.Y. BCL §408(1), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-bsc-408.txt`

> "3. Except as provided in paragraph eight of this section, for the purpose of this section the applicable filing period for a corporation shall be the calendar month during which its original certificate of incorporation or application for authority were filed or the effective date thereof if stated."
>
> — N.Y. BCL §408(3)

## .when none is owed

| condition | cite |
|---|---|
| not "authorized to do business" in New York | §408(1) |
| a farm corporation | §408(5) |
| the statement rides an Article 9-A tax report instead, if the tax commissioner and the Secretary of State agree | §408(8) |

> "However, each corporation required to file a statement under this section must continue to file the biennial statement required by this section with the department of state until the corporation in fact has filed a tax report with the department of taxation and finance that includes all required information."
>
> — N.Y. BCL §408(8)(b)

## .the deadline once triggered

> "The fee for filing a Biennial Statement for a business corporation or LLC is $9."
>
> — NY Department of State, *Biennial Statements*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-dos-biennial-statements.txt`

> "A Biennial Statement should not be filed prior to the calendar month in which the Biennial Statement is due."
>
> — same page

## .if it was missed

> "1. Each corporation which has failed to file its statement within the time required by this chapter after thirty days shall be shown to be past due on the records of the department of state."
>
> — N.Y. BCL §409(1), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ny-bsc-409.txt`

- §409(2): two years unfiled → delinquent 60 days after a mailed notice; removed on the current statement plus "a fine of two hundred fifty dollars"
- DOS: a past-due status shows on any certificate of status and "may prevent the corporation or LLC from completing certain business transactions"
