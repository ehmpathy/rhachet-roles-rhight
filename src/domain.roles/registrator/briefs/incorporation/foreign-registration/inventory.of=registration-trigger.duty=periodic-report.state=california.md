## .answer

once qualified: a **Statement of Information within 90 days** of the statement and designation, **then every year** in a six-month window that ends with the month of original qualification. $25. unfiled after notice → a $250 FTB penalty, then suspension.

⚠️ **annual, not biennial**, for a stock corporation — the biennial cycle is the nonprofit's.

## .the trigger

> "(a) Every foreign corporation (other than a foreign association) qualified to transact intrastate business shall file, within 90 days after the filing of its original statement and designation of foreign corporation and annually thereafter during the applicable filing period, on a form prescribed by the Secretary of State, a statement containing all of the following:"
>
> — Cal. Corp. Code §2117(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-corp-2117.txt`

§2117(f) adopts §1502(d), with "articles" read as the §2105 statement:

> "(d) For the purposes of this section, the applicable filing period for a corporation shall be the calendar month during which its original articles were filed and the immediately preceding five calendar months."
>
> — Cal. Corp. Code §1502(d), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-corp-1502.txt`

## .when none is owed

| condition | cite |
|---|---|
| not "qualified to transact intrastate business" | §2117(a) |
| a foreign association | §2117(a) |
| no change since the last statement → a no-change statement may stand in for the full one (still a submission) | §1502(c), via §2117(f) |

## .the deadline once triggered

> "Statement of Information - Domestic and Foreign Stock: Due within 90 days of initial registration and every year thereafter. Form SI-550 (PDF) $25.00"
>
> — Cal. SoS, *Corporations – Statement of Information*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-sos-forms-statement-of-information.txt`

publicly traded foreign corporations also owe the §2117.1 disclosure statement, within 150 days of fiscal year end.

## .if it was missed

> "If the statement has not been filed timely, the entity is provided a notice of delinquency, and after 60 days from that notice, if no statement has been filed, the Secretary of State's office notifies the Franchise Tax Board, who assesses and collects the penalty."
>
> — Cal. SoS, business entities FAQ, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-sos-faq-penalty.txt`

- Rev. & Tax. Code §19141: on certification under Corp. Code §2204(a), the FTB "shall assess a penalty of two hundred fifty dollars ($250)" (`refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-rtc-19141.txt`)
- §2204(f): the SoS may waive for reasonable cause; the SoS takes requests by mail or its email waiver form
- §2205: a repeat failure leads to suspension of corporate powers rather than the penalty
