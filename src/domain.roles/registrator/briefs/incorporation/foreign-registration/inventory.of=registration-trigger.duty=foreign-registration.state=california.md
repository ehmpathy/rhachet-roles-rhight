## .answer

**before** it transacts **intrastate** business — repeated and successive transactions in California, other than interstate or foreign commerce — a foreign corporation must hold a certificate of qualification. no grace period; a court may assess $20 per day of willful unqualified business.

## .the trigger

> "(a) A foreign corporation shall not transact intrastate business without having first obtained from the Secretary of State a certificate of qualification."
>
> — Cal. Corp. Code §2105(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-corp-2105.txt`

> "(a) For the purposes of Chapter 21 (commencing with Section 2100), "transact intrastate business" means entering into repeated and successive transactions of its business in this state, other than interstate or foreign commerce."
>
> — Cal. Corp. Code §191(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-corp-191.txt`

⭐ the only one of the five states with an **affirmative** definition.

## .what does NOT trigger it

| Corp. Code §191 | activity (condensed) |
|---|---|
| (b) | its subsidiary transacts intrastate business; it is a shareholder, limited partner, or LLC member/manager |
| (c)(1)–(2) | a suit, arbitration, or settlement; board or shareholder meetings, internal affairs |
| (c)(3) | bank accounts |
| (c)(4) | offices or agencies for securities transfer |
| (c)(5) | sales through independent contractors |
| (c)(6) | solicit orders that require acceptance outside California |
| (c)(7) | create debt, mortgages, liens, security interests |
| (c)(8) | an isolated transaction done within **180 days**, not one of a series |
| (d) | a foreign lender's loan acquisition, inspection, and enforcement acts |

(c) and (d) open "Without excluding other activities" — non-exhaustive.

## .the deadline once triggered

> "Statement and Designation by Foreign Corporation - Stock Form S&DC-S/N (PDF) $100.00"
>
> — Cal. SoS, *Corporations – Foreign* forms and fees, `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-sos-forms-foreign-corporations.txt`

§2105(b): annex a certificate from the home-state official that it is an extant corporation in good order there.

## .if it was missed

> "(a) Any foreign corporation which transacts intrastate business and which does not hold a valid certificate from the Secretary of State may be subject to a penalty of twenty dollars ($20) for each day that unauthorized intrastate business is transacted"
>
> — Cal. Corp. Code §2203(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ca-corp-2203.txt`

- §2203(b): the days counted are those the court finds willful; the court sets the amount by size and willfulness
- §2203(c): no suit on intrastate business until it qualifies, pays a **$250** penalty plus the fees due for the statement and designation, and shows payment of all franchise and other taxes for the period
- ⚠️ the FTB nexus test for franchise tax is a different, lower test — see the accountant tax-trigger inventory
