## .answer

**before** it does business in Indiana, a foreign corporation must register with the Secretary of State. no grace period and no late-registration fee appear in IC 23-0.5-5; the exposure is a court bar and an attorney-general civil penalty of up to $10,000.

## .the trigger

> "(a) A foreign entity may not do business in Indiana until it registers with the secretary of state under this article."
>
> — IC 23-0.5-5-2(a), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ic-23-0.5-5-2.txt`

"do business" has no affirmative definition; IC 23-0.5-5-5 lists what does not count.

## .what does NOT trigger it

| IC 23-0.5-5-5(a) | activity (condensed) |
|---|---|
| (1)–(2) | a suit or claim; internal affairs and meetings |
| (3) | accounts in financial institutions |
| (4) | an office or agency for securities transfer |
| (5) | sales through independent contractors |
| (6) | solicit orders that require acceptance outside Indiana |
| (7)–(8) | loans, debt, security interests; collect debts |
| (9) | an isolated transaction done within 30 days, not one of a series |
| (10) | own property **without more** |
| (11) | business in interstate commerce |

> "(c) This section does not apply in determining the contacts or activities that may subject a foreign entity to service of process, taxation, or regulation under law of Indiana other than this article. (d) The list of activities in subsection (a) is not exhaustive"
>
> — IC 23-0.5-5-5(c)–(d), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ic-23-0.5-5-5.txt`

## .the deadline once triggered

> "(1) Seventy-five dollars ($75) for a for-profit entity."
>
> — IC 23-0.5-9-26(a), the fee for an electronic foreign registration statement; $125 on paper per (b), `refs/pulledAt=2026-10-05.ic-23-0.5-9-26.txt`

no late fee for registration was found in IC 23-0.5-5 or 23-0.5-9.

## .if it was missed

> "(b) A foreign entity doing business in Indiana may not maintain an action or proceeding in this state unless it is registered to do business in Indiana."
>
> — IC 23-0.5-5-2(b)

> "(f) A foreign entity is liable for a civil penalty of not more than ten thousand dollars ($10,000) if it transacts business in Indiana without a certificate of authority. The attorney general may collect all penalties due under this subsection."
>
> — IC 23-0.5-5-2(f)

- IC 23-0.5-5-2(c)–(d): contracts stay valid, it may still defend, and the owner/manager liability limit is not waived
