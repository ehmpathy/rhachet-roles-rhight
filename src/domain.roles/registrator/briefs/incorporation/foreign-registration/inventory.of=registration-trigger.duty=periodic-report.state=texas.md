> cell of [`inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md`](./inventory.of=registration-trigger._.md)

## .answer

a registered foreign **for-profit corporation files no Secretary of State periodic report**. the recurrent duty is the Comptroller's: the annual franchise tax report and its Public Information Report (PIR), which the Comptroller forwards to the Secretary of State.

## .the trigger

> "Foreign entities that are subject to state franchise taxes must file an annual franchise tax report with the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts. As part of the report, foreign corporations, professional associations, LLCs, and certain limited partnerships that are subject to franchise taxes must file a Public Information Report (PIR) that lists the names of their governing persons at the time the report is filed."
>
> — Tex. SoS, *Foreign Entities FAQs*, answer to *"Does a foreign entity that registers to transact business in Texas have to file an annual report with the secretary of state?"*, `refs/pulledAt=2026-08-30.tx-sos-foreign-faq.txt`

> "(b) The corporation, limited liability company, limited partnership, or professional association shall file the report once a year on a form prescribed by the comptroller. (c) The comptroller shall forward the report to the secretary of state."
>
> — Tex. Tax Code §171.203(b)–(c), `../../../../accountant/briefs/franchise-tax/state=texas/refs/effectiveAt=2024-01-01.tx-tax-171-reports.txt`

## .when none is owed

| condition | cite |
|---|---|
| a for-profit corporation — the SoS periodic report reaches nonprofits and certain LPs only | SoS FAQ, below |
| no franchise tax imposed on it (no nexus) — the PIR duty runs with the tax | Tax Code §171.203(a); see accountant |

> "Foreign nonprofit corporations and certain foreign limited partnerships that are not subject to franchise taxes are required to file a periodic report with this office not more than once every four years."
>
> — Tex. SoS, *Foreign Entities FAQs*

⚠️ **$0 tax is not "none owed."** §171.203(a) applies "regardless of whether the entity is required to pay any tax" — see accountant `franchise-tax/state=texas/hazard.zero-tax-still-owes-a-report`.

## .if it was missed

> "When a foreign entity fails to file franchise tax reports and/or pay franchise taxes, the secretary of state is authorized under the Texas Tax Code to forfeit its registration to transact business."
>
> — Tex. SoS, *Foreign Entities FAQs*

- reinstatement: every report filed, all tax/penalty/interest paid, Form 801 with a Comptroller tax clearance letter (same FAQ)
- officer liability under Tax Code §171.255 — see the accountant hazard brief
