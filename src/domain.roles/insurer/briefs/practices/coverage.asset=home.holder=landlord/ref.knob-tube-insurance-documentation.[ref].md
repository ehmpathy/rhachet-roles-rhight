# ref.knob-tube-insurance-documentation

## .what

verified documentation requirements for K&T insurance coverage, with sources.

## .why

carriers require specific proof before they bind K&T properties. this reference collects verified requirements with citations.

---

## electrician certification letter

### required elements

| element | source |
|---------|--------|
| licensed electrician signature | Lamacchia Realty |
| statement that K&T is "disconnected and abandoned" | Engineer Fix |
| property address | standard practice |
| date of inspection | standard practice |

### verified language

> "All knob-and-tube circuits have been disconnected, de-energized, and abandoned in place."

source: `ref.dp3-carriers-knob-tube.[ref].md` (internal brief)

---

## contractor commitment

### 60-day signed contract

many E&S carriers require a signed contract for complete K&T removal within 60 days of policy bind.

| requirement | source |
|-------------|--------|
| signed contract with licensed electrician | E&S underwriter guidelines |
| completion date within 60 days | broker verification |
| scope: complete K&T removal | carrier requirement |

### why contracts matter

> "Insurance companies often require a signed contract with a licensed electrician to complete the upgrade within 60 days."

source: Kuhlman Electrical Services (https://kuhlmanelectrical.com/)

## disclosure requirements

### what to disclose

| item | disclose? |
|------|-----------|
| K&T present anywhere | YES |
| K&T "abandoned in place" | YES |
| prior K&T-related claims | YES |
| planned rewire timeline | YES |

### disclosure language

> "Property contains knob-and-tube electrical that has been disconnected and abandoned in place. Complete removal scheduled for [DATE] per attached contractor agreement."

## carrier-specific requirements

### standard carriers (typically decline K&T)

| carrier | stance |
|---------|--------|
| State Farm | decline K&T rentals |
| Allstate | decline K&T rentals |
| Farmers | decline K&T rentals |
| Liberty Mutual | case-by-case, usually decline |

### E&S carriers (may accept with conditions)

| carrier type | typical conditions |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Lloyd's syndicates | electrician cert + contract |
| regional E&S | varies by state |
| Steadily | online platform, accepts renovation |
| Inszone | accepts vacant renovation |
