# howto.knob-tube-interim-coverage

## .what

practical steps to maintain insurance coverage during the gap between K&T disconnect and full rewire.

## .why

- rewire projects take 1-6 months to schedule
- most standard carriers cancel or non-renew K&T properties
- vacancy during renovation adds risk
- E&S market provides bridge coverage

---

## the timeline problem

| phase | duration | coverage challenge |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| discover K&T | day 0 | carrier may cancel |
| electrician disconnects K&T | 1-2 weeks | "abandoned in place" not sufficient |
| find rewire contractor | 1-3 months | most carriers won't bind |
| rewire complete + permit closed | 2-6 months | standard market reopens |

**key insight**: disconnect alone does not satisfy most carriers. they require complete removal within 30-60 days OR a signed contract for full replacement.

---

## step 1: get electrician certification letter

before you shop for coverage, get documentation:

| element | why required |
|---------|-------------|
| electrician license number | proves qualified professional |
| statement: "all K&T circuits disconnected and de-energized" | satisfies abandon-in-place requirement |
| statement: "property safe for occupancy" | addresses habitability |
| date of inspection | establishes timeline |
| scope of work performed | documents what was done |

**sample language**:
> "I certify that all knob-and-tube circuits have been disconnected, de-energized, and abandoned in place. The property is safe for occupancy."

---

## step 2: get contractor commitment letter

E&S carriers often require proof of intent to complete rewire:

| document type | carrier acceptance |
|---------------|-------------------|
| signed contract with dates | highest acceptance |
| letter of intent from contractor | moderate acceptance |
| verbal commitment only | typically rejected |

**60-day rule**: many E&S carriers require signed contract for rewire within 60 days of bind.

---

## step 3: shop E&S market

standard carriers (State Farm, Allstate, Farmers) typically decline K&T. go to E&S:

| E&S option | coverage type | typical term |
|------------|---------------|--------------|
| Steadily | vacant/renovation landlord | 3-12 months |
| Inszone | renovation insurance | project-based |
| Lloyd's syndicates | surplus lines | custom |
| regional E&S brokers | varies | varies |

**access**: E&S requires licensed surplus lines broker. online platforms (Steadily) simplify access.

---

## step 4: document everything

maintain a file with:

| document | purpose |
|----------|---------|
| electrician certification letter | proves K&T disconnected |
| photos of disconnected circuits | visual evidence |
| contractor estimate/contract | proves intent to rewire |
| permit application (if filed) | shows compliance path |
| communication with carrier | paper trail |

---

## what to tell the carrier

be direct about K&T status:

| statement | risk |
|-----------|------|
| "no K&T" (false) | claim denial, rescission, fraud |
| "K&T disconnected, rewire scheduled" (true) | proper underwrite, coverage intact |
| "K&T present but unused" (vague) | may be treated as active K&T |

**disclosure principle**: undisclosed K&T discovered at claim = denial + rescission.

---

## cost expectations

| coverage type | typical premium impact |
|---------------|----------------------|
| standard DP-3 (no K&T) | baseline |
| E&S with K&T in progress | +40-100% |
| vacant renovation policy | +50-150% |
| builder's risk (if major reno) | project-based |

---

## red flags to avoid

| mistake | consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| let policy lapse during contractor search | gap in coverage, claim denial |
| fail to disclose K&T | rescission at claim time |
| assume disconnect = removal | carrier treats as active K&T |
| skip documentation | no proof at claim time |
