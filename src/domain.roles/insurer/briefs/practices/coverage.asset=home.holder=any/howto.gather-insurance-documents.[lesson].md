# howto.gather-insurance-documents

## .what

best practices to collect and retain homeowners insurance documents.

## .why

- **claims require documentation** — without records, claims get delayed or denied
- **CLUE follows the property** — previous owner claims affect your rates
- **errors exist** — you can dispute inaccuracies if you know about them
- **coverage gaps hide** — only full policy review reveals exclusions

## .documents to gather

| document | what it tells you | where to get it |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **declarations page** | coverages, limits, deductibles, premium breakdown, endorsements listed, named insureds | carrier portal or agent |
| **full policy contract** | actual terms, definitions, exclusions, conditions | carrier portal or request from agent |
| **endorsements/riders** | modifications to standard policy (e.g., HQ-xxx forms) | attached to policy or request separately |
| **application** | what you disclosed (occupancy, condition, prior claims) | request from agent |

## .claims & history reports

| report | what it tells you | where to get it |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **CLUE report** | property's claim history (follows the property, 7 years) | [consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com) or 1-866-312-8076 |
| **A-PLUS report** | your personal claim history (follows you) | Verisk (free annually) |

### CLUE report details

- contains **7 years** of claims on your property
- shows date of loss, type of loss, payout amount
- **free once per year** under federal law (FACTA)
- claims follow the **property**, not the owner — previous owner claims affect your rates
- errors can be disputed with LexisNexis

### .why CLUE matters

1. **insurers pull it when you apply** — high claim history = higher premium or denial
2. **you inherit property history** — previous owner's claims are on YOUR property's CLUE
3. **catch errors before they cost you** — dispute inaccuracies proactively
4. **know what insurers see** — no surprises when you switch policies

## .retention periods

| document | how long to keep |
|----------|------------------|
| policy documents | while active + 1 year after expiration |
| claim forms & correspondence | until resolved + 1-3 years |
| receipts for repairs/upgrades | permanently |
| home inventory | update annually, keep indefinitely |

## .storage best practices

- keep **both digital and paper** copies
- paper: waterproof/fireproof safe or bank deposit box
- digital: cloud-based service or external hard drive
- rationale: accessible even if home is damaged

## .evidence of insurance vs full policy

| document | purpose | limitations |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **evidence of insurance** (ACORD 27) | summary for lenders/third parties | does NOT show exclusions, conditions, or your disclosures |
| **declarations page** | summary for policyholder | shows coverages but not full terms |
| **full policy** | complete contract | the actual terms that govern claims |

the evidence of insurance is **not your policy** — it's a summary for your lender.

## .action checklist

- [ ] download declarations page from carrier portal
- [ ] request full policy + endorsements from agent
- [ ] request copy of your original application
- [ ] pull free CLUE report from LexisNexis
- [ ] review for errors and coverage gaps
- [ ] store copies in fireproof safe + cloud backup
