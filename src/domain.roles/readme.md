# rhight roles

roles for intellectual property research, protection, and real estate transactions.

## 🔑 licenser

verify professional licenses (contractors, electricians, plumbers) are legitimate and unexpired.

## 🌎 patenter

research prior art, distill patent proposals, and prepare materials for patent applications.

## 🔎 transcriber

extract text from image-based PDFs and scanned documents via OCR.

## 🏛️ permiter

research permit requirements, code citations, and exemption analysis for specific jurisdictions.

## 🛡️ insurer

verify contractor insurance coverage, analyze policy gaps, and advise on builders insurance.

## ⚖️ counselor

advise on protection mechanisms when homeowners hire contractors — covers insurance requirements, bond verification, contract structure, payment gates, lien waivers, and recourse options.
