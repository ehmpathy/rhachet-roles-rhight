# define.delaware-1100-vs-federal-1120-vs-franchise-tax

## .what

three documents that sound alike but are distinct. confuse them and a reinstatement stalls.

| document | tax type | filed with | what it proves |
|----------|----------|------------|----------------|
| **Federal Form 1120** | federal corporate **income** tax | IRS | income/loss + **Schedule L** balance sheet (total assets) |
| **Delaware Form 1100** | Delaware corporate **income** tax | DE Division of **Revenue** | state income/loss, apportioned to Delaware |
| **Delaware franchise tax + annual report** | franchise tax (a privilege fee, not income) | DE Division of **Corporations** | the corp paid to exist; the report lists shares + gross assets |

---

## .why this matters

- the **void** that triggers reinstatement is a **franchise tax** failure (Division of Corporations) — not an income-tax failure.
- to reinstate cheaply via **Assumed Par Value**, the franchise tax report needs **gross assets**, which Delaware defines as the **"total assets" line on the federal Form 1120, Schedule L**.
- so the franchise tax (Corporations) depends on the **federal 1120** (IRS) — **not** on the Delaware 1100 (Revenue).

> the Delaware 1100 does **not** carry Schedule L and does **not** satisfy the franchise tax substantiation. it's a different tax to a different agency.

## .the dependency chain

```
Federal Form 1120 (IRS)
   ├─ Schedule L "total assets" → gross assets
   │     └─ Delaware franchise tax (Corporations) → Assumed Par Value → reinstate
   └─ federal taxable income
         └─ Delaware Form 1100 (Revenue) → state income tax
```

one federal 1120 feeds **both** Delaware returns — but only the **Schedule L** part feeds the franchise tax that governs reinstatement.

---

## .quick disambiguation

| if you have... | it is... | use it for... |
|----------------|----------|---------------|
| a return titled **"Form 1100"** | DE state income tax | DE income tax only — **not** franchise tax |
| a return titled **"Form 1120"** | federal income tax | franchise tax gross assets (Schedule L) + the source of both |
| a bill from **Division of Corporations** | franchise tax | the reinstatement path |
