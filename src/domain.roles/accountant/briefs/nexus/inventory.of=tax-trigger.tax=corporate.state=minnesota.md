# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=minnesota

## .answer

the **corporation franchise tax** (Minn. Stat. ch. 290). **no dollar bright line**: a corporation that "obtains or regularly solicits business" in Minnesota is taxable without physical presence, and solicitation of **20 or more persons** in Minnesota in a tax period is presumed to meet that test.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (100% for taxable years from 2014), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (where the services are received; for a business customer, only a state where it has a fixed place of business).

> "2014 and later calendar years 100 0 0"
>
> — Minn. Stat. §290.191, subd. 2(b), the sales / property / payroll factor percents

> "(j) Receipts from the performance of services must be attributed to the state where the services are received. For the purposes of this section, receipts from the performance of services provided to a corporation, partnership, or trust may only be attributed to a state where it has a fixed place of doing business."
>
> — Minn. Stat. §290.191, subd. 5(j)

## .the threshold

> "(b) Except as provided in subdivision 3, a person that conducts a trade or business not described in paragraph (a) is subject to the taxes imposed by this chapter if the trade or business obtains or regularly solicits business from within this state, without regard to physical presence in this state."
>
> — Minn. Stat. §290.015, subd. 1(b)

> "(1) it conducts activities described in subdivision 1, paragraph (b), without regard to transactions described in subdivision 3, with 20 or more persons within this state during any tax period"
>
> — Minn. Stat. §290.015, subd. 2(a)(1) (a rebuttable presumption)

> "Any service received in Minnesota can be taxed, even if the service is performed remotely. If the customer is receiving the service in Minnesota, this would create nexus."
>
> — DOR, *Nexus and Business Income Tax*
