# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=north-dakota

## .answer

the **corporation income tax** (Form 40). **any ND-source income** — **no dollar bright line**: a corporation that does business in ND or has ND sources of income must file.

## .apportionment

**equally weighted three-factor formula** (property, payroll, sales) by default, or the sales factor alone by an election that binds five years; SaaS receipts sourced **cost-of-performance** (all to North Dakota only where the seller does more of the work there than in any other state).

> "Except as permitted under subsections 2 through 4, all business income must be apportioned to this state by multiplying the income by a fraction, the numerator of which is the property factor plus the payroll factor plus the sales factor, and the denominator of which is three."
>
> — N.D.C.C. §57-38.1-09(1)

> "For taxable years beginning after December 31, 2018, a taxpayer that is not a passthrough entity may elect to apportion business income to this state by multiplying the income by the sales factor."
>
> — N.D.C.C. §57-38.1-09(4)

> "A sales factor weighting election is binding for five consecutive tax years."
>
> — N.D. Admin. Code §81-03-09.2-02(4)

> "Sales, other than sales of tangible personal property, are in this state if: 1. The income-producing activity is performed in this state; or 2. The income-producing activity is performed both in and outside this state and a greater proportion of the income-producing activity is performed in this state than in any other state, based on costs of performance."
>
> — N.D.C.C. §57-38.1-17, *Other sales*

- a SaaS seller that builds and hosts its product outside North Dakota sources none of those receipts to ND under §57-38.1-17, whichever formula it uses

## .the threshold

> "Every corporation that does business in North Dakota, has sources of income in North Dakota, and/or has federal unrelated business taxable income (UBTI) must file a Form 40 – Corporation Income Tax Return."
>
> — ND Office of State Tax Commissioner, *Corporate Income Tax* page
