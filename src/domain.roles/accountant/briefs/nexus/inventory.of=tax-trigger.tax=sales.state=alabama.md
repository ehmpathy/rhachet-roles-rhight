# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=alabama

## .answer

**partly** — software licensed to a customer is taxable *"regardless of its function or form of transmission"*; remotely accessed SaaS is not addressed in terms (see 🚩). remote seller duty: Alabama retail sales of tangible personal property **over $250,000** in the **previous calendar year**; no transaction count.

## .the threshold

> "An out-of-state seller who is making retail sales of tangible personal property into the state is required to register with the Department and to collect and remit tax pursuant to Section 40-23-67, Code of Ala. 1975, when the seller’s retail sales of tangible personal property sold into the state exceed $250,000 per year based on the previous calendar year’s sales."
>
> — Ala. Admin. Code r. 810-6-2-.90.03(1) (amended November 30, 2018)

> "Computer software is tangible personal property. The retail sale or licensure of computer software is subject to Sales Tax, Use Tax, or Rental Tax, whether the transaction is effected by a transfer of title, possession, or a license to use or consume."
>
> — Ala. Admin. Code r. 810-6-1-.37(4)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | **> $250,000** retail sales into Alabama; no transaction count |
| which sales count | retail sales of tangible personal property (software is tangible personal property by rule) |
| period | the previous calendar year |
| effective | enforced for sales on or after **October 1, 2018** (ALDOR notice, July 3, 2018) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the rule is for an out-of-state seller; one with an Alabama location collects regardless |
| collection route | r. 810-6-2-.90.03(2): Article 2 sellers use tax, the Simplified Sellers Use Tax program, or a marketplace facilitator |
