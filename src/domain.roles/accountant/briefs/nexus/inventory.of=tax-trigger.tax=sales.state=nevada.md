# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=nevada

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** — products delivered electronically, software included, are outside sales and consumer use tax. a remote seller must register and collect once its Nevada sales **exceed $100,000 or exceed 200 separate transactions** in the **previous or current calendar year**.

## .the threshold

> "From October 1, 2019, if total sales exceed $100,000 or if there are more than 200 separate transactions in Nevada during the previous or current calendar year, Marketplace Facilitators, Marketplace Sellers, or Remote Sellers must collect and submit Sales Tax on all their Nevada sales."
>
> — Department of Taxation, *Marketplace Facilitator/Seller FAQs*

> "No, products delivered electronically, such as software, electronic magazines, clipart, and program code, are not subject to Nevada Sales or Consumer Use Tax."
>
> — Department of Taxation, *Sales Tax FAQs*

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 total sales, or > 200 transactions |
| which sales count | all Nevada sales, direct and through online marketplaces |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| start | register by the first day of the month at least 30 days after the threshold is exceeded |
| effective | remote seller rules October 1, 2018; marketplace aggregation from October 1, 2019 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold is the remote-seller test |
