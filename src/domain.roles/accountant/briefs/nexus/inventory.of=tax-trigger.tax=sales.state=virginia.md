# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=virginia

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** — software and services delivered electronically are a nontaxable service (Va. Code §58.1-609.5(1)); only software tied to tangible property is taxed. a remote seller must still register once it has **more than $100,000 in gross revenue from retail sales, or 200+ retail transactions, in Virginia in the previous or current calendar year**.

## .the threshold

> "10. Receives more than $100,000 in gross revenue, or other minimum amount as may be required by federal law, from retail sales in the Commonwealth in the previous or current calendar year, provided that in determining the amount of a dealer's gross revenues, the sales made by all commonly controlled persons as defined in subsection D shall be aggregated; or"
>
> — Va. Code §58.1-612

> "11. Engages in 200 or more separate retail sales transactions, or other minimum amount as may be required by federal law, in the Commonwealth in the previous or current calendar year,"
>
> — same section

> "Conversely, if the software is transmitted via electronic means, the transaction is generally deemed to be a nontaxable service, and any additional charges included in the electronic transfer take on the same nontaxable status."
>
> — Virginia Tax Commissioner, P.D. 25-98 (August 5, 2025)
