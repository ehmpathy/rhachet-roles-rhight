# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=washington

## .answer

**yes, SaaS is taxable** — remote access software (RAS) and digital automated services are retail sales subject to sales or use tax. an out-of-state business must register and collect once its cumulative gross receipts from Washington **exceed $100,000 in the current or prior calendar year** — the same line as B&O; the 200-transaction test ended March 14, 2019.

## .the threshold

> "Starting Jan. 1, 2020, a business must register to report B&O tax and collect/submit applicable sales tax, if the business meets any of the following thresholds in the current or prior year:"
>
> — WA DOR, out-of-state thresholds and nexus page (updated April 2020)

> "(i) More than one hundred thousand dollars of cumulative gross receipts from this state; or"
>
> — RCW 82.04.067(1)(c)(i)

> "RAS is classified as a retail sale for B&O tax purposes and is subject to sales or use tax."
>
> — WA DOR tax topic, *Digital products*

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 cumulative gross receipts |
| which sales count | all WA gross receipts — retail, wholesale, service, and apportionable — not only taxable retail sales |
| period | current or prior calendar year |
| effective | January 1, 2020 in this form; 200-transaction test dropped March 14, 2019 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes |
| SaaS rule | RAS = *"prewritten software provided remotely"*; mere storage and hosted backup are excluded from DAS |
