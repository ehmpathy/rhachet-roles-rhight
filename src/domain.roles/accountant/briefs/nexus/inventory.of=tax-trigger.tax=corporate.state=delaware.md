# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=delaware

## .answer

Delaware **corporate income tax** (8.7%): owed by every corporation that does business in Delaware — **no bright line** published; a corporation that keeps only a statutory office there is exempt. a separate **gross receipts tax** applies to any person that conducts a trade or business in Delaware, with a license requirement.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (gross receipts only, from 2020), SaaS receipts sourced by **where the seller performs the activity** (services rendered within Delaware), a performance rule rather than market-based.

> "Effective January 1, 2020, the apportionment calculation for Delaware will be based entirely on the sales or gross receipts factor on the Delaware corporate income tax return."
>
> — Delaware Division of Revenue, CIT-TAX instructions (2025), § General Instructions

> "5. For taxable periods beginning after December 31, 2019, by multiplying such remainder by the sales factor set forth in paragraph (b)(6)b.3. of this section."
>
> — 30 Del. C. §1903(b)(6)a.5

> "Enter on Line 2 in the column headed “Within Delaware” all other gross income (if any) including receipts from services rendered within Delaware which are not tax exempt, and which are not directly allocated in Schedule 2."
>
> — Delaware Division of Revenue, CIT-TAX instructions (2025), § Schedule 3A

> "Other income is considered gross income from a Delaware source when the activity that gives rise to the income is performed within the State of Delaware."
>
> — Delaware Division of Revenue, CIT-TAX instructions (2025), § Schedule 3A

## .the threshold

> "Every domestic or foreign corporation doing business in Delaware, not specifically exempt under Section 1902(b), Title 30, Delaware Code, is required to file a corporate income tax return (Form CIT-TAX) regardless of the amount, if any, of its gross income or its taxable income."
>
> — Delaware Division of Revenue, *Corporate Income Tax FAQs*

> "(6) A corporation maintaining a statutory corporate office in the State but not doing business within the State;"
>
> — 30 Del. C. §1902(b)(6), an exemption from the income tax

| physical presence also triggers | yes |

| gross receipts tax | a separate business-level tax on most businesses — rates *".0945% to 1.9914%"* |
| license | *"Any person or entity conducting a trade or business in the State of Delaware is required to obtain a Delaware business license"*; the Division's Nexus Questionnaire settles doubt |
