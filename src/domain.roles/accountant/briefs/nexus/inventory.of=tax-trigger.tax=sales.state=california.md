## .answer

**SaaS is not taxable through December 31, 2026, and is taxable from January 1, 2027** — SB 122 (Stats. 2026, Ch. 23) makes software accessed remotely, SaaS named, tangible personal property. a seller must register if it has a California **place of business, representative, or leased server**, or if its sales of tangible personal property for delivery in California (with related persons) **exceed $500,000 in the current or prior calendar year** — and it must register **on the day** it crosses.

## .is the product taxable

> "(D) The sale or lease of a prewritten program is not a taxable transaction if the program is transferred by remote telecommunications from the seller's place of business, to or through the purchaser's computer and the purchaser does not obtain possession of any tangible personal property, such as storage media, in the transaction."
>
> — 18 CCR 1502(f)(1)

> "Beginning January 1, 2027, sales and use tax generally applies to the retail sale of a digital product and any copyright or patent interests associated with the digital product sold in California, or purchased from a retailer for storage, use, or other consumption in California."
>
> — CDTFA, *Digital Product Retailers and Purchasers*

> "Software as a service (SaaS) involving the right to access and use the provider's prewritten computer software running on a cloud infrastructure"
>
> — CDTFA digital products guide, *Definitions* (a listed digital product; IaaS and PaaS are excluded as "digital infrastructure")

## .the trigger

> "(4) (A) Any retailer that, in the preceding calendar year or the current calendar year, has total combined sales of tangible personal property for delivery in this state by the retailer and all persons related to the retailer that exceed five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000)."
>
> — Cal. Rev. & Tax. Code §6203(c)

physical presence examples, per CDTFA: *"Maintaining inventory or office locations in California."* · *"Leasing equipment, including a computer server in California."* (*Wayfair* guide, Overview)

## .thresholds

| figure | which sales count | period | effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **> $500,000** | all sales of tangible personal property for delivery in California, with related persons (IRC §267(b)); nontaxable sales and sales for resale count | the current or the prior calendar year | April 1, 2019 (AB 147) |
| no transaction count | — | — | — |
| SaaS inside that count | from January 1, 2027, when SaaS becomes tangible personal property | — | SB 122 |

## .when collection must start

> "You were required to register with CDTFA on July 7, 2019, and begin collecting the state, local, and district use taxes from your customers on your retail sales for delivery into California."
>
> — CDTFA *Wayfair* FAQ (the example: the July 7 sale is the one that crossed $500,000)

register online for a seller's permit or a Certificate of Registration – Use Tax (CDTFA digital products guide, *Retailers*).

## .what does NOT trigger it

- SaaS sales through 2026 (Reg. 1502(f)(1)(D)): no tangible personal property, so no tax and no count
- $500,000 or less in both the current and the prior calendar year, with no physical presence
- marketplace sales: *"you will no longer be considered the retailer of your sales of tangible merchandise facilitated through a marketplace, provided the marketplace facilitator is registered or required to be registered"* (*Wayfair* FAQ)
