# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=idaho

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** — remotely accessed software is carved out of tangible personal property. a remote seller must collect once its **gross receipts from sales delivered into Idaho exceed $100,000** in the prior or current calendar year; **no transaction count**.

## .the threshold

> "(h)  On and after June 1, 2019, any retailer without a physical presence in Idaho that has, in the previous calendar year or the current calendar year, cumulative gross receipts from sales delivered into Idaho in excess of one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000)."
>
> — Idaho Code 63-3611(3)(h), retailer engaged in business in this state

> "The term "tangible personal property" includes any computer software except the following: custom computer programs; computer software that is delivered electronically; remotely accessed computer software;"
>
> — Idaho Code 63-3616(b)

> "the term "remotely accessed computer software" means computer software that a user accesses over the internet, over private or public networks, or through wireless media, where the user has only the right to use or access the software by means of a license, lease, subscription, service or other agreement."
>
> — Idaho Code 63-3616(b)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 cumulative gross receipts |
| which sales count | all sales delivered into Idaho (taxable or not, per the statute's text) |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| effective | June 1, 2019 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — 63-3611 lists in-state places of business and agents |
| SaaS | not taxable; digital music, books, videos, games with a permanent right of use stay taxable (63-3616(b)) |
