# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=vermont

## .answer

**yes, SaaS is taxable** — since **July 1, 2024** all prewritten software is taxable, remotely accessed included (exempt 2015 – June 30, 2024). an out-of-state vendor must register and collect once it made sales into Vermont of **at least $100,000 or 200 transactions** in the prior twelve months.

## .the threshold

> "(ii) has either made sales from outside this State to destinations within this State of at least $100,000.00, or totaling at least 200 individual sales transactions, during the 12-month period preceding the monthly period with respect to which that person's liability for tax under this chapter is determined."
>
> — 32 V.S.A. §9701(9)(F)(ii), as quoted on the VT Department of Taxes *South Dakota v. Wayfair* page

> "Effective July 1, 2024, all sales of prewritten computer software are subject to sales and use tax, including software purchased on storage media, downloaded to a computer system, or accessed remotely via the internet."
>
> — VT Department of Taxes, *Prewritten Computer Software* page (Act 183 of 2024)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | ≥ $100,000 **or** ≥ 200 transactions |
| which sales count | sales from outside VT to destinations in VT |
| period | the 12 months before the monthly period, rolled |
| effective | Act 134 of 2016, live after *Wayfair* (2018); SaaS from July 1, 2024 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — §9701(9)(F) is for sellers with no place of business in VT |
| custom software | not taxable unless sold with prewritten software without a separate charge |
