# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=west-virginia

## .answer

**corporation net income tax** on any non-exempt corporation that engages in business in West Virginia **or derives income from sources** there. **any income — no bright line**: no dollar threshold is published.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** from tax year 2022 (before it, property + payroll + double-weighted sales), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** — a service by whether it was delivered to a customer in West Virginia; a licensed intangible where it is used.

> "West Virginia uses a single sales factor formula and market-based sourcing for services and certain intangible property."
>
> — WV Tax Division, *Corporate Income Tax* page (pulled 2026-10-05)

> "Under the “market-based sourcing” rule, sales of services go into the numerator of the sales factor based on whether the service was delivered to a customer in this state. Intangible property that is rented or licensed, or a contract right or government license, should also be included in the numerator of the sales factor if they were used in this state."
>
> — same page

> "Prior to tax year 2022, West Virginia apportioned the income of multi-state corporations through use of a four-factor formula consisting of a property factor, a payroll factor, and a double weighted sales factor."
>
> — same page

## .the threshold

> "Any non-exempt corporation (domestic or foreign) that engages in business or that derives income from property, activity or other sources in West Virginia is subject to the corporation net income tax."
>
> — WV Tax Division, TSD 100 *Business Taxes* (Rev. July 2026)
