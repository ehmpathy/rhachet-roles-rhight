# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=colorado

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** under state-administered sales tax — software through an application service provider is excluded. remote seller duty: retail sales into Colorado of **$100,000 or more** in the current or previous calendar year (all retail sales count, taxable or not); no transaction count.

## .the threshold

> "Any retailer who does not maintain a physical location in Colorado is exempted from state sales tax licensing and collection requirements if the retail sales of tangible personal property, commodities, and/or services made annually by the retailer into Colorado in both the current and previous calendar years are less than $100,000. All retail sales are considered for the purpose of the $100,000 threshold, regardless of whether those sales would be subject to Colorado tax."
>
> — CDOR, *Colorado Sales Tax Guide*, small retailer exception

> "Computer software is not subject to sales tax if it is delivered to the purchaser through any of the following methods: (1) it is provided to the customer through an application service provider; (2) it is delivered to the customer by electronic computer software delivery; or (3) it is transferred to the customer by load and leave computer software delivery."
>
> — CDOR, *Sales & Use Tax Topics: Computer Software* (rev. February 2021)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | **$100,000** retail sales; no transaction count |
| which sales count | all retail sales into Colorado, taxable or not |
| period | current or previous calendar year |
| start, if crossed mid-year | license and collect *"by the first day of the first month commencing at least 90 days after"* the $100,000 is passed |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the exception is for a retailer with no Colorado location |
