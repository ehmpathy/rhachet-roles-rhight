# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=georgia

## .answer

Georgia **corporate income tax**: **no bright line** — a corporation is in business in Georgia if it performs acts or consummates transactions there for profit, by rule; no dollar or percent threshold is published.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (gross receipts only, from 2008), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (a service by where the recipient receives the benefit; prewritten software by delivery to a Georgia customer).

> "However, for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2008, the Georgia apportionment ratio shall be computed by applying only the gross receipts factor."
>
> — Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 560-7-7-.03(5)

> "Gross receipts are in this state if the receipts are derived from customers within this state or if the receipts are otherwise attributable to this state's marketplace."
>
> — Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 560-7-7-.03(5)(c)1

> "Services. Except as otherwise provided, all gross receipts from the performance of services are included in the numerator of the apportionment factor if the recipient of the service receives all of the benefit of the service in Georgia."
>
> — Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 560-7-7-.03(5)(c)6.(ii)

## .the threshold

> "A corporation will be considered to be doing business within this state if it performs acts or consummates transactions within this state which result in financial profit or gain, or if it engages within this state in any activities or transactions for the purpose of financial profit or gain."
>
> — Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 560-7-7-.03(1)
