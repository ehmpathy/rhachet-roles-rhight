# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=minnesota

## .answer

⚠️ **SaaS: not established** — prewritten software is taxable "delivered by any means", and remote database access is not taxable; no pulled DOR source addresses remotely accessed software. a remote seller must collect once its sales over the **prior 12 months** exceed **200 retail sales** or **$100,000** shipped to Minnesota.

## .the threshold

> "Remote sellers are required to file and collect Minnesota state and local sales tax if their total sales over the prior 12-month period total exceed either:"
>
> — DOR, *Sales Tax for Remote Sellers* (last updated March 13, 2026)

the two prongs, as the page lists them: *"200 or more retail sales shipped to Minnesota"* · *"More than $100,000 in retail sales shipped to Minnesota"*.

> "Prewritten computer software is always considered tangible personal property. This is true no matter how the software is transferred to the purchaser."
>
> — DOR, *Digital Products*

> "Charges to access data from a database at a remote location are not taxable."
>
> — Sales Tax Fact Sheet 134, *Computer Software* (Revised September 2018)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | 200 retail sales, or > $100,000 retail sales |
| which sales count | retail sales shipped to Minnesota; resale sales excluded |
| period | the prior 12-month period |
| start | the first day of a month no later than 60 days after the exception is exceeded |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the exception is for remote sellers only |
