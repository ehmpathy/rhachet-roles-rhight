# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=north-dakota

## .answer

**SaaS: not addressed** — ND taxes COTS software delivered electronically and treats the sale of computer time as a nontaxable service; no ND source pulled names SaaS. remote-seller threshold: **taxable** sales into ND **> $100,000** in the **current or previous calendar year**; no transaction count.

## .the threshold

> "North Dakota law includes an exception for small sellers that requires sales tax collection by remote sellers ONLY IF their taxable sales into the state exceed $100,000 in the current or previous calendar year."
>
> — ND Office of State Tax Commissioner, *Sales and Use Tax* page, *Remote Seller*

> "Commercial Off the Shelf (COTS) software delivered electronically or by load and leave is considered a sale of tangible personal property and subject to tax."
>
> — ND *Sales Tax: Computers Guideline*, *Digital Products*

> "The sale or lease of computer time using the terminal or as a result of a batch service arrangement is a nontaxable service and is not subject to tax if separately billed or charged."
>
> — ND *Sales Tax: Computers Guideline*, computer-time paragraph

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 taxable sales; no transaction-count test |
| which sales count | taxable sales into ND (tangible personal property, services, specified digital products) |
| period | current or previous calendar year |
| effective | June 21, 2018 (the page's statement of the law's start) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold is an exception for sellers with no ND physical presence |
| SaaS | ⚠️ not stated; downloaded COTS taxable, computer time nontaxable |
