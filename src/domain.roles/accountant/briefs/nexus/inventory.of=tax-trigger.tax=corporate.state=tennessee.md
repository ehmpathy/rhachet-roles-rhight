# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=tennessee

## .answer

**franchise & excise tax** (franchise on net worth, excise on net earnings). customer sales alone create it once Tennessee receipts **exceed the lesser of $500,000 or 25% of total receipts** in the tax period — the *"bright-line presence"* test, tax years from 2016-01-01. below it, any other contact sufficient for substantial nexus still counts.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** for tax years that end on or after December 31, 2025 (an annual election back to property + payroll + 3× sales is open only where it yields a higher ratio and the year shows net earnings), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (a service where it is delivered; software outside a tangible medium under the market rule), from tax years that begin July 1, 2016.

> "For tax years ending on or after December 31, 2025, the standard apportionment formula will consist of the sales factor only."
>
> — TN Franchise & Excise Tax Manual (June 2026), p. 21, § Single Sales Factor Apportionment

> "then the taxpayer may annually elect to use the property/payroll/3x sales factor apportionment formula, but only if:"
>
> — same section, § Election to Use 3-Factor Apportionment

> "A service is sourced to Tennessee to the extent the service was delivered to a location in Tennessee."
>
> — same manual, p. 344, § Other Than Tangible Property Sales – Tax Years Beginning on or after July 1, 2016

> "In all other cases, the receipts from a license or sale of software are to be assigned to Tennessee as determined otherwise under this market-based sourcing rule."
>
> — same manual, Rule 42 discussion, special rules, § a) Software Transactions

## .the threshold

> "The taxpayer’s total receipts in this state during the tax period, as determined under Tenn. Code Ann. § 67-4-2012, exceed the lesser of $500,000 or 25% of the taxpayer’s total receipts everywhere during the tax period;"
>
> — TN Franchise & Excise Tax Manual (June 2026), bright-line presence

> "However, for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2016, this taxpayer would be subject to the tax if its sales into the state exceed the lesser of $500,000 or 25% of the taxpayer’s total receipts, or if any other contact with the state is sufficient to create substantial nexus. Note that physical presence is not required."
>
> — same manual, *Revenue Modernization Act Expands Nexus*
