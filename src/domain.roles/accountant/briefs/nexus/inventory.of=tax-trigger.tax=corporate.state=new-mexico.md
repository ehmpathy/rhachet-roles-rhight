# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=new-mexico

## .answer

the **corporate income tax** plus the **$50 corporate franchise tax** (Form CIT-1). **any income** from business "in, into, or from" New Mexico — **no dollar bright line**. the separate gross receipts tax is covered in the sales entry.

## .apportionment

**equally weighted three-factor formula** (property, payroll, sales), with a single-sales election open only to manufacturers, computer facilities, and New Mexico headquarters; SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (a service where it is delivered, a licensed intangible where it is used).

> "Except as provided in Subsections B and C of this section, all business income shall be apportioned to this state by multiplying the income by a fraction, the numerator of which is the property factor plus the payroll factor plus the sales factor and the denominator of which is three."
>
> — NMSA 1978 §7-4-10(A) (the version in force until January 1, 2027), TRD *Uniform Division of Income For Tax Purposes Act* compilation (revised May 20, 2026)

> "For most businesses New Mexico uses the standard three-factor formula."
>
> — FYI-350, *Corporate Income Tax and Corporate Franchise Tax* (Rev. 01/02/2025), *Income Apportionment*

> "(3) in the case of sale of a service, if and to the extent the service is delivered to a location in this state; and (4) in the case of sale, rental, lease or license of intangible property, if and to the extent the intangible property is used in this state."
>
> — NMSA 1978 §7-4-18(A) (Laws 2019, ch. 270), same compilation

- the single-sales elections (§7-4-10(B)–(C)) need 80% of the NM property and payroll numerators in manufacture or a computer facility, or a NM headquarters — a remote SaaS seller fits neither
- §7-4-18 sources a service and a licensed intangible the same way, so SaaS lands market-based under either characterization

## .the threshold

> "Any corporation with income from the transaction of business in, into, or from New Mexico or from property or employment in this state has nexus — that is, sufficient presence for New Mexico to impose the corporate income tax — unless its activities are immune under Public Law (P.L.) 86272 (31 USCA 351)."
>
> — FYI-350, *Corporate Income Tax and Corporate Franchise Tax* (Rev. 01/02/2025), Appendix, *Determining Nexus*

> "New Mexico Corporate Franchise Tax is $50 per year or partial year."
>
> — FYI-350 (Rev. 01/02/2025)
