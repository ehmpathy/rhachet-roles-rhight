# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=north-carolina

## .answer

the **corporate income tax** (and, on the same return CD-405, the **franchise tax**). trigger: "doing business" in North Carolina — **no dollar bright line**; the rule reaches any business activity there for economic gain.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor**, SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (a service where it is delivered, a licensed intangible where it is used).

> "apportionable income of the taxpayer, other than those subject to special apportionment, is divided between the jurisdictions in which the business is conducted pursuant to the sales apportionment factor set forth in G.S. § 105-130.4(l)."
>
> — NCDOR, *2025 Corporate Income, Franchise, and Insurance Tax Bulletin*, apportionment of income

> "Receipts are in this State if the taxpayer's market for the receipts is in this State."
>
> — same bulletin, *Market-Based Sourcing of Receipts (G.S. § 105-130.4(l))*

> "d. If the receipts are from the sale of a service, if and to the extent the service is delivered to a location in this State. e. If the receipts are from intangible property that is rented, leased, or licensed, if and to the extent the property is used in this State."
>
> — same section

## .the threshold

> "all foreign corporations (those organized outside North Carolina) with a certificate of authority to do business or doing business in North Carolina, are subject to income tax and are required to file annual income tax returns"
>
> — NCDOR 2025 Corporate Income, Franchise, and Insurance Tax Bulletin, which cites 17 NCAC 05C .0101

> "For income tax purposes, the term “doing business” means the operation of any business enterprise or activity in North Carolina for economic gain, including, but not limited to, the following:"
>
> — same bulletin, which cites 17 NCAC 05C .0102

the listed examples include *"The rendering of a service to clients or customers in North Carolina by agents or employees of a foreign corporation"* and the ownership or rental in NC of *"Trademarks, trade names, franchise rights, computer programs, copyrights, patented processes, licenses."*
