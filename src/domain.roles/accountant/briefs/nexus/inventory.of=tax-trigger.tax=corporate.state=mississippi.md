# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=mississippi

## .answer

two taxes on one return: the **corporate income tax** and the **franchise tax** (on capital, $25 minimum). **no bright line** — "doing business" (= nexus) is any activity in Mississippi for profit; the DOR names no sales-volume figure for income tax.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** for a service company (manufacturers use a three-factor formula), SaaS receipts sourced **where the seller performs the work** (pro rata to the activity performed in Mississippi), not where the customer is.

> "The net business income of retailers, wholesalers, lessors and other service companies, merchants, traders, vendors, or dealers buying, selling or renting, other than those specifically provided for elsewhere in this Regulation, which has not been allocated, directly assigned, or excluded as otherwise provided, shall be apportioned to Mississippi by multiplying such net business income by a single sales-factor apportionment formula as defined in subsection 402.09 paragraph 3 of this Regulation."
>
> — Miss. Admin. Code 35.III.8.06, §402.06

> "Under this section gross receipts are attributed to this state if the income-producing activity is performed wholly within this state. Gross receipts, with respect to a particular item of income, derived from income-producing activity performed within and without this state shall be attributed to this state to the extent of such gross receipts which represent services or activities actually performed within this state."
>
> — Miss. Admin. Code 35.III.8.06, §402.09(3)(d), sales other than sales of tangible personal property

## .the threshold

> "For Mississippi income tax purposes, the term "doing business" means the operation of any enterprise or activity in Mississippi for financial profit or economic gain. For the purposes of this regulation, the terms "doing business" and "nexus" have the same meaning."
>
> — Miss. Admin. Code 35.III.8.06, §203.01

> "All corporations, associations, or entities doing business, earning income, or existing in Mississippi are required to file a corporate income and franchise tax return."
>
> — DOR, *Business Tax FAQs*

> "A franchise tax is imposed on corporations for the privilege of doing business in the State of Mississippi."
>
> — DOR, *Business Tax FAQs*
