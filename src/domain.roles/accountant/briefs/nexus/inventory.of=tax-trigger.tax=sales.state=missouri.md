# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=missouri

## .answer

**no** — canned software delivered digitally is not taxable, so SaaS is outside the tax. a remote seller must collect **vendor's use tax** once its **gross receipts from taxable sales of tangible personal property into Missouri exceed $100,000** over the prior 12 months, checked each quarter (no transaction count). a SaaS-only seller has no taxable sales to count.

## .the threshold

> "Any remote seller or marketplace facilitator that sells tangible personal property into Missouri must collect and remit vendor’s use tax on the retail sale, if their gross receipts from taxable sales in Missouri exceed $100,000 in a calendar year."
>
> — DOR, *Remote Seller and Marketplace Facilitator FAQs*

> "At the end of each calendar quarter, determine if your gross receipts from taxable sales in Missouri exceed $100,000 over the preceding 12-month period. If your sales in Missouri are in excess of $100,000 in that 12-month period, you are required to collect and pay Missouri vendor’s use tax, effective no later than three months following the close of the quarter."
>
> — same FAQs

> "Canned software that is delivered digitally, or any custom software, is not taxable."
>
> — DOR, *Sales/Use Tax, Taxability and Exemptions FAQs*

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 |
| which sales count | taxable sales of tangible personal property shipped into Missouri, marketplace sales included |
| period | the prior 12 months, tested at each quarter end |
| start | no later than three months after the close of the quarter |
| effective | ⚠️ the FAQ examples run from 2023; the statute date was not pulled |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold applies to a seller that *"does not have a physical presence in the state"* |
