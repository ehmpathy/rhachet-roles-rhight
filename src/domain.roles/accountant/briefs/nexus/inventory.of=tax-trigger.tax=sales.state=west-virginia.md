# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=west-virginia

## .answer

**partly / likely yes** — the consumers sales and service tax reaches all services except professional and personal services (§11-15-8); no WV statement on SaaS specifically was found. a remote seller must collect once its annual sales into West Virginia **exceed $100,000 or reach 200 transactions**, tested on the prior calendar year or reached in the current one.

## .the threshold

> "A small-seller exception is provided for remote sellers that have $100,000 or less in annual gross sales and less than 200 separate sales transactions delivered in West Virginia. A remote seller that qualifies for the small-seller exception is not required to register and collect West Virginia state and municipal sales and use taxes."
>
> — WV Tax Division, *Remote Sellers and West Virginia Sales and Use Tax* page (pulled 2026-10-05)

> "The small-seller exception is determined on an annual basis by looking at the seller’s business during the preceding calendar year. However, if during the current calendar year either of these thresholds is satisfied, the seller must begin collecting West Virginia State and municipal sales taxes on all sales made after the day the threshold was reached."
>
> — same page

> "The provisions of this article apply not only to selling tangible personal property and custom software, but also to the furnishing of all services, except professional and personal services,"
>
> — W. Va. Code §11-15-8

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 **or** ≥ 200 transactions (the exception needs both under) |
| which sales count | gross sales of products and services delivered into WV |
| period | prior calendar year; a mid-year cross collects from the next day |
| effective | January 1, 2019 (Administrative Notice 2018-18) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the exception is for remote sellers only |
