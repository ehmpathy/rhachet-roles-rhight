# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=michigan

## .answer

**partly — pure SaaS is not taxable**: remote-access software with no delivered code is not prewritten software (Auto-Owners); a downloaded component makes it fact-specific. a remote seller must register once it made **over $100,000 of gross sales or 200 or more transactions** with Michigan customers **in the previous calendar year**.

## .the threshold

> "A remote seller has nexus with Michigan and is required to report and pay sales or use tax if, in the previous calendar year, the remote seller made over $100,000 of gross sales or 200 or more separate transactions with Michigan customers."
>
> — Treasury, *Remote Seller FAQ*

> "Gross sales are used for purposes of the remote seller threshold. That is, a remote seller has nexus with Michigan if it has gross sales exceeding $100,000, or 200 or more transactions (whether attributable to taxable, nontaxable, or exempt sales), into Michigan in the previous calendar year."
>
> — same FAQ

> "When the product you have described is sold without the software application being downloaded it falls within the first type of software at issue in Auto-Owners. Therefore, it does not constitute the sale of prewritten computer software and it is not taxable."
>
> — Treasury technical advice letter, *Sales and Use Tax Treatment of Software Products* (October 4, 2019)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 gross sales, or ≥ 200 transactions |
| which sales count | gross sales — taxable, nontaxable, and exempt |
| period | the previous calendar year; register from January 1 of the next year |
| effective | ⚠️ RAB 2021-21 governs; its date was not pulled |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold applies to a seller with no physical presence |
