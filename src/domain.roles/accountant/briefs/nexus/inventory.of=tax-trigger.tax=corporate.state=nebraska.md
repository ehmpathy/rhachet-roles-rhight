# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=nebraska

## .answer

the **corporate income tax**. **any income — no bright line**: the tax reaches every corporation that earns any part of its federal taxable income from Nebraska sources, unless P.L. 86-272 protects it.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor**, SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (an application service is sourced where the buyer uses it).

> "A multistate business apportions its income using only a sales factor. The sales factor is computed by dividing sales sourced to Nebraska by sales everywhere."
>
> — Nebraska DOR, Business Income Tax FAQs

> "333.02 Application Services. Sales of application services are attributable to Nebraska if the buyer uses the application service in Nebraska. The application service is used in Nebraska if the buyer:"
>
> — Neb. Admin. Code Title 316 Ch. 24, Reg-24-333.02 (tax years from January 1, 2014)

> "333.02B Is a business entity, and uses the application service in a trade or business in Nebraska."
>
> — Reg-24-333.02B

## .the threshold

> "Nebraska imposes a corporate income tax on all corporations that earn any part of their federal taxable income from Nebraska sources."
>
> — DOR, *Business Income Tax FAQs*

> "Corporations that are protected from Nebraska income tax under 15 U.S. Code § 381;"
>
> — same FAQ, the first listed exception
