# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=iowa

## .answer

**partly — SaaS is a taxable service, but exempt when sold to a commercial enterprise** for its exclusive use (so consumer and nonprofit sales are taxed, most business sales are not). a remote seller must collect once its **gross revenue from Iowa sales reaches $100,000** in the prior or current calendar year; **no transaction count**.

## .the threshold

> "Iowa law includes an economic nexus threshold. Remote sellers must collect Iowa sales tax only if the remote seller has $100,000 or more in gross revenue from Iowa sales."
>
> — Iowa Department of Revenue, *Remote Sellers & Marketplace Facilitators*

> "Software as a service means the sale, storage, use, or other consumption of vendor-hosted computer software, such as software accessible on the cloud."
>
> — IDR, *Taxation of Specified Digital Products, Software, and Related Services*

> "The following are exempt from sales tax when purchased by a commercial enterprise and used exclusively by or furnished to that commercial enterprise:"
>
> — same page, Commercial enterprise exemption, Iowa Code 423.3(104) — the list includes *"Software as a service"*

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | $100,000 gross revenue |
| which sales count | *"all revenue from Iowa sales"* — exempt, wholesale, resale, and marketplace-collected sales count too |
| period | prior or current calendar year; collection starts the first month at least 30 days after the threshold is passed |
| effective | July 1, 2019 (SF 2417); SaaS taxable from January 1, 2019 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold *"does not apply to sellers with a physical presence in Iowa"* |
| SaaS | taxable; exempt for a for-profit *"commercial enterprise"* that uses it exclusively |
