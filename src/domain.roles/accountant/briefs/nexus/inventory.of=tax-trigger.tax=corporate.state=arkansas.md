# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=arkansas

## .answer

Arkansas **corporate income tax**: economic nexus at **$250,000 in gross receipts** for a nonresident corporation with no physical presence, **effective January 1, 2026** (Act 719 of 2025). before 2026, no bright line was published.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (from 2021), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (where delivered to the customer) under Act 719 of 2025; ⚠️ the year the market-based rule takes effect is unverified.

> "For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2021, all taxpayers with income from sources within and without Arkansas must use a single sales factor to apportion income from Arkansas."
>
> — Arkansas DFA, *Corporation Income Tax FAQs*, § apportionment of multistate income

> "The Act adopts Market-Based Sourcing for Sales of Services and Intangibles, which is based on the location where they are delivered to the customer."
>
> — Arkansas DFA, *Corporation Income Tax FAQs*, § apportionment of multistate income

## .the threshold

> "Act 719 of 2025 amended ACA §26-51-202 concerning the taxation of non-residents, adopting an economic corporate income tax nexus. Effective January 1, 2026, Arkansas established a corporate economic nexus threshold of $250,000 in gross receipts for nonresident corporations or partnerships with no physical presence."
>
> — DFA, *Corporate FAQs*

> "However, any non-resident corporation or partnership with a physical presence in Arkansas engaging in unprotected activities in Arkansas will still also establish nexus and an income tax filing requirement, even if it is below the economic receipt’s threshold."
>
> — DFA, *Corporate FAQs*
