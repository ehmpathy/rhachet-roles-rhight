# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=ohio

## .answer

**SaaS: partly** — taxable when sold **for use in business** as an "electronic information service" or "automatic data processing"; not taxable to a consumer for personal use. remote-seller threshold: sales to Ohio customers **> $100,000**, **or 200+** separate sales, in the **current or previous year** (since August 1, 2019).

## .the threshold

> "Your total sales to Ohio customers were over $100,000 in the current or previous year, OR You made 200 or more separate sales to Ohio customers in the current or previous year."
>
> — Ohio Department of Taxation, *Sales and Use Tax* page, *Out-of-State Sellers*

> "(e) Automatic data processing, computer services, or electronic information services are or are to be provided for use in business when the true object of the transaction is the receipt by the consumer of automatic data processing, computer services, or electronic information services rather than the receipt of personal or professional services to which automatic data processing, computer services, or electronic information services are incidental or supplemental."
>
> — Ohio Rev. Code 5739.01(B)(3)(e) (effective September 30, 2025)

> "(c) “Electronic information services” means providing access to computer equipment by means of telecommunications equipment for the purpose of either of the following:"
>
> — Ohio Rev. Code 5739.01(Y)(1)(c)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 sales **or** ≥ 200 separate sales |
| which sales count | total sales to Ohio customers (the page does not limit to taxable sales) |
| period | current or previous calendar year |
| effective | August 1, 2019 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — *"Businesses with a physical location in Ohio must register to collect sales tax, no matter how much they sell."* |
| SaaS | taxable for business use under 5739.01(B)(3)(e); consumer use outside that division |
