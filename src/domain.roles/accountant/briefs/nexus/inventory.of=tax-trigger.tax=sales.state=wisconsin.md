# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=wisconsin

## .answer

**partly** — prewritten software accessed online can be a taxable digital good, but remote use of software that works on a client's data under the provider's control is a nontaxable data service; most SaaS falls in the second bucket. a remote seller must collect once its gross sales into Wisconsin **exceed $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year**; the 200-transaction test ended February 20, 2021.

## .the threshold

> "As a result, a remote seller is only required to collect and remit sales or use tax if its gross sales into Wisconsin exceed $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year. Gross sales include all sales into Wisconsin, including sales for resale, taxable, and exempt sales."
>
> — WI DOR, *Remote sellers – Wayfair decision* page (2021 Wis. Act 1)

> "A digital good may include prewritten computer software that is accessed through means other than tangible storage media (e.g., video game accessed online). However, a digital good does not include charges for remote access to prewritten computer software if the software is used to process a client's data and the processing is under the direction and control of the service provider"
>
> — WI DOR Publication 240 (01/25), *Digital Goods*

> "The use of the software is a nontaxable data processing service"
>
> — same, footnote to the subscription example

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 gross sales; no transaction count |
| which sales count | all sales into WI — taxable, exempt, and for resale |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| effective | February 20, 2021 (2021 Wis. Act 1); $100,000 / 200 from October 1, 2018 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — *"The small seller exception does not apply to sellers with a physical presence in Wisconsin."* |
