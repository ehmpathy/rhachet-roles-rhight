# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=pennsylvania

## .answer

**SaaS: yes** — canned software "electronically or digitally delivered, streamed or accessed" is tangible personal property (72 P.S. § 7201(m), Act 84 of 2016). remote-seller threshold: **> $100,000** of gross PA sales, all channels (taxable and nontaxable), measured by **calendar year**; no transaction count. collection runs April 1 – March 31 on the prior calendar year's sales.

## .the threshold

> "1. Pennsylvania’s economic nexus applies only to those persons who, in the previous twelve months, made more than $100,000 of gross sales into the Commonwealth."
>
> — Sales and Use Tax Bulletin 2019-01, *Maintaining a Place of Business in the Commonwealth*

> "The sales threshold of $100,000 will be measured by calendar year."
>
> — PA DOR, *Online Retailers*, *Calculating Gross Sales*

> "Economic presence in Pennsylvania is measured by sales threshold only. There is no transactional count requirement.Sales are the gross amount on all channels, including taxable and nontaxable sales."
>
> — same panel

> "The term shall include the following, whether electronically or digitally delivered, streamed or accessed and whether purchased singly, by subscription or in any other manner, including maintenance and updates:"
>
> — PA DOR, *Taxability of Canned Computer Software, Digital Goods, and Related Services*, which quotes 72 P.S. § 7201(m)(2); the list includes *"canned software, notwithstanding the function performed"*
