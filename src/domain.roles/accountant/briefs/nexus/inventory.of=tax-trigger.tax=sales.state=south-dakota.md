# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=south-dakota

## .answer

**yes, SaaS is taxable** in effect — South Dakota taxes products transferred electronically and **the sale of all services**, so a remotely accessed service falls inside the base absent an exemption. a remote seller must license and collect once its gross revenue from South Dakota sales **exceeds $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year**; the 200-transaction test is gone (SL 2023, ch 38).

## .the threshold

> "Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any seller selling tangible personal property, products transferred electronically, or services for delivery into South Dakota, who does not have a physical presence in the state, is subject to chapters 10-45, 10-46E, and 10-52, and shall remit the sales tax and follow all applicable procedures and requirements of law as if the seller had a physical presence in the state, provided the seller's gross revenue from the sale of tangible personal property, any product transferred electronically, or services delivered into South Dakota exceeds one hundred thousand dollars in the previous or current calendar year."
>
> — SDCL §10-64-2 (SL 2016, ch 70; SL 2023, ch 38)

> "Sales tax applies to the gross receipts of all retail sales, including the sale, lease or rental of tangible personal property or any product transferred electronically, and the sale of all services."
>
> — SD DOR, *Sales & Use Tax* page (pulled 2026-10-05)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 gross revenue |
| which sales count | sales of tangible goods, products transferred electronically, and services delivered into SD |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| start | first day of the first month at least 30 days after the line is crossed (SDCL §10-64-2.1) |
| effective | §10-64-2 as amended 2023 (transaction count removed) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the thresholds *"do not apply to businesses with a physical presence in South Dakota"* |
