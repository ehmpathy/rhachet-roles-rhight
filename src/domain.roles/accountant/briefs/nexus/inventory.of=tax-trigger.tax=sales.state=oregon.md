# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=oregon

## .answer

**no state sales tax.** Oregon has no general sales or use tax, so there is no SaaS rule and no remote-seller threshold. (the CAT, a gross-receipts tax on the seller, is in the corporate entry.)

## .the threshold

> "Oregon doesn't have a general sales or use/transaction tax. However, Oregon does have a vehicle use tax that applies to new vehicles purchased outside of the state."
>
> — Oregon DOR, *Sales Tax in Oregon*
