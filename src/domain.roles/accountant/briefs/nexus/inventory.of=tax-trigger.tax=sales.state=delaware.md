# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=delaware

## .answer

**no state sales tax.** Delaware taxes the **gross receipts** of most businesses instead — a tax on the seller, not collected from customers; see the corporate entry.

## .the threshold

> "Delaware does not impose a state sales tax; however, Delaware does impose a tax on the gross receipts of most businesses. Gross receipts are the total amount received from the sale of goods or services sold by the business."
>
> — Delaware Division of Revenue, *Business Licenses FAQs*
