# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=kentucky

## .answer

two taxes: the **corporation income tax** and the **limited liability entity tax (LLET)**, a gross receipts / gross profits tax with a **$175 minimum**. both attach to a corporation that does business in Kentucky — which includes *"Directing activities at Kentucky customers"* — **no dollar bright line**.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (receipts only, tax years from Jan. 1, 2018), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (a service where it is delivered to a location in Kentucky; a licensed intangible where it is used in Kentucky).

> "(9) All apportionable income shall be apportioned to this state by multiplying the income by a fraction the numerator of which is the total receipts of the taxpayer in this state during the taxable year and the denominator of which is the total receipts of the taxpayer everywhere during the taxable year."
>
> — KRS 141.120(9)

> "(11) (a) Receipts, other than receipts described in subsection (10) of this section, are in this state if the taxpayer's market for the sales is in this state. The taxpayer's market for sales is in this state: … 3. In the case of sale of a service, if and to the extent the service is delivered to a location in this state; and 4. In the case of intangible property: a. That is rented, leased, or licensed, if and to the extent the property is used in this state"
>
> — KRS 141.120(11)(a)

> "For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2018: Single Sales Factor Market Based Sourcing of receipts for sales of intangible property Three-factor apportionment retained for providers."
>
> — Kentucky DOR, Corporation Income and Limited Liability Entity Tax page, Apportionment

## .the threshold

> "(13) "Doing business in this state" includes but is not limited to: (a) Being organized under the laws of this state; (b) Having a commercial domicile in this state; (c) Owning or leasing property in this state; (d) Having one (1) or more individuals performing services in this state; (e) Maintaining an interest in a pass-through entity doing business in this state; (f) Deriving income from or attributable to sources within this state,"
>
> — KRS 141.010(13)

> "(g) Directing activities at Kentucky customers for the purpose of selling them goods or services."
>
> — KRS 141.010(13)(g)

> "If either total gross receipts or total gross profits amounts to $3 million or less, then the company just pays a $175 minimum LLET."
>
> — Kentucky DOR, Corporation Income and Limited Liability Entity Tax page
