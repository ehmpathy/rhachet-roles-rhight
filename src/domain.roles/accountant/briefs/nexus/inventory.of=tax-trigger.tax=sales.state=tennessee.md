# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=tennessee

## .answer

**yes, SaaS is taxable** — software that stays in the seller's possession and is remotely accessed from Tennessee is taxable since July 1, 2015. an out-of-state dealer must register and collect once its retail sales to Tennessee customers **exceed $100,000 in the previous twelve-month period** (from October 1, 2020); **no transaction count**.

## .the threshold

> "Beginning October 1, 2020, out-of-state dealers shall register with the department to collect and remit tax if the dealer engages in the regular or systematic solicitation of consumers in this state through any means and made sales that exceeded $100,000 to consumers in this state during the previous twelve-month period."
>
> — TN Sales and Use Tax Notice #20-14 (July 2020), Public Chapter 759 (2020)

> "Provisions of the Revenue Modernization Act went into effect July 1, 2015, and provide that computer software that remains in the possession of the seller that is remotely accessed and used from locations in Tennessee is subject to sales and use tax."
>
> — TN Sales and Use Tax Manual (August 2026)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 |
| which sales count | all retail sales, *"including exempt retail sales, but should exclude sales for resale"* (Notice #20-14) |
| period | previous twelve-month period |
| start | first day of the third calendar month after the month the line is met (Notice #20-14) |
| effective | October 1, 2020 ($500,000 from October 1, 2019) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold is for dealers with no physical presence |
