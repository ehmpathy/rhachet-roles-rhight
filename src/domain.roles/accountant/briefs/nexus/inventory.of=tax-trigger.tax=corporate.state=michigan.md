# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=michigan

## .answer

the **corporate income tax** (CIT). customer sales alone create it once a corporation **actively solicits** Michigan customers and has **$350,000 or more of gross receipts sourced to Michigan**; physical presence for more than one day creates it at any volume.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (100%), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (where the benefit of the service is received).

> "A taxpayer that has business activities subject to tax within and without the state will apportion its tax base using a 100% sales factor."
>
> — Michigan Department of Treasury, Corporate Income Tax, Nexus and Apportionment

> "Sales from the lease/rental of tangible personal property, and income from royalties or the use of intangible property, are sourced based on where the property is used. Sales of services are sourced according to where the benefit of the service is received."
>
> — Michigan Department of Treasury, Corporate Income Tax, Nexus and Apportionment

## .the threshold

> "A person has nexus with Michigan if the person 1) is physically present in the state for more than one day, 2) actively solicits sales in Michigan and has gross receipts of $350,000 or more sourced to Michigan, or 3) has an ownership or beneficial interest in a flow-through entity (directly or indirectly through 1 or more flow-through entities) which has substantial nexus in this state."
>
> — Treasury, *Corporate Income Tax: Nexus and Apportionment* (pulled 2026-10-05)

> "Sales of services are sourced according to where the benefit of the service is received."
>
> — same page
