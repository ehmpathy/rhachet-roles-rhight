# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=massachusetts

## .answer

**yes, SaaS is taxable** — charges to access software on a remote server are taxable as prewritten software. a remote seller must register and collect once its **Massachusetts sales exceed $100,000 in a calendar year** (prior or current); there is **no transaction count**.

## .the threshold

> "Remote retailers that have no traditional in-state contacts (i.e., generally, stores, employees, inventory, representatives, etc.) are generally required to collect sales or use tax as vendors when they exceed $100,000 in Massachusetts sales in a calendar year."
>
> — 830 CMR 64H.1.9, promulgated December 13, 2019 (amended 4/12/2024)

> "If a remote seller’s Massachusetts sales in either the prior or current calendar year exceed $100,000, it must register as a vendor, collect tax on its Massachusetts sales, and remit the tax to DOR."
>
> — DOR, *Remote Seller and Marketplace Facilitator FAQs*

> "Generally, charges for the access or use of software on a remote server are subject to tax."
>
> — 830 CMR 64H.1.3

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 Massachusetts sales; *"regardless of the number of their Massachusetts transactions"* (DOR FAQs) |
| which sales count | the seller's direct Massachusetts sales; marketplace-facilitated sales excluded |
| period | prior or current calendar year |
| start | Jan 1 if crossed before Nov 1 of the prior year; else the first day of the first month two months after the month crossed (64H.1.9(3)) |
| effective | October 1, 2019 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — an in-state seller collects at any volume |
