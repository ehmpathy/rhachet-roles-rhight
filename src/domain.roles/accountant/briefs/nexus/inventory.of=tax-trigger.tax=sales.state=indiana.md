# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=indiana

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** in Indiana — remotely accessed prewritten software is not a retail transaction. a seller must register if it has an Indiana **place of business or representative**, or if its gross revenue from sales into Indiana (tangible goods, electronic products, services) **exceeds $100,000 in the current or prior calendar year**. ⚠️ a SaaS-only seller over $100,000 meets two DOR statements that point opposite ways — see 🚩.

## .is the product taxable

> "Prewritten computer software sold, rented, leased, or licensed for consideration that is remotely accessed over the internet, over private or public networks, or through wireless media, is not considered an electronic transfer of computer software and is not considered a retail transaction."
>
> — Sales Tax Information Bulletin #8 (January 2023)

the bulletin cites IC 6-2.5-4-16.7 among its references; the statute text was not pulled here.

## .the trigger

> "A retail merchant has physical presence in Indiana when the retail merchant: (1) maintains an office, place of distribution, sales location, sample location, warehouse, storage place, or other place of business which is located in Indiana"
>
> — Sales Tax Information Bulletin #89 (April 2024)

> "Sellers must get a registered retail merchant’s certificate and collect and remit applicable sales tax(es) if its gross revenue from sales into Indiana exceeds $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year. These sales include tangible personal property, digital products, and services delivered into Indiana (IC 6-2.5-2-1(d))."
>
> — DOR, *Remote Seller*

## .thresholds

| figure | which sales count | period | effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **> $100,000** gross revenue | tangible goods, products transferred electronically, and services delivered in Indiana — taxable or not | the current or the prior calendar year | enforced from October 1, 2018 |
| ~~200 transactions~~ | repealed | — | removed January 1, 2024 |

## .when collection must start

the DOR states no grace period. the duty runs for any calendar year in which the test is met, and registration is immediate: *"You may register immediately through the Streamlined Sales Tax Registration System or with Indiana’s INBiz portal."* (DOR, *Remote Seller FAQs*)

## .what does NOT trigger it

- SaaS and other remotely accessed software (SIB #8)
- a pure service seller: *"a service provider will not be required to register if it meets Indiana’s economic nexus threshold, but does not sell tangible personal property or provide taxable services to Indiana residents."* (SIB #89)
- marketplace sales: *"Remote sellers that utilize a marketplace facilitator do not include sales made through the marketplace to Indiana customers within their calculation of the $100,000 gross revenue into Indiana."* (DOR, *Remote Seller FAQs*)
