# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=south-dakota

## .answer

**no corporate-level tax** on a general business corporation — South Dakota imposes no corporate income tax. the one entity-level income-measured tax it runs is the **bank franchise tax**, which reaches financial institutions only.

## .apportionment

**none — no corporate income tax**, so **no apportionment formula and no receipt-source rule** apply to a SaaS seller; the bank franchise tax, the one entity-level income tax, reaches financial institutions only.

> "South Dakota does not impose a corporate income tax."
>
> — SD DOR, *Business Taxes* page, § Corporate Income Tax (pulled 2026-10-05)
