# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=alabama

## .answer

Alabama **corporate income tax** (plus the **business privilege tax**, owed by any foreign entity that does business there): factor-presence nexus once Alabama **sales exceed $675,000** (tax years from 2025), or property / payroll over $68,000, or 25% of any total.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor**, SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (where the service is delivered to the customer, by Rule 810-27-1-4-.17.01).

> "Except for special cases provided for in Rule 810-27-1.18, the apportionment factor is the total of Alabama Sales divided by total Everywhere Sales."
>
> — ALDOR, 2025 Form 20C instructions, § Instructions For Schedule D-1

> "Effective for tax years beginning after December 31, 2010, §40-27-1, Article IV, Paragraph 17, Code of Alabama 1975 has been amended to state that sales other than sales of tangible personal property pursuant to §40-27-1, Article IV, Paragraph 16, are to be sourced to this state if the taxpayer’s market for the sale is in this state."
>
> — Ala. Admin. Code r. 810-27-1-4-.17.01(1)

> "The taxpayer’s market for a sale is in this state if and to the extent the service is delivered to a location in this state."
>
> — Ala. Admin. Code r. 810-27-1-4-.17.01(2)

## .the threshold

> "Nonresident individuals and business entities are deemed to have “substantial nexus” for the tax period in which they exceed the following thresholds for Alabama:"
>
> — ALDOR, *Nexus* (Code of Ala. §40-18-31.2)

> "For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025: $68,000 of property; $68,000 of payroll; $675,000 of sales; or 25 percent of the total property, total payroll, or total sales."
>
> — ALDOR, *Nexus*

> "Every corporation, limited liability entity, and disregarded entity doing business in Alabama or organized, incorporated, qualified, or registered under the laws of Alabama is required to file an Alabama Business Privilege Tax Return."
>
> — ALDOR, *Business Privilege Tax*

| receipts that count · period | Alabama sales, per the factor rules · the tax period |
