# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=arkansas

## .answer

**unclear for SaaS** — prewritten software is tangible personal property by rule, and data-process services are not taxed; no pulled text addresses remote access. remote seller duty: sales into Arkansas **over $100,000 or 200 taxable transactions**, in the **current or previous year**.

## .the threshold

> "All remote sellers and marketplace facilitators are required to collect and remit Sales and Use tax to the State of Arkansas if within the current or previous year the sale of tangible personal property, taxable services, a digital code, or specified digital products for delivery into Arkansas exceeded one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) or two hundred (200) taxable transactions."
>
> — DFA, *Remote Sellers and Marketplace Facilitators* (Act 822 of 2019)

> "Tangible personal property includes: (i) Electricity; (ii) Water; (iii) Gas; (iv) Steam; and (v) Prewritten computer software;"
>
> — 26 CAR § 30-102(17)(B)
