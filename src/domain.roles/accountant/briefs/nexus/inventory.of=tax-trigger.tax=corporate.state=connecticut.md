# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=connecticut

## .answer

Connecticut **corporation business tax**: economic nexus from a substantial economic presence; bright line — a corporation with **less than $500,000** of Connecticut-source receipts in the taxable year is not deemed to have economic nexus (income years from 2010).

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (receipts only, from 2016), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (where the service is used).

> "(b) Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, on and after January 1, 2016, the net income of the taxpayer shall be apportioned within and without the state by means of an apportionment fraction. The apportionment fraction shall represent the part of the taxpayer's gross receipts from sales or other sources during the income year, computed according to the method of accounting used in the computation of its entire net income, which is assignable to the state"
>
> — Conn. Gen. Stat. §12-218(b)

> "(2) Gross receipts from services are assignable to this state if the market for services is in this state. The taxpayer's market for the services is in this state if and to the extent the service is used at a location in this state."
>
> — Conn. Gen. Stat. §12-218(b)(2)

## .the threshold

> "effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2010, any companies, partnerships, and S corporations that derive income from Connecticut or have a substantial economic presence within Connecticut, in either case attributable to the purposeful direction of business activities toward Connecticut, will be subject to tax in Connecticut."
>
> — DRS, IP 2010(29.1), *Q & A on Economic Nexus*, Q1

> "A company, partnership, or S corporation that is not otherwise subject to income taxation or a requirement to file a return in this state under Chapter 208 or Chapter 229 of the Connecticut General Statutes shall not be deemed to have economic nexus for a taxable year if the frequency, quantity, and systematic nature of the business’s economic contacts with the state are such that it has receipts from business activities that are less than $500,000 attributable to Connecticut sources during such taxable year."
>
> — DRS, IP 2010(29.1), Q3

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | **$500,000** Connecticut-source receipts (under it: not deemed to have economic nexus) |
| receipts that count | receipts from business activities attributable to Connecticut sources; passive investment income excluded (Q5) |
| period | the taxable year |
| effective | tax years from January 1, 2010 (2009 Conn. Pub. Acts 3, §§90-91, June Spec. Sess.) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the bright line applies only to an entity *"not otherwise subject to income taxation"* |
| P.L. 86-272 | DRS: protection *"not afforded to transactions other than sales of tangible personal property"* — no shield for software or services |
