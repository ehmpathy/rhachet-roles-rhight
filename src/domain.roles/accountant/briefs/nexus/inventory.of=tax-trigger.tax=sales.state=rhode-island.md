# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=rhode-island

## .answer

**SaaS: yes** — vendor-hosted prewritten computer software is taxable from **October 1, 2018**. remote-seller threshold: gross revenue into RI **≥ $100,000**, **or 200+** separate transactions, in the **prior calendar year** (from ninety days after March 29, 2019).

## .the threshold

> "Has not been collecting or remitting sales tax in this state and, in the immediately preceding calendar year either: (i) Has gross revenue from the sale of tangible personal property, prewritten computer software delivered electronically or by load and leave, vendor-hosted prewritten computer software, specified digital products, and/or has taxable services delivered into this state equal to or exceeding one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000); or (ii) Has sold tangible personal property, prewritten computer software delivered electronically or by load and leave, vendor-hosted prewritten computer software, specified digital products, and/or taxable services for delivery into this state in two hundred (200) or more separate transactions."
>
> — R.I. Gen. Laws § 44-18.2-3(E)

> "Effective October 1, 2018, vendor-hosted prewritten computer software will become subject to Rhode Island sales and use tax – including prewritten computer software used or accessed “in the cloud” (cloud computing)."
>
> — RI Division of Taxation, ADV 2018-38, *Software as a service (SaaS)* (September 4, 2018)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | ≥ $100,000 gross revenue **or** ≥ 200 separate transactions |
| which sales count | tangible personal property, prewritten software (downloaded or vendor-hosted), specified digital products, taxable services delivered into RI |
| period | the prior calendar year |
| effective | ninety days after March 29, 2019 (the (E) remote seller regime); SaaS taxable from October 1, 2018 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold applies to sellers with no RI physical presence |
| SaaS | taxable at 7% |
