# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=arizona

## .answer

Arizona's sales tax is the **transaction privilege tax (TPT)**, a tax on the seller. **partly** — prewritten software sold or rented is taxable, and in a published decision (LR 17-001) ADOR held remotely accessed hosted software a taxable rental on its facts. remote seller duty: Arizona gross sales **over $100,000** (2021 and later), in the **previous or current calendar year**; no transaction count.

## .the threshold

> "For a remote seller Arizona gross sales (before any deductions) of: $200,000 (2019) $150,000 (2020) $100,000 (2021 and beyond)"
>
> — ADOR, *Economic Threshold* (H.B. 2757) — met *"in the previous calendar year or are met in the current year"*

> "Gross receipts from the sale or rental of computers, computer equipment, hardware, pre-written software, and pre-written software updates are subject to the transaction privilege tax under the retail classification or the personal property rental classification."
>
> — ADOR, TPR 93-48

> "taxpayer’s customers manipulate its software so that they have the requisite possession and control to constitute a rental within the meaning of the personal property rental classification."
>
> — ADOR, LR 17-001 (redacted)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | **> $100,000** gross sales (2021+); was $150,000 (2020), $200,000 (2019) |
| which sales count | Arizona gross sales *"before any deductions"*; sales through a marketplace facilitator excluded; affiliates' income included |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| start | *"remit tax in the month following 30 days after the threshold was met"* |
| physical presence also triggers | yes |
