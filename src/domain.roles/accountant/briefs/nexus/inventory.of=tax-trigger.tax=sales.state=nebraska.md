# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=nebraska

## .answer

⚠️ **SaaS: likely yes, not stated by name** — REG-1-088 taxes software receipts "regardless of the manner in which it is conveyed"; the DOR software guide is marked "being updated". a remote seller must collect once its Nebraska **retail sales exceed $100,000 or it makes 200 or more retail transactions**, in the **prior or current calendar year**.

## .the threshold

> "if their retail sales (all sales other than resale), including sales made through a Multivendor Marketplace Platform (MMP), in Nebraska exceeded $100,000 in the previous year or current calendar year, or if the seller made 200 or more separate Nebraska retail sales transactions in the prior calendar year or current calendar year."
>
> — DOR, *Notice for Remote Sellers and Marketplace Facilitators* (LB 284, effective April 1, 2019)

> "088.01 The gross receipts from furnishing software regardless of the manner in which it is conveyed are taxable."
>
> — 316 NAC 1, REG-1-088 (February 22, 2009)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 retail sales, or ≥ 200 retail transactions |
| which sales count | retail sales (all but resale), marketplace sales included |
| period | prior or current calendar year |
| start | on or before the first day of the second calendar month after the threshold is exceeded |
| effective | April 1, 2019 (LB 284) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold is for remote sellers *"those without a physical presence"* |
