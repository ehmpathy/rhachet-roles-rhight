# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=nevada

## .answer

**no corporate income tax.** the business-level tax is the **commerce tax** (NRS ch. 363C), a gross-receipts tax owed only once **Nevada gross revenue exceeds $4,000,000** in a taxable year (July 1 – June 30); at or below that, no return is required.

## .apportionment

**no corporate income tax**; the commerce tax on gross revenue sitused **market-based** — service revenue follows where the purchaser receives the benefit (NRS 363C.220(1)(f)).

> "(f) Gross revenue from the sale of any services not otherwise described in this section is sitused to this State in the proportion that the purchaser’s benefit in this State, with respect to what was purchased, bears to the purchaser’s benefit everywhere with respect to what was purchased. For the purposes of this paragraph, the physical location at which the purchaser of a service ultimately uses or receives the benefit of the service that was purchased is paramount in determining the proportion of the benefit in this State to the benefit everywhere."
>
> — NRS 363C.220(1)(f), Situsing (commerce tax)

> "(g) Gross revenue not otherwise described in this section is sitused to this State if the gross receipts are from business conducted in this State. For the purposes of this paragraph, the physical location of the purchaser is paramount in determining if business is done in this State."
>
> — NRS 363C.220(1)(g)

## .the threshold

> "1. For the privilege of engaging in a business in this State, a commerce tax is hereby imposed upon each business entity whose Nevada gross revenue in a taxable year exceeds $4,000,000 in an amount determined pursuant to NRS 363C.300 to 363C.560, inclusive."
>
> — NRS 363C.200(1)

> "The Department shall not require a business entity whose Nevada gross revenue for a taxable year is $4,000,000 or less to file a return for that taxable year."
>
> — NRS 363C.200(2)

the Department's list of the taxes it administers carries no income tax: *"Commerce Tax"*, *"Modified Business Tax (MBT)"*, *"Sales & Use Tax"*, and others (Tax Types page).
