# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=virginia

## .answer

**corporate income tax** (6%, Form 500) on every foreign corporation with **income from Virginia sources**. **any income — no bright line**: no dollar figure; Virginia Tax states that nexus for foreign corporations is decided under P.L. 86-272.

## .apportionment

**three-factor, double-weighted sales** (property + payroll + 2 × sales, over 4), SaaS receipts sourced by **cost of performance**, winner-take-all — a subscription fee is a Virginia sale only if its income-producing activity is performed in Virginia, or more of it in Virginia than in any other state.

> "shall be apportioned to the Commonwealth by multiplying such income by a fraction, the numerator of which is the property factor plus the payroll factor, plus twice the sales factor, and the denominator of which is four;"
>
> — Va. Code §58.1-408(A)

> "A. Sales, other than sales of tangible personal property, are in the Commonwealth if:
>
> 1. The income-producing activity is performed in the Commonwealth; or
>
> 2. The income-producing activity is performed both in and outside the Commonwealth and a greater proportion of the income-producing activity is performed in the Commonwealth than in any other state, based on costs of performance."
>
> — Va. Code §58.1-416(A), the same in both versions on the page

> "The determination as to whether a transaction or sale is a Virginia transaction or sale is an all or nothing test."
>
> — Virginia Tax Commissioner, P.D. 16-135 (subscriptions to software and cloud-computing services), § Sales Factor

## .the threshold

> "A tax at the rate of six percent is hereby annually imposed on the Virginia taxable income for each taxable year of every corporation organized under the laws of the Commonwealth and every foreign corporation having income from Virginia sources."
>
> — Va. Code §58.1-400

> "Other foreign corporations must file Virginia returns for taxable years in which they receive income from Virginia sources.
>
> Nexus for foreign corporations is determined under the provisions of Public Law 86-272."
>
> — Virginia Tax, *Corporation Income Tax* page, § Foreign Corporations (pulled 2026-10-05)
