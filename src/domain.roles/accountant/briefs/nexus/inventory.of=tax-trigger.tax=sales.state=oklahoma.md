# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=oklahoma

## .answer

**SaaS: no, by rule** — an OTC rule puts prewritten software delivered electronically, and data-manipulation services, outside the levy; SaaS is not named. remote-seller threshold: **taxable** Oklahoma sales **> $100,000** in the **prior or current calendar year**; no transaction count.

## .the threshold

> "Remote sellers are only required to obtain a sales tax permit from OTC and collect Oklahoma state and local sales tax if they have more than $100,000 in taxable Oklahoma sales in the preceding calendar year or current calendar year for delivery into the state."
>
> — OTC Help Center, *Is there a small seller exception from this collection responsibility?* (cites 68 O.S. § 1392)

> "(5) Sales of prewritten computer software that is delivered electronically. For purposes of this paragraph, “delivered electronically” means delivered to the purchaser by means other than tangible storage media."
>
> — Okla. Admin. Code 710:65-19-156(b), among transactions to which *"The levy of sales tax does not apply"*
