# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=utah

## .answer

**corporate franchise and income tax** (form TC-20, **$100 minimum**). **any income — no bright line**: a corporation that earns income from Utah sources, other than mere solicitation of tangible-goods orders, must file. no dollar threshold is published.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** for a software seller (a "sales factor weighted taxpayer"; the equally weighted three-factor option is open only to taxpayers whose Utah property and payroll sit mostly in a short list of excluded industries, software not among them), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based, winner-take-all** — a service receipt is in Utah if the purchaser receives a greater benefit of the service in Utah than in any other single state; an intangible receipt by its use in Utah.

> "(2)	Subject to the other provisions of this part, a sales factor weighted taxpayer shall calculate the fraction for apportioning business income to this state using a fraction where:
> (a)	the numerator of the fraction is the sales factor as calculated under Section 59-7-317; and
> (b)	the denominator of the fraction is one."
>
> — Utah Code §59-7-311(2) (effective 1/1/2019)

> "If the average is not more than 50 percent, you are not an optional apportionment taxpayer and must apportion income to Utah using the sales factor."
>
> — Utah 2025 TC-20 Instructions, Schedule J, § Optional Apportionment Taxpayers

> "a receipt from the performance of a service is considered to be in this state if the purchaser of the service receives a greater benefit of the service in this state than in any other single:"
>
> — Utah Code §59-7-319(3)(a) (effective 5/14/2019)

> "The former “cost of performance” method no longer applies."
>
> — Utah 2025 TC-20 Instructions, Schedule J, § Lines 5a-5h, Sales Factor

## .the threshold

> "Businesses that earn income from Utah sources, other than from merely soliciting sales of tangible personal property, are required to file a Utah corporation franchise and income tax return."
>
> — Utah State Tax Commission, Publication 37, *Business Activity and Nexus in Utah*, § Corporate Tax

> "Every C corporation incorporated in Utah (domestic), qualified in Utah (foreign), or doing business in Utah, whether qualified or not, must file a corporate franchise tax return. C corporation returns are filed on form TC-20. Every corporation that files form TC-20 must pay a minimum tax (privilege tax) of $100, regardless of whether or not the corporation exercises its right to do business."
>
> — Utah State Tax Commission, *C Corporation Tax* page (pulled 2026-10-05)
