# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=south-carolina

## .answer

**yes, SaaS is taxable** — software accessed via an Application Service Provider is taxable (as a communications service). a remote seller has economic nexus once its gross revenue from South Carolina sales of tangible goods, products transferred electronically, and services **exceeds $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year**; **no transaction count**.

## .the threshold

> "A seller whose gross revenue exceeds $100,000, in the previous or current calendar year, from sales of:
> Tangible personal property
> Products transferred electronically
> Services delivered into South Carolina"
>
> — SCDOR, *Remote Sellers* page (pulled 2026-10-05)

> "Software delivered via an Application Service Provider, whereby the seller maintains the software on a website and the purchaser pays to access the software on that website, is subject to the sales and use tax."
>
> — SC Sales and Use Tax Manual, 2025 edition

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 gross revenue |
| which sales count | all sales into SC — taxable or not — of tangible goods, products transferred electronically, and services (RR #18-14, part III) |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| start | retail license and collection by the first day of the second calendar month after nexus (RR #18-14) |
| effective | RR #18-14, after *Wayfair* (2018) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — an office, warehouse, or agent in SC |
