# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=wyoming

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** — SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS are not subject to Wyoming sales tax unless the customer receives tangible property or an enumerated service. a remote seller must license and collect once its gross revenue from sales into Wyoming **exceeds $100,000 in the current or immediately prior calendar year**; the 200-transaction test was repealed in 2024 (Laws 2024, ch. 67).

## .the threshold

> "(i) The seller's gross revenue from the sale of tangible personal property, admissions or services delivered into this state exceeds one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000.00);
>
> (ii) Repealed by Laws 2024, ch. 67, § 2."
>
> — W.S. 39-15-501(a), as quoted in the WY DOR *Remote Sellers* bulletin (last revised August 2026)

> "Wyoming’s threshold is computed based on gross sales that include both wholesale and retail sales as well as exempt sales."
>
> — same bulletin

> "(IaaS), are not subject to Wyoming sales tax provided the customer does not receive any tangible personal property or enumerated service embedded within the service."
>
> — WY DOR *Computer Sales & Services* bulletin (last revised August 2026), § Cloud, SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 gross revenue; no transaction count |
| which sales count | gross sales into WY — wholesale, retail, and exempt |
| period | current or immediately prior calendar year |
| effective | Laws 2024, ch. 67 (transaction test repealed); voluntary licenses ended July 1, 2024 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the threshold is for sellers with no physical presence |
