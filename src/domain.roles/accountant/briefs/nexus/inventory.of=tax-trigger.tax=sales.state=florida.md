# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=florida

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** in Florida — the DOR treats electronic access to software as no sale of tangible personal property. a seller of **taxable tangible goods** must register before it does business from a Florida location, or once its **taxable remote sales** of tangible goods into Florida **exceeded $100,000 in the previous calendar year**. a SaaS-only seller never reaches that count.

## .is the product taxable

> "The Florida Department of Revenue has long held the position that licenses for the use of software accessed electronically are not considered sales of tangible personal property as defined in s. 212.02(19), F.S., and therefore, are not subject to Florida sales tax, so long as no transfer of tangible personal property occurs as a part of the transaction."
>
> — DOR Technical Assistance Advisement 05A-026 (June 2, 2005)

> "(19) “Tangible personal property” means and includes personal property which may be seen, weighed, measured, or touched or is in any manner perceptible to the senses"
>
> — Fla. Stat. §212.02(19)

⚠️ a TAA binds the DOR only as to its requester; it is persuasive for anyone else.

## .the trigger

> "(a) “Remote sale” means a retail sale of tangible personal property ordered by mail, telephone, the Internet, or other means of communication from a person who receives the order outside of this state and transports the property or causes the property to be transported from any jurisdiction, including this state, to a location in this state."
>
> "(b) “Substantial number of remote sales” means any number of taxable remote sales in the previous calendar year in which the sum of the sales prices, as defined in s. 212.02(16), exceeded $100,000."
>
> "(2) Every person making a substantial number of remote sales is a dealer for purposes of this chapter."
>
> — Fla. Stat. §212.0596

## .thresholds

| figure | which sales count | period | effective |
|---|---|---|---|
| **> $100,000** | **taxable** remote sales of tangible personal property only | the previous calendar year | July 1, 2021 (TIP 21A01-03) |
| no transaction count | "any number of taxable remote sales" | — | — |

## .when collection must start

> "Beginning July 1, 2021, persons not located in Florida who make a substantial number of remote sales for delivery in Florida are required to register with the Department and collect and remit tax."
>
> — DOR TIP 21A01-03 (May 14, 2021)

a remote seller registers **electronically** on the online Florida Business Tax Application (Form DR-1, TIP 21A01-03). an in-state dealer: *"The application must be submitted to the department before the person, firm, copartnership, or corporation may engage in such business."* (§212.18(3)(a))

## .what does NOT trigger it

- SaaS or other software accessed electronically with no tangible transfer (TAA 05A-026)
- remote sales at or under $100,000 in the previous calendar year; nontaxable sales do not count
- sales through a marketplace provider: *"the marketplace seller shall consider only those sales made outside of a marketplace."* (§212.05965(4)(b)2.)
