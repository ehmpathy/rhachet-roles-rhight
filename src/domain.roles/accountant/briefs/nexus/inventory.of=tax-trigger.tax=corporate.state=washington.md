# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=washington

> ⚠️ **not legal advice**, and not tax advice. this entry helps you find what to ask; confirm the answer for your facts with a licensed attorney or CPA.

## .answer

**business & occupation (B&O) tax** — a gross receipts tax; Washington has no corporate income tax. customer sales alone create it once cumulative gross receipts from Washington **exceed $100,000 in the current or immediately prior calendar year** (from January 1, 2020; RCW 82.04.067).

## .apportionment

no income tax; the B&O tax on remote access software (SaaS, a retail sale in Washington) is apportioned by a **single sales factor** (RCW 82.04.258), with receipts sourced **market-based** — to where the purchaser receives the service under the sales-tax source rules of RCW 82.32.730, else to the purchaser's address; other apportionable service income goes where the customer receives the benefit (RCW 82.04.462).

> "(b)(i) The term also includes the charge made to consumers for the right to access and use prewritten computer software, custom software, and customization of prewritten computer software, where possession of the software is maintained by the seller or a third party, regardless of whether the charge for the service is on a per use, per user, per license, subscription, or some other basis."
>
> — RCW 82.04.050(6)(b)(i), the definition of retail sale (as amended by 2026 c 238, the first version on the page)

> "(2) Apportionable income must be apportioned to Washington by multiplying the apportionable income by the sales factor."
>
> — RCW 82.04.258(2)

> "(b) For purposes of computing the sales factor, sales are considered in this state if the sale was sourced to this state under RCW 82.32.730 for sales tax purposes or would have been sourced to this state under RCW 82.32.730 if the sale had been taxable under chapter 82.08 RCW."
>
> — RCW 82.04.258(3)(b)

> "(b) When the tangible personal property, extended warranty, digital good, digital automated service, or other service defined as a retail sale under RCW 82.04.050 is not received by the purchaser at a business location of the seller, the sale is sourced to the location where receipt by the purchaser or the purchaser's donee, designated as such by the purchaser, occurs"
>
> — RCW 82.32.730(1)(b)

> "(i) Where the customer received the benefit of the taxpayer's service or, in the case of gross income from royalties, where the customer used the taxpayer's intangible property."
>
> — RCW 82.04.462(3)(b)(i)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 cumulative gross receipts |
| which receipts count | all gross income of the business attributed to WA — apportionable income by the receipts-factor numerator (RCW 82.04.462), other income by the RCW 82.32.730 rules (RCW 82.04.067(2)) |
| period | current or immediately prior calendar year |
| effective | January 1, 2020 (one threshold for all activities) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — RCW 82.04.067(1)(c)(ii), *"demonstrably more than a slightest presence"* |
| P.L. 86-272 | no relevance — B&O is a gross receipts tax, not a tax on net income; and the shield never reaches software |
