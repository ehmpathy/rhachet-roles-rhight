# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=new-jersey

## .answer

the **Corporation Business Tax** (CBT, a franchise tax measured by net income). customer sales alone create it once NJ-source receipts **exceed $100,000**, or **200 or more** separate transactions are delivered to NJ customers, in the fiscal or calendar year — for privilege periods that end on and after **July 31, 2023**.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (100% for privilege periods that begin on or after January 1, 2014), service receipts sourced **market-based** (N.J.A.C. 18:7-8.10A).

> "For privilege periods beginning on or after January 1, 2014, the sales fraction accounts for 100% of the allocation."
>
> — NJ Division of Taxation, Corporation Business Tax overview, Allocation Factor

> "Service receipts and transactions are sourced according to the market based sourcing rules set forth at N.J.A.C. 18:7-8.10A."
>
> — NJ Division of Taxation, TB-108(R), Corporation Business Tax nexus

## .the threshold

> "(1) The corporation derives receipts from sources within this State, pursuant to sections 6 through 10 of P.L.1945, c.162 (C.54:10A-6 through C.45:10A-10), in excess of $100,000 during the corporation’s fiscal or calendar year; or (2) The corporation has 200 or more separate transactions delivered to customers in this State during the corporation’s fiscal or calendar year. For the purposes of this paragraph, for any transaction that is a service transaction, “delivered to a customer” shall mean where the benefit is received"
>
> — TB-108(R), *Nexus for Corporation Business Tax for Privilege Periods Ending on and after July 31, 2023* (revised June 5, 2026), which quotes P.L. 2023, c.96, §6

> "on a foreign corporation for the privilege of having or exercising its corporate charter in this State or doing business, employing or owning capital or property, maintaining an office, deriving receipts, or engaging in contracts in New Jersey."
>
> — NJ Division of Taxation, Corporation Business Tax overview

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 NJ-source receipts, **or** ≥ 200 separate transactions delivered to NJ customers |
| which receipts count | receipts sourced to NJ under N.J.S.A. 54:10A-6 to -10; a service is "delivered" where the benefit is received |
| period | the corporation's fiscal or calendar year |
| effective | privilege periods that end on and after July 31, 2023 (TB-79(R) governs earlier periods) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — an office, employees, property, or capital in NJ |
| P.L. 86-272 | covers solicitation of orders for tangible goods only; never shields software or services |
| minimum tax | C corporation: $500 to $2,000, by NJ gross receipts (CBT overview) |
