# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=maine

## .answer

the **corporate income tax** (Form 1120ME). a corporation organized elsewhere has nexus once its Maine **sales exceed $500,000**, or Maine property or payroll exceeds **$250,000**, or any of the three exceeds **25%** of its total — for tax years from **January 1, 2022**.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor**, SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** (a service where it is received — the customer's home, or for a business the office that ordered it; a licensed intangible where the licensee uses it in Maine).

> "8.  Formula for apportionment of income to State.  All income shall be apportioned to this State by multiplying the income by the sales factor."
>
> — 36 M.R.S. §5211(8)

> "A. Except as otherwise provided by this subsection, receipts from the performance of services must be attributed to the state where the services are received. If the state where the services are received is not readily determinable, the services are deemed to be received at the home of the customer or, in the case of a business, the office of the customer from which the services were ordered in the regular course of the customer's trade or business."
>
> — 36 M.R.S. §5211(16-A)(A)

> "B. Gross receipts from the license, sale or other disposition of patents, copyrights, trademarks or similar items of intangible personal property must be attributed to this State if the intangible property is used in this State by the licensee."
>
> — 36 M.R.S. §5211(16-A)(B)

## .the threshold

> "B. Is organized or commercially domiciled outside this State, if the corporation's property, payroll or sales, as calculated pursuant to subsection 2, in this State exceed any of the following thresholds for the taxable year: (1) For property, $250,000; (2) For payroll, $250,000; (3) For sales, $500,000; or (4) Twenty-five percent of the corporation's property, payroll or sales."
>
> — 36 M.R.S. §5200-B(1)(B); PL 2021, c. 181, Pt. E

> "A corporation that has nexus with Maine is required to file a corporate income tax return (Form 1120ME) if it has Maine income and is subject to federal corporate income tax."
>
> — Maine Revenue Services, Corporate Income Tax FAQ, Q3 (revised April 17, 2026)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | Maine sales > $500,000, or property / payroll > $250,000, or > 25% of any factor |
| which receipts count | sales as sourced under the apportionment chapter (36 M.R.S. ch. 821) |
| period | the taxable year |
| effective | tax years from January 1, 2022 (MRS FAQ) |
| physical presence also triggers | the FAQ calls the thresholds a safe harbor *"for corporations with little activity within the State"*; MRS Rule 808 governs below them |
| P.L. 86-272 | §5200-B(4): a threshold does not override it — but it reaches tangible-goods solicitation only, never software or services |
