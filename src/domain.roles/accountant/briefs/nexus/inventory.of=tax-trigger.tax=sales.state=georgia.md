# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=georgia

## .answer

**no, SaaS is not taxable** — excluded by rule, as is prewritten software transferred electronically. remote seller duty: gross revenue **over $100,000 or 200 or more** retail sales of tangible personal property into Georgia, in the **previous or current calendar year** (from January 1, 2020).

## .the threshold

> "Obtained gross revenue, in an amount exceeding $100,000 in the previous or current calendar year, from conducting retail sales of tangible personal property to be delivered electronically or physically to a location within this state to be used, consumed, distributed, or stored for use or consumption in this state, OR"
>
> — DOR Policy Bulletin SUT-2019-02, Issue 5 (effective January 1, 2020)

> "Conducted 200 or more separate retail sales of tangible personal property in the previous or current calendar year to be delivered electronically or physically to a location within this state to be used, consumed, distributed, or stored for use or consumption in this state."
>
> — DOR Policy Bulletin SUT-2019-02, Issue 5

> "Exemptions and exclusions. Sales and use tax does not apply to: (a) Internet Access Service; or (b) The sale of Prewritten Computer Software Transferred Electronically to the purchaser or delivered to the purchaser by means of load and leave; provided, however, that the exemption granted by this paragraph does not include sales of Specified Digital Products, Other Digital Goods, or Digital Codes; (c) Software as a Service (SaaS);"
>
> — Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 560-12-2-.118(5)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | **> $100,000** gross revenue **or ≥ 200** retail sales — either |
| which sales count | retail sales of tangible personal property delivered into Georgia |
| period | previous or current calendar year |
| effective | January 1, 2020 (was $250,000 or 200 for 2019) |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — the bulletin is for sellers *"from outside of Georgia"* |
| digital goods | specified digital products, other digital goods, and codes with a right of permanent use are taxable from January 1, 2024 (560-12-2-.118(3)) |
