# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=corporate.state=vermont

## .answer

**corporate income tax** (form CO-411, with a minimum tax on every active corporation). **any income — no bright line**: a C corporation that received income allocable or apportioned to Vermont must file. no dollar threshold is published.

## .apportionment

**single sales factor** (Vermont gross sales or charges for services over the same everywhere; payroll and property are reported for information only), SaaS receipts sourced **market-based** — a service where it is delivered, a licensed intangible where it is used — from January 1, 2020.

> "the amount of the corporation’s Vermont net income that shall be apportioned to this State, so as to allocate to this State a fair and equitable portion of that income, shall be determined by multiplying that Vermont net income by the gross sales, or charges for services performed, within this State, expressed as a percentage of such sales or charges whether within or outside this State."
>
> — 32 V.S.A. §5833(a)

> "(C) in the case of sale of a service, if and to the extent the service is delivered to a location in this State; and"
>
> — 32 V.S.A. §5833(a)(2)(C)

> "Under the market-based approach, effective January 1, 2020, sales of services are assigned to Vermont when the services or benefits of the services are received in or delivered to Vermont, or if the customer or marketplace is located in Vermont."
>
> — VT 2025 Schedule BA-402 Instructions (Rev. 10/25), Line 4

> "SECTION B: Salaries and Wages Factor (Informational purposes only)"
>
> — same instructions

## .the threshold

> "A C Corporation must file Form CO-411, Vermont Corporate Income Tax Return, if:
>
> It was incorporated under the laws of the State of Vermont or
>
> It received income allocable or apportioned to Vermont, including income received as a shareholder, partner, or member."
>
> — VT Department of Taxes, *Corporate Income Tax* page (pulled 2026-10-05)

> "The Corporate Income Tax is a net income tax based on income allocated or apportioned to Vermont. A minimum tax applies to all active Corporations (including LLCs electing to be taxed as C-Corporations)."
>
> — same page
