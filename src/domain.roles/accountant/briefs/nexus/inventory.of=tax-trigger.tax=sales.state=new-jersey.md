# inventory.of=tax-trigger.tax=sales.state=new-jersey

## .answer

**SaaS: mostly no** — NJ treats SaaS as a service not listed as taxable, **except** SaaS that is an *information service* (taxable). remote-seller threshold: gross revenue from sales delivered into NJ **> $100,000**, **or 200+** separate transactions, in the **current or prior calendar year** (sales on and after November 1, 2018).

## .the threshold

> "1. The remote seller's gross revenue from sales of tangible personal property, specified digital products, or services delivered into New Jersey during the current or prior calendar year, exceeds $100,000; or 2. The remote seller sold tangible personal property, specified digital products, or services delivered into New Jersey in 200 or more separate transactions during the current or prior calendar year."
>
> — S&U-5, *Making Mail-Order and Internet Sales* (Rev. 5/25)

> "Use of a software application is not listed as a taxable service. Therefore, most charges for SaaS are not subject to Sales Tax. However, SaaS which meets the definition of an information service is subject to Sales Tax."
>
> — TB-72, *Cloud Computing (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS)* (July 3, 2013)

## .details

| item | value |
|---|---|
| figure | > $100,000 gross revenue **or** ≥ 200 separate transactions |
| which sales count | tangible personal property, specified digital products, or services delivered into NJ |
| period | current or prior calendar year |
| effective | sales made on and after November 1, 2018 |
| physical presence also triggers | yes — an office, employees, or business property in NJ (S&U-5) |
| SaaS | not taxable unless it is an information service (N.J.S.A. 54:32B-3(b)(12)); downloaded prewritten software is taxable as tangible personal property |
