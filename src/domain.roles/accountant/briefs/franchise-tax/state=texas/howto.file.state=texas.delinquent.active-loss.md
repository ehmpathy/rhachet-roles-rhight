# howto.file.state=texas.delinquent.active-loss

## .what

file Texas franchise tax reports **after** May 15 has passed, for a corporation with a net loss or little revenue, and bring the account back to *"active"* — the status the Comptroller describes as the entity's right to transact business in Texas.

the reports are the same as on-time ones — see `howto.file.state=texas.current.active-loss` for which form and which fields. this brief covers what changes once the date is gone: the penalty, forfeiture, officer liability, the open look-back, and the order of moves.

## ⛔ FIRST: was the year owed at all? — nexus, per report year

a late report is owed only for a year in which the entity was organized in Texas or had nexus there. settle that **per year**, before any form goes out (`nexus/state=texas/shield.no-duty-below-the-bright-line`).

a report filed for a year that owed none is not harmless — it is evidence against you:

> "In Comptroller Decision No. 114,533 (2021), the taxpayer filed a Texas Franchise Tax Public Information Report and franchise tax return for report year 2010. The Comptroller found that fact alone was sufficient to satisfy Staff’s prima facie burden to show the taxpayer had nexus with regard to that franchise tax report year."
>
> — STAR 202302010H, Comptroller contested-case decision

| the year | the move |
|---|---|
| no nexus that year | file no Texas form for it |
| nexus, report merely late (a notice already came) | file now; read the penalty and forfeiture sections |
| nexus, **never filed, no Comptroller contact yet** | ⛔ read *the order of moves* below before any first move |

## .what a late loss-year report costs

| item | for a corporation at or below the threshold | source |
|---|---|---|
| late-report penalty | **$50 per report** filed after the due date | §171.362(f); hub; Pub 98-918 |
| late-payment penalty | 5%, then 5% more after 30 days — **of the tax due**, which is $0 here | §171.362(a)–(b); hub |
| interest | runs on past-due **tax**; none accrues on $0 | 05-915; hub |
| forfeiture | the real exposure — see below | §171.251–.255 |

> "In addition to any other penalty authorized by this section, a taxable entity who fails to file a report as required by this chapter shall pay a penalty of $50. The penalty provided by this subsection is assessed without regard to whether the taxable entity subsequently files the report or whether any taxes were due from the taxable entity for the reporting period under the required report."
>
> — Tex. Tax Code §171.362(f)

> "A $50 penalty is assessed on each report filed after the due date."
>
> — Comptroller franchise tax hub, *Penalties*

⚠️ the $50 is per **report**, and the FAQ ties it to *"each required franchise tax report filed after the report due date"*. whether a PIR-only year (2024 and later) counts as one report or none is **not stated** in any page pulled — see the gaps.

⚠️ §171.362(c) sets *"The minimum penalty under Subsections (a) and (b) is $1."* whether that minimum applies where the tax due is $0 is not stated; ask the Comptroller rather than assume.

## ⛔ the real cost is FORFEITURE, and it reaches the officer

the sequence, in the Comptroller's own words:

> "If an entity does not file all required reports and/or does not pay tax, penalty or interest due within 45 days of Notice of Intent to Forfeit Right to Transact Business, its powers, rights and right to transact business in Texas may be forfeited. Entities that fail to file or pay within 120 days of the forfeiture of the right to transact business are subject to having their registration forfeited."
>
> — Form 05-915 (Rev. 4-26/2), *Forfeiture*

| step | trigger | statute | notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a report not filed | §171.251(1) — *"within 45 days after the date notice of forfeiture is mailed or provided by electronic means"* | Form 05-211, Notice of Intent to Forfeit Right to Transact Business |
| 2 | 45 days pass | forfeiture of the right to transact business, *"without a judicial proceeding"* (§171.257) | Form 05-212, Notice of Forfeiture of Right to Transact Business |
| 3 | 120 more days pass | registration / charter forfeiture (§171.301(1), §171.309) | Form 05-213, Notice of Forfeiture of Registration |

⚠️ the three notice names come from the FAQ's list; their match to steps 2 and 3 is read from the titles, not stated by the Comptroller.

the effects, from the statute:

> "(1)  the corporation shall be denied the right to sue or defend in a court of this state;  and (2)  each director or officer of the corporation is liable for a debt of the corporation as provided by Section 171.255 of this code."
>
> — Tex. Tax Code §171.252

> "If the corporate privileges of a corporation are forfeited for the failure to file a report or pay a tax or penalty, each director or officer of the corporation is liable for each debt of the corporation that is created or incurred in this state after the date on which the report, tax, or penalty is due and before the corporate privileges are revived."
>
> — Tex. Tax Code §171.255(a)

### ⛔ revival does not erase the officer's liability

> "If a corporation's charter or certificate of authority and its corporate privileges are forfeited and revived under this chapter, the liability under this section of a director or officer of the corporation is not affected by the revival of the charter or certificate and the corporate privileges."
>
> — Tex. Tax Code §171.255(d)

⇒ a debt incurred in Texas inside the forfeiture window stays on the officer after reinstatement. the cheap moment is **before** day 45 of the notice; the notice goes to the address of record (§171.256(c)), so keep that address current.

## ⛔ an unfiled report leaves the assessment window open

> "(a)  The limitation provided by Section 111.201 of this code does not apply and the comptroller may assess a tax imposed by this title at any time if: … (2)  no report for the tax has been filed;  or"
>
> — Tex. Tax Code §111.205(a)(2)

⚠️ the Comptroller's waiver page states *"The statute of limitations is generally within four years from the date the tax became due and payable."* — true for a year with a report on file (§111.201). for a year never filed, §111.205(a)(2) controls: no limit. a filed report is what starts the clock.

## ⛔ the order of moves — for a year never filed

the Comptroller runs its own Voluntary Disclosure Program, and contact forfeits it:

> "it has not been previously contacted by the Comptroller, either verbally or in writing, concerning a liability or estimated liability; and"
>
> — Publication 96-576 (08/2026), the eligibility list

> "The Comptroller’s office will limit its review to reports due four years from the date the business initially contacts us."
>
> — Publication 96-576 (08/2026)

> "Statutory penalties and interest will be waived, except interest on taxes collected and not remitted."
>
> — Publication 96-576 (08/2026)

| the state of the account | the lighter path |
|---|---|
| a notice or letter has already arrived | the VDA is gone. file every late report now, then ask for a waiver |
| no contact, few late years, $0 tax each | file the late reports; the exposure is the per-report penalty, and the waiver below may cover one year |
| no contact, many late years, or tax due | ⛔ write to the Business Activity Research Team **first** — the initial contact may be anonymous (*"It is not necessary to name the company or client during the initial contact"*) |

⚠️ the VDA's four-year review limit is a **program term** in a publication the Comptroller *"may change … at any time"*, not a statute. §111.205(a)(2) is unchanged by it. the multistate route is `limitations/state=texas/lever.mtc-disclosure-caps-an-uncapped-window`.

⚠️ paying a Comptroller estimate does not cure the delinquency: *"Paying the estimate DOES NOT resolve your entity’s tax reporting delinquency."* (franchise tax FAQ). the reports must be filed.

## .back to "active" — the reinstatement path

the account is current only when every report is in:

> "Regardless of your entity’s annualized total revenue, you must file a Public Information Report or Ownership Information Report."
>
> — Comptroller, the account-current page (2024 report year and later)

> "Step 1. File all outstanding annual Franchise Tax and Public/Ownership Information Reports. Step 2. Pay any tax, penalty and interest due. Steps 1 and 2 must be completed before continuing to Step 3. Step 3. Request the tax clearance letter online using Webfile or you may complete and submit Form 05-391, Tax Clearance Letter Request for Reinstatement (PDF)."
>
> — Comptroller, the reinstate-or-terminate page

| the account | what restores it |
|---|---|
| right to transact business forfeited, charter not yet forfeited | file the reports, pay what is due; §171.258 has the Comptroller revive the privileges |
| registration forfeited with the Secretary of State | steps 1–3 above, then the page's step 4: Form 05-377 (the tax clearance letter), the SOS reinstatement forms, and the SOS fees to the Secretary of State (§171.312) |
| check the result | the Comptroller's public account-status search — *"The "Franchise Tax Account Status," previously referred to as "Good Standing," provides the status of an entity's right to transact business in Texas."* |

## .ask for the penalty waiver — after every report is in

> "After filing your tax report and paying the amount due, you may request a waiver of all penalties assessed by downloading, completing and submitting Form 89-224, Request for Waiver of Penalty for Late Report and/or Payment (PDF)."
>
> — franchise tax FAQ

the Comptroller's limits, from its waiver page:

- *"We will not grant a waiver unless all reports have been filed and all tax due has been paid."*
- one waiver reaches *"One annual report period,"* at most
- a waiver in the past two years bars another, absent extenuating circumstances
- many late periods route to a Voluntary Disclosure Agreement instead

## .steps

1. settle nexus **per report year**; drop every year with none
2. list the late years; take each year's threshold from the table and its form from that year's forms page
3. if no Comptroller contact yet and the years are many or carry tax: write to BART for a VDA **before** any report goes in
4. otherwise, file every late report — PIR alone for 2024 and later; 05-163 e-filed plus the PIR for 2023 and earlier
5. pay any tax, penalty and interest the Comptroller bills
6. if the registration was forfeited: request the tax clearance letter (Webfile or 05-391), then reinstate with the Secretary of State
7. file Form 89-224 for the penalty on one annual period
8. confirm "active" on the account-status search; keep a printout
9. calendar May 15 for next year, and a zero-money extension if needed (`howto.file.state=texas.current.active-loss`)

## 🚩 what is NOT established here

| gap | why it is open |
|---|---|
| whether the $50 of §171.362(f) attaches to a **PIR-only** year (2024+) | the PIR is a report the chapter requires (§171.203), so it likely does — but no page pulled says so of a PIR-only filer. inference |
| whether §171.362(c)'s $1 minimum applies where tax due is $0 | the text does not say; not asked of the Comptroller |
| the VDA's reach for a $0-tax, report-only delinquency | Pub 96-576 speaks of *"previously unpaid or underpaid taxes"*; whether BART takes a PIR-only VDA is not stated |
| whether a taxpayer's own first letter to BART counts as the contact that ends VDA eligibility for later years | not stated beyond the publication's text |
| the Secretary of State's reinstatement forms and fees | owned by the Secretary of State; not pulled here (`registrator` role) |

## .enforcement

- a late report filed for a year with no nexus = **blocker** (STAR 202302010H: the filing is prima facie nexus)
- a four-year limit asserted for a year never filed = **blocker** (§111.205(a)(2))
- an unfiled year filed cold, with many years or tax due, before the VDA was considered = **blocker** (Pub 96-576: contact ends eligibility)
- revival treated as a cure for officer liability = **blocker** (§171.255(d))
- a Comptroller estimate paid in place of the reports = **blocker** (the FAQ: the estimate does not cure the delinquency)
- a 2023-or-earlier year filed on a 2026 form = **blocker**
- a waiver requested before every report is in = **nitpick** (the Comptroller will not grant it)
