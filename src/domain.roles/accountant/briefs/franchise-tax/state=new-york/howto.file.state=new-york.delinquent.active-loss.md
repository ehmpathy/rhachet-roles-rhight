# howto.file.state=new-york.delinquent.active-loss

## .what

file a New York State corporation franchise tax return (**CT-3**, Article 9-A) after its due date has passed, for a C corporation with a net loss, and ask the Department to waive the additions to tax.

the return itself is the same as an on-time one — see `howto.file.state=new-york.current.active-loss` for the lines. this brief covers what changes once the date is gone.

> ⭐ a loss year is **not** a $0 year in New York. the tax due is the fixed dollar minimum (or the capital base tax, if larger), so a late loss-year return carries a real, if small, tax — and every addition to tax is a percentage of it.

## ⛔ a lapsed due date cannot be extended

> "File Form CT-5 on or before the due date of your tax return."
>
> — CT-5-I (2025), *"When to file"*

so there is no extension to request now. file the return, and pay the tax with it.

> "You should file all tax returns that are due, regardless of whether or not you can make a full payment with the return."
>
> — Department, late file / late pay page

## .the additions to tax — each a percentage of the fixed dollar minimum

the four items, verbatim from CT-3-I (2025), *"Penalties and interest"*:

> "If you do not file a return when due, or if the request for extension is invalid, add to the tax 5% per month up to 25% [§ 1085(a)(1)(A)]."
>
> — CT-3-I (2025), item A

> "If you do not file a return within 60 days of the due date, the additional charge in item A above cannot be less than the smaller of $100 or 100% of the amount required to be shown as tax [§ 1085(a)(1)(B)]."
>
> — CT-3-I (2025), item B

> "If you do not pay the tax shown on a return when due, add to the tax ½% per month up to a total of 25% [§ 1085(a)(2)]."
>
> — CT-3-I (2025), item C

> "The total of the additional charges in items A and C may not exceed 5% for any one month, except as provided for in item B above [§ 1085(a)]."
>
> — CT-3-I (2025), item D

| the charge | base | on a loss year |
|---|---|---|
| late file | the tax required to be shown | 5% per month or fraction, cap 25% |
| the 60-day floor | the same | the **smaller of $100 or 100% of that tax** — where the tax is a small fixed dollar minimum, the floor is the whole minimum |
| late pay | the tax shown, unpaid | ½% per month, cap 25% |
| interest | the unpaid tax, from the original due date | the corporation underpayment rate, compounded daily |

⇒ read the dollar figure off the Department's penalty and interest calculator, never by hand: *"Use our penalty and interest calculator to determine late filing and late payment penalties and interest. (Only use this calculator when you're sending your return and payment together.)"* (late file / late pay page)

### ⭐ tax paid on time shrinks the late-file base

> "(C) For purposes of this paragraph, the amount of tax required to be shown on the return shall be reduced by the amount of any part of the tax which is paid on or before the date prescribed for payment of the tax and by the amount of any credit against the tax which may be claimed upon the return."
>
> — Tax Law §1085(a)(1)(C)

so a corporation that paid the fixed dollar minimum by the original due date (with a CT-5, an MFI, or estimated payments) has a reduced base for the late-file addition, even when the return itself runs late. next year: pay the minimum on time, even if the return will not be.

### interest has no waiver

> "Interest is always due, without any exceptions, on any underpayment of tax."
>
> — CT-3-I (2025), *"If you pay after the due date"*

> "For purposes of section 1096(e) of the Tax Law, the underpayment rate for this period is 11%."
>
> — Department, interest rates for 10/1/2026–12/31/2026

the rate resets each quarter; take the quarter's figure from the Department's interest page for each period the tax sat unpaid.

## ⛔ if the year was SKIPPED rather than late, the clock never started

> "(1) Assessment at any time.---The tax may be assessed at any time if--- (A) no return is filed,"
>
> — Tax Law §1083(c)(1)(A)

the general rule is three years from the return:

> "Except as otherwise provided in this section, any tax under article nine, nine-a, nine-b or nine-c shall be assessed within three years after the return was filed (whether or not such return was filed on or after the date prescribed)."
>
> — Tax Law §1083(a)

⇒ an unfiled year is open forever; the return starts the three-year clock, late or not. there is no age at which a skipped Article 9-A year falls away.

two more costs of a skipped run of years:

| who | the cost | source |
|---|---|---|
| a New York corporation | liable each year until formally dissolved — every skipped year owes at least the minimum | CT-3-I (2025), *"Domestic corporations"* |
| a foreign corporation authorized by the Department of State | authority may be annulled if it *"fails to file franchise tax returns for two or more consecutive years, and"* does not surrender it | foreign corporations page |

### the Voluntary Disclosure and Compliance Program — decide BEFORE the first return goes in

> "Do not file your return(s) before you receive an acceptance letter and agreement."
>
> — Department, Voluntary Disclosure and Compliance Program — General Program Information

| term | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| eligibility | not under audit for the tax and years; no bill received for them; not under criminal investigation; no tax shelter disclosed | program information page |
| not for a filed-but-unpaid year | *"(If you filed a return but didn't pay in full, you aren't eligible for this program. Wait for a bill and then request an installment payment agreement.)"* | program information page |
| the price | *"In order to participate in the program and receive its benefits, you must pay any tax and interest that you disclosed in the agreement."* | program information page |
| the benefit | *"We won't impose any penalties on you."* — for the disclosed taxes and periods only | program information page |
| limited look-back | *"If you owe taxes for more than three years, you may request a limited look-back clause during the online Voluntary Disclosure and Compliance (VDC) program application process."* | limited look-back page |
| the look-back for an honest miss | the third row — *"Mistake, confusion, ignorance of the law, inability to comply, or some other similar explanation (other than cases involving collected trust taxes)"* — lines up with *"Three-year look-back period"* | limited look-back page |
| the 20-year rule | *"However, a six year look-back will be imposed in instances of non-filing for 20 years or greater."* | limited look-back page |
| contact | 518-457-4448 | program information page |

| the situation | the move |
|---|---|
| one late loss year, minimum tax | file it, pay it, attach a reasonable-cause statement. the additions are small; the program's paperwork costs more than it saves |
| several skipped years, minimum tax each | weigh the program: the look-back can close the years before it, which §1083 otherwise leaves open forever |
| any year with real tax due | read the program terms before any first return goes in — a return filed first cannot be taken back |

⚠️ the look-back rows are read by table order — three circumstances, then three periods, in the same order. the page renders them as a table whose columns did not survive the text capture.

## .which edition of the form

> "Important: Use the correct form for the correct tax year when you file (see When to use the 2025 tax return). If you use an incorrect form or form for the incorrect tax year:"
>
> — CT-3-I (2025)

the three consequences it lists: *"we will not process your return,"* the return is late, and penalties and interest may follow. the form itself repeats it:

> "If you use it for any prior periods, the return will not be processed and will not be considered timely filed."
>
> — CT-3 (2025), caution line

| taxable year | edition | where |
|---|---|---|
| began in 2025 | **CT-3 (2025)**, CT-3-I (2025) | the current forms page |
| began in 2024 | the 2024 CT-3 and CT-3-I | the 2024 prior-year forms page (harvested; the 2024 form itself was not pulled) |
| earlier | that year's edition | the Department's prior years and periods index, by year |

### e-file reaches back only three tax years

| tax year | e-file? | source |
|---|---|---|
| 2025, 2024, 2023 | yes — the supported-forms table has a column for each, with CT-3, CT-3-M, CT-3.4, CT-5 checked | e-file-supported forms page |
| 2023, specifically | *"Tax year 2023 can still be electronically filed and falls under the mandate in processing year 2026."* | preparer mandate page |
| before 2023 | paper — *"If you can't electronically file the return because it's not supported by your approved e-file software or it can't be filed on our website, then you aren't required to e-file it."* | preparer mandate page |

paper goes to NYS CORPORATION TAX, PO BOX 15181, ALBANY NY 12212-5181 (CT-3-I, *"How to file"*), sent tracked.

## ⚠️ a late return loses the NOL carryback waiver

> "You must make the election on your original return (determined with regard to valid extensions) timely filed for the tax year in which the NOL was incurred."
>
> — CT-3.4-I (2025), *"Election to waive carryback"*

| the loss-year return | the New York NOL |
|---|---|
| timely, line A marked | carried forward only, up to 20 years |
| **late** | no waiver possible — it is carried back to the three prior years first, then forward |

file CT-3.4 with the late return anyway — it *"must be filed for every tax year"* (CT-3.4-I (2025)) and it starts the carryforward record.

## .ask for the additions to be waived — reasonable cause

every addition in §1085(a) carries the same escape:

> "unless it is shown that such failure is due to reasonable cause and not due to willful neglect"
>
> — Tax Law §1085(a)(1)(A)

and CT-3-I names the channel:

> "If you think you are not liable for these additional charges, attach a statement to your return explaining the delay in filing, payment, or both. See § 1085."
>
> — CT-3-I (2025), *"Penalties and interest"*

the statement, kept short and factual:

- what the corporation is, and when it became subject to Article 9-A
- why the return was not filed by its date
- when it learned of the duty, and that it filed promptly after
- that it now files on time (and has calendared the next due date)

⚠️ reasonable cause never waives interest — CT-3-I: *"Interest is always due, without any exceptions"*.

## .steps

1. list every Article 9-A year the corporation was subject and did not file — each owes at least the fixed dollar minimum
2. if more than one year, or any year with real tax: read the voluntary disclosure terms **before** any return goes in
3. file the federal 1120 for each year first (`federal/howto.file.federal.delinquent.active-loss`)
4. get the CT-3 edition for each tax year
5. complete it as `howto.file.state=new-york.current.active-loss` describes; line 2 is the fixed dollar minimum or the capital base tax, never $0
6. complete CT-3.4 for each year; complete CT-3-M for each year with MCTD activity
7. attach the federal return and the reasonable-cause statement
8. compute the additions and interest with the Department's calculator, and pay them with the return
9. e-file for 2023 and later; paper for earlier years, tracked
10. calendar the next year: the return by April 15, and the minimum paid by then even if the return needs a CT-5

## .enforcement

- a CT-5 filed after the original due date, treated as an extension = **blocker**
- "a loss year, so no penalty" = **blocker** (each addition is a percentage of the fixed dollar minimum, with a 60-day floor)
- a prior year filed on the current edition = **blocker** (the CT-3 caution: *"will not be processed"*)
- a skipped run of years filed cold, before the voluntary disclosure terms were read = **blocker**
- a §1083 three-year limit claimed for a year with no return filed = **blocker** (§1083(c)(1)(A))
- a late loss-year return that claims the carryback waiver = **blocker** (CT-3.4-I: timely original return only)
- interest omitted from the payment = **blocker**
- a reasonable-cause request with no stated facts = **nitpick**
- penalties or interest computed by hand rather than by the Department's calculator = **nitpick** (`rule.forbid.hand-computed-figures`)
