## the frame

the referral-selling statutes stay narrow: they attach only on an element stack —
**names-of-prospects + inducement of the referrer's own purchase + (FL) a chance element**. a
link-based, guaranteed reward lacks those elements and sits outside the statutes; a naive design
that adds them steps back INTO the statutes. these hazards are the ways a design crosses that line.

## the elements each statute keys on

| element | the two sides of the line |
|---------|---------------------------|
| link vs names | a per-referrer link/code, vs a collected prospect name list |
| referral vs own purchase | a reward on a friend's purchase, vs a reward that induces the referrer's own purchase |
| guaranteed vs chance | a reward paid to every qualified referrer, vs a raffle |
