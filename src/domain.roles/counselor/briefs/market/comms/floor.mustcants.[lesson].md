# floor.mustcants

## .what

⭐ **the todos.** `floor.landmines` states facts. `floor.redlines` draws lines. this file says **what to
do about them** — 61 entries in four buckets, on two axes: **must / can't** × **establish / eliminate**
(30 · 13 · 7 · 11).

⭐ **five of them are 🪨 baseline todos** (`ME26`–`ME30`) — what the **plain** rules force on a reader
who holds no model of this law. every other row is a ⚡ **surprise**: it repairs a model a competent
practitioner would otherwise carry in. ⛔ **both are enumerated; only the baseline block is marked**,
so a reader who already runs a program can skip straight past it.

⛔ **it supersedes two earlier cuts.** `floor.northstar` held the same material as **properties** ("the
record is per-number"); this file holds it as **todos** ("write a per-number record — done when you can
produce one"). `floor.armory` held the sources' **protections** in a separate file, on the claim that
one class of them was a passive fact you simply hold. ⭐ **that claim was false** — to *rely* on a
protection you must establish its conditions, so every one is a todo too. those entries now live in
`floor.landmines` group `J`, and reach this file through `R17`–`R19`.

> **recommendation disclaimer.** this is offered to the best of our knowledge, based on the
> information found in research — it carries **no guarantee of success or outcome**, and it must be
> reviewed by a licensed professional (attorney) against your own real scenario and current law
> before any reliance. it is groundwork for that review, not a substitute for it.

> **not legal advice.** informational groundwork for counsel to validate.

> ⚠️ **a note on gerunds.** this file trips `rule.forbid.gerunds` on verbatim regulatory text
> (`"established and implemented"`, `"maintains records documenting this process"`, `"assisting in its
> compliance"`, `"records establishing compliance"`, `"bearing the signature"`) and on terms of art
> (`telemarketing`, `advertising`, `automatic telephone dialing system`). to reword a quote is
> citation fraud.

## ⭐ the matrix

|  | **ESTABLISH** — make it true | **ELIMINATE** — make it not happen |
|---|---|---|
| **MUST** — you owe an action | 🔨 **`ME`** — build it, and be able to produce it | 🛑 **`MX`** — stop it |
| **CAN'T** — ⭐ **no action reaches it** | ⏰ **`CE`** — too late | 🧱 **`CX`** — no exit |

### ⛔ `CAN'T` means **cannot**, never **may not** — and the difference is the whole matrix

⚠️ **the word earns a misread, so it is settled here before the buckets are used.** *"we can't run a
program that spams people"* is a natural sentence and it uses `CAN'T` in the **forbidden** sense. that
is **not** this row.

⭐ **the two senses were tested against the grid rather than chosen by taste, and the forbidden reading
collapses it:**

| under `CAN'T` = **may not** (forbidden) | the result |
|----------------------------------------|-----------|
| ⏰ *can't establish* a spam program | ⛔ **identical to** 🛑 `MX` — "never build it" and "stop running it" are one duty at two moments, not two buckets |
| 🧱 *can't eliminate* … | ⛔ **meaningless** — "you are forbidden to stop" names no duty any source imposes |

| under `CAN'T` = **cannot** (impossible) | the result |
|-----------------------------------------|-----------|
| ⏰ *can't establish* the consent artifact for a send already made | ✅ distinct — a real class, and one no other file in the family holds |
| 🧱 *can't eliminate* your liability by delegation to a vendor | ✅ distinct — a real class, and the one readers spend money against |

⛔ **so the prohibition reading degenerates a 2×2 into a 1×2, and the impossibility reading keeps four
live cells.** the buckets stand; the label is glossed rather than renamed, and 🛑 `MX` is where every
"you may not" lands.

⚠️ **and note where a prohibition actually goes.** *"don't build a spam program"* decomposes across 🛑
`MX1`–`MX13` — it is not one row, because "spam" is not a legal category. the sources forbid an
**exclusive** revocation channel, a **stale** registry copy, a **>3%** abandonment rate. each is its own
line with its own authority, which is exactly why the file holds thirteen and not one.

each cell is a **different shape of action**, and that is the whole reason to split them:

| cell | the action | ⭐ what "done" means | what it costs you to misfile |
|------|-----------|--------------------|------------------------------|
| 🔨 **`ME`** | build an artifact, and hold it | you can **produce** the artifact on demand | you build the thing and cannot show it — the `Dish` failure |
| 🛑 **`MX`** | cease a practice | the practice is **not able to run** | you layer a control on top instead of removal, and the practice survives |
| ⏰ **`CE`** | ⭐ **act before a moment, or never** | ⛔ **"done" is unavailable afterward** | you plan to do it later, and later does not exist |
| 🧱 **`CX`** | ⭐ **bound it and budget it** | ⛔ **never done** — it is a residual, not a task | you spend on a fix that cannot work, and stay exposed |

⭐ **the two `CAN'T` cells are the payload of this file.** the `MUST` cells largely restate the redlines
in a different mood — useful, but not new. the `CAN'T` cells name a class no other file in this family
named: **false beliefs about escape**. that is exactly where `floor.landmines` clusters hardest, and it
had no home until now.

⚠️ **the columns are not independent, and the overlap is a lifecycle, not a defect.** `ME1` and `CE1`
are the same object at two moments — build the consent record (greenfield) and you cannot build it
after the send (brownfield). a reader on a **new** system reads the `MUST` column; a reader with
**traffic already out the door** reads the `CAN'T` column first, because it tells them which repairs
are not available.

⚠️ **"establish" is overloaded and the two senses are both live here.** `§ 227(c)(5)` says
*"established and implemented"* — that is **build**. litigation says "establish an element" — that is
**prove**. a `ME` todo is not done until **both** senses are satisfied, which is why every `ME` row's
done-condition is phrased as a production, never as a build.

## ⭐ the rule of admission — why a todo list can be published at all

a "best practice guide" is appetite by construction: it ranks, it sequences, it prefers. that is the
artifact `rule.require.fullsun-facts-not-tactics` says not to ship.

this file avoids it by one rule, inherited from `floor.redlines`:

> **a todo enters only where at least one redline forces it — and the trace column names which.**

⭐ **so the file is derivable, not authored.** a second reader who holds the same 52 lines and asks
"what must I do, and what can I not do" arrives at the same buckets, because the projection of a fixed
set onto a fixed axis is not a matter of taste.

⛔ **and the harder half: no order.** the matrix is a **classification**, never a sequence. not one todo
here is ranked by cost, by urgency, or by build order, and none should be read as "first".

⭐ **the one exception proves the rule.** ⏰ `CE` is the only bucket with any time pressure in it — and
that pressure is **in the source**, not in our preference. a consent record is contemporaneous because
`(f)(9)` says so, not because we ranked it. **the only order this file may carry is the order the
sources themselves impose.**

### how to read an entry

| column | what it holds |
|--------|---------------|
| **ID** | stable — `ME` · `MX` · `CE` · `CX`, then a number |
| **the todo** | the action, in the imperative |
| ⭐ **done when** | the **verifiable** condition — a production, a cessation, or a stated impossibility |
| **forced by** | the redline(s) that admit it. ⛔ an empty cell here would fail the derivability test |

### the authorities, named once

⚠️ **the trace column names redlines, not authorities** — that is what keeps the derivation auditable.
but a reader should not have to hop two files to pin a todo to its source, so the short forms used
below are expanded here:

| short form | the authority in full | where it bears |
|------------|----------------------|----------------|
| `Dish` | `United States v. Dish Network LLC`, 256 F. Supp. 3d 810 (C.D. Ill. 2017) | `ME1`–`ME3`, `CE1`, and the scope limit in `CX3` |
| `IMC v. FCC` | `Insurance Marketing Coalition Ltd. v. FCC`, No. 24-10277 (11th Cir. Jan. 24, 2025) | footnote 1, behind `CX5` |
| `Knutson` | `Knutson v. Sirius XM Radio Inc.`, 771 F.3d 559 (9th Cir. 2014) | why `ME18` is answered per acquisition source |
| `Dahdah` | `Dahdah v. Rocket Mortgage, LLC`, No. 24-1910 (6th Cir. 2026) | why `ME18` is re-asked per state |
| `Duguid` | `Facebook, Inc. v. Duguid`, 592 U.S. 395 (2021) | why `ME22`'s dialer status is a burden, not a given |

⭐ **and the regulation is pinned in the rows themselves** — every `47 C.F.R. § 64.1200` paragraph a
todo rests on is named where it is used, so no todo requires a hop through the source list.

## 🔨 ME — must establish: build it, and be able to produce it

is the single most common failure in the whole family — see `floor.landmines` `C3`.

## 🛑 MX — must eliminate: stop it

⭐ **a `MX` todo is a removal, and a control layered on top is not a removal.** the test is whether the
practice is still able to run.

| ID | the todo | ⭐ done when | forced by |
|----|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **MX1** | remove any **exclusive** revocation channel or keyword | no single keyword or channel is the only gate — ⚠️ **scoped** to `(a)(1)`–`(3)` and `(c)(2)` traffic; for two `(a)(9)`-exempt classes the regulation **prescribes** an exclusive means, and the tension is unreconciled | `H1` · `R14` |
| **MX2** | stop sends against a registry copy older than **31 days** | no send path can reach a stale copy | `H3` |
| **MX3** | stop use of a **shared or resold** registry subscription | the subscription is your own, with no cost-share | `H13` |
| **MX4** | stop calls outside the **strictest** applicable window | the dialer cannot place a call outside it — ⚠️ California's 9 a.m. boundary makes a federal-window dialer non-compliant for an hour every day | `H8` |
| **MX5** | stop abandonment above **3%** | measured per campaign per 30-day period, and the measurement is retained | `H16` |
| **MX6** | stop autodialer engagement of **two or more lines** of a multi-line business | ⛔ turns on equipment behaviour alone — consent quality is irrelevant to it | `H17` |
| **MX7** | stop dials placed to **detect the line type** | the practice is absent from every campaign | `H17` |
| **MX8** | strip promotion from a **revocation confirmation**, send only one, inside five minutes | no marketing content, one message, and the latency is recorded where it runs long | `P10` |
| **MX9** | stop forwarding a suppression request beyond the caller or an affiliate | prior express permission is on file before any share — ⚠️ the act that sounds more protective is itself gated | `H4` |
| **MX10** | stop the plan to **assemble proof at claim time** | the proof is written contemporaneously; no runbook says "reconstruct on demand" | `P2` |
| **MX11** | stop treatment of one state's answer as another's | no state outside the surveyed set is served on an extrapolation | `R11` · `R12` |
| **MX12** | stop destruction of a consent record inside the **claim window** | no retention policy is able to delete inside five years | `P8` |
| **MX13** | stop reliance on a **certificate** to answer a coverage question | no coverage decision cites a certificate | `P6` |

## ⭐ what a full sheet does **not** get

1. ⛔ **it is not compliance.** every todo here is admitted because a redline **forces** it — so the
   sheet is the intersection of what defeats you, not the whole of what the law asks.
2. ⛔ **it is bounded by the redlines, which are bounded by the inventory** — a by-product of research
   rather than a systematic sweep of the field. ⚠️ **the inventory's group `I` proves the bound bites**:
   a second sweep of paragraphs nobody had asked for added 10 facts, moved the line count from 29 to 43,
   and **limited a published hard line** (`R14` against `H1`). an absence here is not a safe harbour.
3. ⚠️ **two todos rest on contested or unread ground.** `MX1` carries the unreconciled `(a)(9)`/`(a)(10)`
   tension; `ME5`–`ME8` approximate a *"due care"* standard that `CE7` records as unconstrued.
