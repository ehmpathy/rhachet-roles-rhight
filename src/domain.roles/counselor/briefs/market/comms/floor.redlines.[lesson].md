# floor.redlines

## .what

⭐ **a `floor.*` brief — a floor, never a peak.** the lines below are the **minimum** a program must
hold. to hold all of them does not make a program compliant; to cross any one makes it **defeated**,
and that asymmetry is the whole point of the form.

each line defuses one or more entries in `floor.landmines.[lesson].md`, cited by ID. ⛔ **a
redline with no landmine behind it is invented caution**, and the trace column is what makes that
visible to a reviewer.

⭐ **52 lines, and both walkers are served.** 🪨 **the six baseline lines (`H18`–`H23`) are plain** —
they state what the law forbids outright, for a reader who holds **no model** of it. every other line
is a ⚡ **surprise** — it corrects a model that a competent practitioner would otherwise carry in.

⛔ **the plain lines were absent from this file until 2026-08-06, and their absence was a defect
rather than a scope choice.** every line here traces to a landmine, and every landmine was framed as a
correction to a belief — so **a rule nobody was wrong about could not enter**, however large it was.
that made this family's *"not defeated"* promise **false for a nightwalker**. see `K7`.

> **recommendation disclaimer.** this is offered to the best of our knowledge, based on the
> information found in research — it carries **no guarantee of success or outcome**, and it must be
> reviewed by a licensed professional (attorney) against your own real scenario and current law
> before any reliance. it is groundwork for that review, not a substitute for it.

> **not legal advice.** informational groundwork for counsel to validate.

> ⚠️ **a note on gerunds.** this file trips `rule.forbid.gerunds` on text that cannot be altered:
> verbatim statutory language (`"maintaining a list"`, `"records documenting this process"`, `"an
> automatic telephone dialing system"`, `"using an artificial or prerecorded voice"`, `"bearing the
> signature"`, `"arising out of"`), verbatim judicial text (`"Lead Tracking System"`, `"calling
> lists"`, `"purchasing a vehicle from Toyota"`, `"without considering or resolving that issue"`), a
> party name (`Insurance Marketing Coalition`), and terms of art (`telemarketing`, `advertising`,
> `marketing call`, and `holding` in its judicial sense).
> to reword a quote is citation fraud; to rename a party breaks the search path to the case.

## ⭐ the redline test

not every risk is a redline, and a file that treats them alike is useless — it either drowns the real
lines in caution, or licenses the crossings it failed to rank. so one question decides admission:

> **if you cross this line, are you defeated *whatever else you do*?**

| answer | verdict |
|--------|---------|
| yes — **the text forbids it** | ⛔ a **hard** redline |
| yes — **your defense becomes unprovable** | ⛔ a **proof** redline |
| yes — **the claim itself is false**, so what is built on it inherits the error | ⛔ a **claim** redline |
| no — it raises risk, or it is one of several permitted options | ✅ **not a redline.** it is a judgment |

⛔ **note what this test does NOT ask: whether the line is surprising.** the baseline lines `H18`–`H23`
pass it more cleanly than anything else in the file — cross `H18` and you are defeated on the statute's
plainest words. ⚠️ **they were nevertheless missing**, because the admission test one rung *below* —
*"did a source contradict a competent reader's belief?"* — is **reader-relative**, and it silently
narrowed this reader-independent one. ⭐ **a rung's test can be quietly bounded by the rung it traces
into**, which is a defect of the ladder rather than of this file.

⭐ **the test is what makes this file publishable.** every line below is a consequence the sources
**force** — so a second reader with the same authorities draws the same lines. that is a very
different artifact from a list of things we would advise, and the difference is exactly the
`fullsun` boundary.

⚠️ **the test cuts across the inventory's two types, and deliberately so.** a landmine is classified
there by **how you would find it** — a wrong belief (I) or an absent one (II). a line is classified
**here** by **what happens if you cross it**. those are independent axes: `H1` defuses a type I
(`D1`), `H8` a type II (`G1`), and both are hard lines because the text forbids both. ⛔ **so do not
read the type mark as a severity** — a fact you merely never met defeats you exactly as completely as
one you believed wrongly.

⛔ **so where the law permits a choice, this file is silent.** that silence is deliberate and is
**not** a gap: a preference among permitted options is an appetite, and an appetite is not a floor.
if the question arises — "but which of the allowed options should we pick?" — it is a real question,
and this is the wrong document for it. ask counsel, against your own footprint.

## 1 — hard redlines: the text forbids it

⛔ **to cross one of these is non-compliant by construction — not by a judgment call, and not by a
risk assessment.** no control elsewhere repairs it.

| # | the line | why the text forbids it | trace |
|---|----------|------------------------|-------|
| **H1** | ⛔ never designate a single channel or keyword as the **only** way to revoke — ⚠️ **for messages inside `(a)(1)`–`(3)` or `(c)(2)`** | `§ 64.1200(a)(10)`: a caller *"may not designate an exclusive means to request revocation of consent."* ⭐ a `STOP`-only or web-form-only program fails on its architecture, before any message is sent. ⛔ **but read the scope**: `(a)(9)` exempts four classes from `(a)(1)(iii)` and, for two of them, **prescribes** `STOP` as *"the exclusive means."* the paragraphs pull opposite ways and no decision reconciles them — so this line governs your `(a)(1)`–`(3)` traffic and is **contested** for `(a)(9)` traffic | **D1 · I3** |
| **H2** | ⛔ never rest on a written policy you do not **run** | `§ 227(c)(5)` gives the defense to a defendant who *"has **established and implemented**, with due care"*. read the conjunction: the artifact and its exercise are both elements | **D6** |
| **H3** | ⛔ never let a registry copy age past **31 days**, and never run a scrub you cannot **document** | `§ 64.1200(c)(2)(i)(D)` requires a version obtained *"no more than 31 days prior to the date any call is made"* **and** that the caller *"maintains records documenting this process."* ⭐ the record is **part of** the standard | **D5** |
| **H4** | ⛔ never forward a suppression request beyond the caller or an affiliate without the consumer's **prior express permission** | `§ 64.1200(d)(3)`: you *"must obtain a consumer's prior express permission to share or forward"* it. ⚠️ the act that sounds more protective is itself gated | **D3** |
| **H5** | ⛔ never treat a vendor's custody of the list as a transfer of the liability | *"the person or entity on whose behalf the call is made **will be liable** for any failures to honor the do-not-call request."* custody is delegable; liability is not | **D2** |
| **H6** | ⛔ never exceed **ten business days** on a revocation, or drop an internal request before **5 years** | `§ 64.1200(a)(10)` sets the first; `(d)(6)`: *"A do-not-call request must be honored for 5 years from the time the request is made."* | **D1** |
| **H7** | ⛔ never build to **one clock** where two apply | ⚠️ the federal duty runs in **business** days; Florida's cure gate under `Fla. Stat. § 501.059` runs in **calendar** days. ⭐ ten business days can **exceed** fifteen calendar days whenever a holiday falls in the window — so a program on either clock alone can miss the other | **G2** |
| **H8** | ⛔ never call outside the **strictest** window that applies in a state you serve | `Cal. Pub. Util. Code § 2872(c)` sets a **9 a.m.** early boundary against the federal 8 a.m. a federal-window dialer is non-compliant for an hour every day in California | **G1** |
| **H9** | ⛔ never treat a written consent record as sufficient in a **live-consent** state | `Cal. Pub. Util. Code § 2874` requires an *"unrecorded, natural voice announcement"* that must *"[i]nquire as to whether the person called consents"*; Indiana offers a route where the message is *"immediately preceded by a live operator who obtains the subscriber's consent."* ⭐ **no written artifact satisfies either** — the statute asks for a live exchange | **A4** |
| **H10** | ⛔ never keep a do-not-call policy you cannot **produce on request** | `§ 64.1200(d)(1)`: the written policy must be *"available upon demand."* ⚠️ a policy that is run well but cannot be handed over does not meet the standard | **D7** |
| **H11** | ⛔ never scope training to your own staff alone | `(d)(2)` requires personnel be *"informed and trained in the existence and use of the do-not-call list"*, and `(c)(2)(i)(B)` reaches *"any entity assisting in its compliance."* ⭐ the duty crosses the org boundary into your vendors | **D8** |
| **H12** | ⛔ never place a call that omits the `(d)(4)` identification | the caller must give *"the name of the individual caller"*, the entity's name, and a contact number or address — which *"may not be a 900 number."* four elements, in the call itself | **D9** |
| **H13** | ⛔ never obtain registry access through a **shared or resold** subscription | `(c)(2)(i)(E)` requires purchase from the administrator with no cost-share arrangement that divides access costs among client sellers. ⚠️ the safe harbour can fail on **procurement alone**, with the scrub itself faultless | **D10** |
| **H14** | ⛔ never treat a **transactional or informational** message as consent-free when it is autodialed or carries a prerecorded voice to a wireless number | `§ 64.1200(a)(1)` bars such a call *"(other than a call made for emergency purposes or is made with the **prior express consent** of the called party)"*. ⭐ purpose selects the **standard** — written for a solicitation, oral-sufficient otherwise — it never removes the requirement | **I1** |
| **H15** | ⛔ never operate under an `(a)(9)` exemption while you meet only part of its conditions | each of the four classes carries a **conjunctive** set: content limits, a message-count cap, a length bound, and an opt-out deadline. ⚠️ **to miss one condition is to be outside the exemption entirely**, back under `(a)(1)(iii)` with no consent on file | **I2** |
| **H16** | ⛔ never disconnect before **15 seconds or four rings**, exceed the **three percent** abandonment rate, or run a campaign without the `(a)(7)` records | `§ 64.1200(a)(6)`–`(7)` set all three, and `(a)(7)(iii)` makes the record itself a duty: *"The seller or telemarketer must maintain records establishing compliance."* ⭐ a dialer configuration is a **regulated** parameter, not an operations preference | **I6** |
| **H17** | ⛔ never seize two or more lines of a multi-line business at once, and never dial to **detect the line type** | `(a)(5)` and `(a)(8)` bar both outright. ⚠️ neither turns on consent, purpose, or list hygiene — a program perfect on every other line here still crosses these on **equipment behaviour alone** | **I10** |

had to be a surprise. ⭐ **they are the lines a reader with no model of this law is defeated by**, and
their absence made this file's *"not defeated"* promise false for that reader (`K7`).

🌞 **a lightwalker may skim this block.** 🌑 **a nightwalker should start here and read nowhere else
first.**

| # | the line | why the text forbids it | trace |
|---|----------|------------------------|-------|
| 🪨 **H18** | ⛔ never place an autodialed or prerecorded-voice call to a wireless number without consent, **whatever your industry or volume** | `47 U.S.C. § 227(b)(1)(A)(iii)` bars a call to a number *"assigned to a … cellular telephone service"* made *"using any automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice"*. ⭐ **no sector, size, or volume threshold appears in the text** — one call is a violation, and the right of action is private | **K1** |
| 🪨 **H19** | ⛔ never run a consent program **without** a suppression program | `§ 227(c)(5)` is a **second** private right, on its own trigger — *"more than one telephone call within any 12-month period"* — and its defense has no consent element. ⭐ **two tracks demand two artifacts**; neither substitutes for the other | **K2** |
| 🪨 **H20** | ⛔ never apply the lower consent standard to a message that **includes or introduces an advertisement** | `§ 64.1200(a)(2)` requires *"prior express written consent"* for such a call. ⚠️ the lower *prior express* standard is for the non-solicitation call to the same number — ⭐ **purpose selects the standard, never whether consent is needed** | **K3** |
| 🪨 **H21** | ⛔ never let an internal campaign label decide the purpose gate | the text reaches a call that *"includes or introduces an advertisement"* — so **one promotional sentence appended to an order confirmation** puts that message inside the paragraph. ⭐ **the content decides; your classification of it decides not at all** | **K4** |
| 🪨 **H22** | ⛔ never exclude a business contact's mobile from the program on the ground that the call is B2B | the hook attaches to the **number** — *"assigned to a … cellular telephone service"* — and the text does not ask who the person is or why they were called. 🔶 the outer edge of that phrase is litigated and unsurveyed here | **K5** |
| 🪨 **H23** | ⛔ never treat a text-only program as outside the regime | `§ 64.1200(a)(9)`: *"the term 'call' includes a text message, including a short message service (SMS) call."* ⚠️ the Supreme Court **assumed** rather than decided the point, so the agency's construction governs and the judicial question stays open — ⛔ **a program built on the openness of that question is built on a reservation, not a holding** | **K6** |

## 2 — proof redlines: the defense becomes unprovable

⚠️ **these are not forbidden acts. they are acts that destroy your ability to *prove* a defense you
genuinely have** — which lands in the same place, one step later. every one is **build-time**: the
proof cannot be manufactured after the fact.

| # | the line | why the defense fails | trace |
|---|----------|----------------------|-------|
| **P1** | ⛔ never send to a number whose authorization **for that number** you cannot produce | the consent `§ 64.1200(f)(9)` defines is an agreement *"bearing the signature of the person called"* that authorizes delivery **to the telephone number specified**. a record keyed to a person or an account cannot answer the question the regulation asks | **C1 · C2** |
| **P2** | ⛔ never plan to assemble the proof at claim time | `Dish` failed twice on exactly this: *"very little competent evidence on how the Lead Tracking System was formulated or how calling lists were derived"*, and *"no testimony from any representative … who had pe[rsonal] knowledge."* ⭐ a custodian cannot acquire personal knowledge retroactively | **C3 · C5** |
| **P3** | ⛔ never accept a vendor's certification **in place of** the artifact | an assurance that leads are compliant is a contractual promise between the vendor and the sender. it is not the artifact a court asks for | **C4** |
| **P4** | ⛔ never rely on a clause whose flow does not say, **at the act**, that the act is acceptance | `Soliman` enumerated *"no statement that the act would constitute agreement"* among the features that defeated notice — and a destination page headed as terms for the **website** among them too. a link labeled only "terms" satisfies neither | **E3** |
| **P5** | ⛔ never assume the clause reaches numbers from outside **your own** contract flow | `Knutson`: *"A reasonable person in Knutson's position could not be expected to understand that purchasing a vehicle from Toyota would simultaneously bind him or her to any contract with Sirius XM."* ⭐ formation is answered **per source** | **E4 · E5** |
| **P6** | ⛔ never answer a coverage question from a **certificate** | the certificate is a summary. the complete policy, the full endorsement schedule, and the form edition stamp are what decide it — and ⛔ **none of this is curable after a claim** | **F1 · F5** |
| **P7** | ⛔ never search an exclusion by its **title** | a clause that bars injury arising out of an act that violates a statute reaches the TCPA without a mention of it. ⚠️ and a buy-back is worth what its **replacement text** says, never what its deletion says | **F2 · F4** |
| **P8** | ⛔ never destroy a consent record inside the **claim window** | the TCPA states no limitations period of its own, so a claim runs on `28 U.S.C. § 1658(a)` — **four years**. ⚠️ **that it governs a TCPA claim is an inference, not a read holding.** the internal-list duty's express **5 years** is the longer floor, so a single 5-year retention answers both | **C6** |
| **P9** | ⛔ never buy or renew cover without asking whether **defence costs erode the limit** | ⭐ on a per-message statute the defence spend on a class action can rival the indemnity — so this term can be worth more than the limit's headline number. ⚠️ ask in the same breath: is there a **sublimit**, and is it a **duty to defend** or indemnity only | **F6** |
| **P10** | ⛔ never let a revocation-confirmation message carry promotional content, repeat, or land later than **five minutes** without a recorded reason | `§ 64.1200(a)(12)` presumes a confirmation inside consent when it *"merely confirms"*, is *"the only additional message"*, and arrives within five minutes. ⭐ beyond that the statute shifts the burden onto you: *"the sender will have to make a showing that such delay was reasonable"* — and a queue latency nobody logged is a showing you cannot make | **I4** |

## 3 — claim redlines: the statement itself is false

⚠️ **these govern what may be *asserted*** — in a memo, a board paper, a reserve model, or a broker
conversation. each is a claim the sources contradict, so what is built on it inherits the error.

| # | never assert that… | the contradiction | trace |
|---|-------------------|-------------------|-------|
| **R1** | a consent record answers the do-not-call track | ⛔ `§ 227(c)(5)`'s defense has **no consent element**; the subsection is framed around **objection** | **A1** |
| **R2** | "we do not autodial" answers `§ 227(b)` | the statute reaches a call made *"using an automatic telephone dialing system **or** an artificial or prerecorded voice"* — two hooks, joined by "or" | **A2** |
| **R3** | the do-not-call duties are scoped to robocalls | ⛔ `§ 227(c)` is **dialer-agnostic**; a manually-dialed live marketing call sits inside it | **A3** |
| **R4** | the arbitration clause caps total exposure | ⛔ it reaches **private** claims only. an attorney general's action is not a class action, so no waiver touches it | **A5** |
| **R5** | the 2025 vacatur removed the written-consent baseline | `IMC v. FCC` footnote 1: *"The 2012 Order is not at issue in this case."* — and the 2012 Order is what carries that baseline | **B1** |
| **R6** | *Duguid* narrowed the statute generally, or settled that texts are covered | it narrowed the **autodialer** hook; the Court noted *"The statute separately prohibits calls using 'an artificial or prerecorded voice'"*, and proceeded on texts *"without considering or resolving that issue"* | **B2 · B3** |
| **R7** | `135 F.4th 230` is good authority | ⛔ **withdrawn and substituted** on 2025-08-22 — cite `AT&T v. FCC`, No. 24-60223 (5th Cir. Aug. 22, 2025) | **B4** |
| **R8** | an FCC forfeiture figure from another service states TCPA exposure | ⭐ **the count method transfers; the dollar figures do not** — the amount tier is service-specific | **H1** |
| **R9** | one reserve figure covers "TCPA risk" | the tracks carry different units, caps, and claimants; a reserve against the wrong track is wrong in **either** direction | **H2** |
| **R10** | the statutory maximum approximates the expected loss | ⭐ **the statutory maximum is not what gets paid; it is the leverage that decides what does** | **H3** |
| **R11** | a five-state read generalizes to fifty | ⛔ those five produced **three different consent architectures**. ⭐ the variance is a **measured result** — a uniform sample could be generalized; this one cannot | **G3** |
| **R12** | one review settles whether your signup flow forms a contract | `Dahdah`: *"state law likewise governs whether the parties formed a binding contract to arbitrate."* ⭐ **one flow can form in one state and fail in another**, so a single federal review does not answer it for a national footprint | **E7** |
| **R13** | every violative call is an actionable `§ 227(c)` claim | ⭐ **this one runs your way, and asserting it costs you money.** `§ 227(c)(5)` reaches only a person who received *"more than one telephone call within any 12-month period"* by or for the same entity. a single violative call creates no `(c)` claim — while a single autodialed message can create a `(b)` one. ⚠️ a reserve built on the symmetric assumption **over-states** | **D11** |
| **R14** | ⚠️ **`H1` reaches every message you send** | ⛔ **it does not — and this is a line about our own line.** `(a)(10)`'s no-exclusive-means rule is scoped to messages under `(a)(1)`–`(3)` and `(c)(2)`; for two of the four classes `(a)(9)` exempts, the regulation **prescribes** `STOP` as *"the exclusive means."* ⭐ a fraud-alert or appointment-reminder program built to `H1` may be built against the paragraph that governs it. ⬜ **unreconciled — no decision was read** | **I3** |
| **R15** | an internal suppression request automatically silences your affiliates | `(d)(5)`: absent a specific contrary request it *"shall apply to the particular entity making the call … and **will not** apply to affiliated entities unless the consumer reasonably would expect them to be included."* ⭐ **this one runs your way** — the default is narrow, and a group-wide suppression exceeds the rule | **I8** |
| **R16** | every marketing call is a "telephone solicitation" that the registry duty reaches | `(f)(15)` excludes a call to a person with *"prior express invitation or permission"*, to a person *"with whom the caller has an established business relationship"*, or by a tax-exempt nonprofit. ⚠️ **but do not invert it either** — the term as `(f)(15)` uses it has no definition in what was read, so its boundary is unknown in both directions | **I9** |
| **R17** | ⭐ a protection you hold is a **fact about you** rather than a condition you must prove | ⛔ **every one carries conditions, and they are yours to establish.** to rely on `R13` you must show the call count per number per 12 months; to rely on `R2` you must show your dialer is not an autodialer — **the whole of `Duguid`**; `I2`'s exemptions are **conjunctive**, so one missed condition puts you back under `(a)(1)(iii)` with no consent on file | **J6** |
| **R18** | a wireless number, a prerecorded residential call, or a registered number is **always** inside its prohibition | ⛔ four carve-outs say otherwise, and each **cuts your way**: `(a)(1)(iv)`'s 15-day ported-number grace, `(a)(3)(ii)`–`(v)`'s numerical limits in place of consent, `(a)(2)`'s nonprofit/HIPAA drop to *prior express*, and `(c)(2)(iii)`'s personal-relationship exception. ⚠️ **a reserve blind to these over-states** | **J1 · J2 · J3 · J4** |
| **R19** | the registry duty and the consent duty are answered by **one** artifact | `(c)(2)(ii)` gives an independent route out of the registry duty — *"a signed, written agreement … [that] includes the telephone number to which the calls may be placed"* — which is **narrower** than `(f)(9)` consent and does not substitute for it. ⛔ **and it is a proof, not a status**: the permission may exist and be unprovable | **J5** |

## ⭐ what a clean sheet does NOT mean

⚠️ **the redlines are a floor, and a floor is not a certificate.** three limits, stated plainly
because each is a way this file could be misused:

1. ⛔ **to hold every line does not make a program compliant.** these are the moves that defeat you;
   compliance requires the affirmative build, which is in the `floor.howto.*` briefs.
2. ⛔ **the list is bounded by the inventory behind it**, and that inventory is a by-product of
   research rather than a systematic sweep. an absence here is not a safe-harbour.
3. ⚠️ **a redline can go stale.** each rests on text or a decision that can move — `§ 64.1200(a)(10)`
   has an open proceeding against it, and the newest authority cited here is months old at the date
   below.

| question | answer |
|----------|--------|

## the honest gap

- ⛔ **`§ 227(c)(5)`'s "due care" is unconstrued.** `H2` restates the statute's conjunction, which is
  certain; what a court demands of a program under that standard is **not** — no decision that applies
  it was read.
- ⬜ **the state lines are five-state.** `H7`, `H8`, and `H9` come from the surveyed five. per `R11`
  the sample forbids extrapolation, so a served state outside those five has **no line here at all** —
  which is a hole in the floor, not a clearance.
- ⚠️ **`P4` and `P5` rest on formation cases that all turn on the *absence* of an agreement.** they
  establish how formation **fails**; no decision read here holds that a validly-formed waiver survives
  every challenge, and unconscionability was not researched.
- ✅ **two gaps this file previously declared are now closed, and the closures are recorded rather
  than quietly deleted.** it said the residential/wireless boundary was *"unresolved, so every
  group-`D`-derived line carries an unstated scope question"* — `§ 64.1200(e)` answers it: `(c)` and
  `(d)` reach *"text messages to wireless telephone numbers."* it also said *"no line here addresses
  transactional or non-telemarketing messages"* — `H14` now does.
- ⚠️ **but the first closure arrives with a narrower gap attached.** `(e)` extends the program rules
  only *"to the extent described in"* FCC Report and Order 03-153, **which was not read**. so the
  group-`D` lines are confirmed for wireless with their **bounds unread** — a better-shaped hole than
  the one it replaces, not an absent one.
- ⛔ **`R14` is a line against one of our own lines, and it is the sharpest caution in this file.**
  `(a)(9)` prescribes an exclusive opt-out means for two exempted classes while `(a)(10)` forbids one.
  ⬜ **no decision reconciles them**, so `H1` is stated with a scope it did not previously carry, and
  a reader with `(a)(9)` traffic needs counsel rather than this table.
- ⬜ **`R16`'s exclusion turns on a term with no captured definition.** `(f)(15)` leans on
  *"established business relationship"*; the only definition read, at `(f)(6)`, is expressly scoped to
