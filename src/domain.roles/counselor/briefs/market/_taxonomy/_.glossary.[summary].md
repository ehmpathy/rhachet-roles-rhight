# _.glossary — the market-mechanics term index

> scan-first index of the terms used across these market-mechanics law briefs. one line each, with
> a link to its full `term.$slug.definition.md` and a status of whether that definition is authored.
>
> this is a **navigation artifact** — it makes no independent factual claim, so it inherits its
> citations from the term briefs it points to (per `rule.require.seven-distinct-citations` exception).

## why a glossary first

these programs go by many overloaded names, and the wrong label imports the wrong body of law.
"referral program", "affiliate program", "loyalty program", "cashback", "incentivized review",
"referral selling", "lead-gen fee" are **not** interchangeable — each is a distinct term with a
distinct legal home. name the things before we classify them.

## how the terms relate

the one-tree map of how these terms nest (referral ⊂ word-of-mouth promotion; affiliate ⊂
referral-with-commission; cashback ⊂ rewards-instrument; etc.) lives in
`taxonomy.program-types-map.[lesson].md`.

## per-term example catalogs

each term carries an `term.$slug.example._.catalog.md` rollup — who ran a program of that type and
whether it is **still in use** (in use / modified / discontinued), with a cited source + last-verified
date — plus one `term.$slug.example.$exampleslug.md` leaf per real instance.
