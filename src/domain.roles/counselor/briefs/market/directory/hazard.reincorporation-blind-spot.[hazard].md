# hazard.reincorporation-blind-spot

## .what

entity grain is the answer that exits four regimes at once. this is what it **costs**.

a business can dissolve and re-form. the new entity has a new name, a new registration number, a new
EIN — and **no history**. every badge resets to `⬜`. the same people, the same trucks, the same
phone number, and a directory that has been made structurally unable to say so.

⭐ **and the sharp part is not that the gap exists. it is that the obvious fix re-opens every regime
entity grain closed.**

> **not legal advice.** informational groundwork for counsel to validate; verify current text before
> any reliance. claims are tagged `[observed]` / `[inferred]` / `[unverified]`.

## ⭐⭐ .the trap: the only way to link two entities is through a person

to say *"New Co is Old Co"* you must match on something that persists across the dissolution. the
candidates are all **natural persons or their attributes**:

| linkage key | what it actually is | grain |
|---|---|---|
| officer or member name | ⭐ **a natural person** | ⛔ person |
| registered agent | often the owner; sometimes a service | ⚠️ mixed |
| the trade qualifier / master license holder | ⭐ **a natural person** — the credential is theirs | ⛔ person |
| business address | a place, but frequently a home | ⚠️ mixed |
| phone number | ⚠️ frequently a personal mobile | ⚠️ mixed |
| EIN / registration number | genuinely entity-grain | ✅ entity — ⛔ **but it is exactly what changes** |

⛔ **the one key that is cleanly entity-grain is the one the reincorporation replaces.** every key
that survives the event is a person, or is person-adjacent.

⭐ **so the linkage is not a feature that can be added carefully. it is a grain change.** and the
grain carries four separate exits:

| regime | the exit | verbatim |
|---|---|---|
| **FCRA** | *"consumer"* is a natural person | `[observed]` § 1681a(c): *"The term 'consumer' means an individual."* |
| **right of publicity** (all five) | reaches only a natural person | `[observed]` FL § 540.08(1) *"any natural person"*; IN § 32-36-1-6 *"a living or deceased natural person"*; TX (Tex. Prop. Code ch. 26) § 26.002 *"an individual"*; CA § 3344(a)(1) *"in the case of a minor, the prior consent of their parent or legal guardian"* `[inferred]`; NY § 51 *"Any person whose name … is used within this state"* `[inferred]` |
| **CA DELETE Act** | data broker sells *a consumer's* personal information | `[observed]` § 1798.99.80(c) |
| **CCPA** | *"consumer"* is a natural person | `[observed]` § 1798.140(i): *"'Consumer' means a natural person who is a California resident"* |

**a link built on an officer's name is person-grain data published on a commercial page, which is the
precise fact pattern all four address.**

## .the exposure, stated plainly

`[inferred]` a reincorporation-link feature would:

1. **re-enter right of publicity** in all five states — the officer's *name* used commercially
2. **re-enter the consumer-report gate** if a reader uses the link for an eligibility decision — and
   an *"is this the same guy who took my deposit"* signal is close to that purpose by design
3. **re-enter CCPA and the DELETE Act** — the officer becomes a *"consumer"* again
4. ⭐ **and it publishes an ADVERSE inference**, which `rule.require.asymmetric-disclosure` forbids
   outright. *"New Co appears related to Old Co, which has 4 judgments"* is an adverse assertion about
   a natural person, arrived at by the publisher's own analysis.
5. ⚠️ **and the inference can be wrong.** `[inferred]` at scale, name matches across entity records
   will produce false links — an officer with a common name, a genuinely unrelated successor
   business, an honest dissolution after a partner's death. **a false reincorporation link is a
   defamation-shaped harm with an economic loss attached**, and the accuracy layer sharpens at entity
   grain exactly as `hazard.stale-public-record-status` found.

## ⭐ .what remains publishable — the terrain, not the synthesis

the underlying facts are public and each is individually safe. what is unsafe is **the conclusion
drawn across them**.

| publishable | why |
|---|---|
| *"registered 2026-03, per the state registry"* | ⭐ a **presence-fact with a favorable-or-neutral status**, sourced and dated |
| *"this registration is 4 months old"* | arithmetic on a published date; asserts no relationship |
| *"first seen in this directory on …"* | a fact about **the directory**, not about the subject |
| ⛔ *"formerly operated as Old Co"* | ⛔ **the publisher's inference**, drawn through a natural person |
| ⛔ *"related to an entity with judgments"* | ⛔ that inference **and** an adverse item |

⭐⭐ **the settlement, and it is the same move that settled the whole design:** publish the
**age of the record**, never the **identity of the predecessor**. a consumer who sees
*"registered 4 months ago"* has the signal that matters — this business has no track record here —
and no link, no adverse fact, and no claim about any natural person has been asserted.

⭐ **note what this is:** the age of a registration is a **favorable-or-neutral presence-fact**. it
publishes cleanly under `rule.require.asymmetric-disclosure` precisely because it says what IS
(a registration date) rather than what is suspected. **the rule that governs publication answers this
hazard too**, which is the fourth distinct layer it has reached.

## .the honest accounting

⚠️ **this control does not close the gap. it declines to close it.**

a determined bad actor re-forms, and a consumer who reads only the badges sees `⬜` across the board
rather than a warning. the age signal is real but weak — many honest new businesses are also four
months old, which is the point: **the signal cannot distinguish them, and that is a feature of the
regime, not a defect in any one implementation.**

⭐ **the cost is paid to purchase four exits.** it is a trade the design makes deliberately, and it
should be recorded as a trade rather than presented as a solved problem. any future proposal to
"just link the entities" is a proposal to **re-enter four regimes**, and it should be evaluated at
that price.

### ⭐⭐ .UPDATED — the gap is narrower than this brief first priced it

> **there is one comparison that surfaces the pattern and re-enters no regime: the registry date
> beside the subject's OWN claimed tenure.**

⛔ **this brief's linkage table asked the wrong question of itself.** it enumerated keys that link
**two entities** — officer, agent, qualifier, address, phone — found every one of them person-grain or
person-adjacent, and concluded the gap could only be declined. **it never considered a comparison
between two facts about the SAME entity.**

registry says 2026-03; the profile says *"serving Indy since 1998."* both facts are already on the
page, neither is person-grain, and no inference across entity records is drawn. **it costs no grain
and it re-enters no regime.**

⚠️ **and it narrows the gap rather than closes it — the honest accounting above still stands.** the
mismatch appears only where the subject volunteers a tenure claim, which is precisely what a
reincorporated operator has reason to omit. and the mismatch is **ambiguous** — relocation, merger,
conversion, and counted apprenticeship years all produce it innocently — so it may be **rendered**
and never **labeled**.

⭐ **the boundary is owned in full by `define.boundary.registry-date-vs-claimed-tenure`**, which also
carries the § 230(f)(3) attribution control that keeps the tenure claim the subject's own. **this
brief keeps the trade; that brief keeps the one increment recovered from it.**

## .the test

> **"does this statement link two entities through a natural person?"**

- it states a date, an age, or a registry fact about one entity → publishable
- it names or implies a predecessor, an officer, or a shared person → ⛔ person grain, four regimes
- it draws an adverse inference across entities → ⛔ blocked by `rule.require.asymmetric-disclosure`
  regardless of grain

## .enforcement

> ⭐ **these are DERIVED checks, not original mandates** — each says what to verify so this body of
> law is not tripped. the convention is declared in `hazard._.catalogue` § *the convention*; the
> original mandates live in `rule.*` files. **a `[hazard]` that enforces is checkable, not normative.**

- publication of a **predecessor-entity link** = **blocker** (person-grain linkage; four regimes)
- publication of an **officer, member, or registered-agent name** on a profile = **blocker**
- an **adverse inference** drawn across entities = **blocker** (`rule.require.asymmetric-disclosure`)
- publication of a **registration date or age** = ✅ permitted, sourced and dated
- any **internal** linkage data surfaced through an API field, export column, sort order, or ranking
  = **blocker** — the asymmetry is a property of the **output**, not of the copy

## ⚠️ .what is NOT covered here

- ⚠️ `[unverified]` **successor-liability doctrine** — whether a successor entity inherits the
  predecessor's obligations is a real body of state law, un-captured. it bears on whether the link is
  *true*, not on whether it may lawfully be published.
- ⚠️ `[unverified]` **defamation and false-light** exposure for a wrong link. truth answers
  defamation; a wrong link is not true.
- ⚠️ `[unverified]` **state corporate-registry terms of use** — several registries restrict bulk
  reuse of officer data. un-swept.

## .publishability

☀️ **fullsun** (share) — the **terrain**: that entity grain has a reincorporation gap, that every
linkage key is a natural person or person-adjacent, and that the age-of-registration signal is the
control that stays inside the rule. all of it reads off public statutes and public registry
structure. no client identity, no coverage data.

🔒 **held back — the synthesis:** whether a given adopter holds linkage data internally, at what match
confidence, and what it does with it. that is simultaneously a plaintiff's roadmap **and** the exact
material that would prove person-grain processing. it belongs in a restricted artifact, not here.

⚠️ the split is deliberate and worth naming: **the gap is publishable; an adopter's posture toward the
gap is not.** an adversary who reads this learns that a reincorporation defeats the badges — which
they would learn from one afternoon with any such product anyway. what they do not learn is whether
a particular directory detects it.
