# define.boundary.registry-date-vs-claimed-tenure

> **not legal advice.** informational groundwork for counsel to validate; verify current text before
> reliance. every claim is tagged `[observed]` (quoted from a captured source), `[inferred]`
> (reasoning over those sources), or `[unverified]` (not yet sourced).

## .what

**"established in 1998" is two different facts, and a directory that renders one label conflates
them.**

| the fact | its referent | who says it | provenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **registry date** | ⭐ the **entity** — when this legal person was formed | the state | ✅ verified, sourced, dated |
| **claimed tenure** | ⭐ the **operator** — how long this practice has traded | the subject | 🟡 self-attested |

they are usually the same number. after a **reincorporation** they are not, and the divergence is the
whole point of this brief.

## .why the boundary matters

`hazard.reincorporation-blind-spot` establishes the trade: entity grain exits four regimes at once,
and the price is that a dissolve-and-reform resets every badge. its settlement is to publish the
**age of the registration**, never the **identity of the predecessor** — because every key that would
link the two entities is a natural person.

⛔ **that settlement is correct and it leaves one thing on the table.** the registry date alone is a
weak signal: many honest businesses are four months old. what the directory never does is place it
**beside the subject's own claim**.

## ⭐⭐ the mismatch — the ONLY place the pattern surfaces without person-linkage

> **registry says 2026-03. the subject's own profile says *"serving Indy since 1998."***

⭐ **look at what that comparison requires: no new datum.** no officer name, no registered agent, no
qualifier, no address match, no inference across entity records. **both facts are already on the page,
and neither is person-grain.**

| linkage route | what it needs | grain |
|---|---|---|
| officer-name match across registrations | a natural person's name | ⛔ person — four regimes |
| shared qualifier licence | a natural person's credential | ⛔ person — four regimes |
| ⭐ **registry date vs the subject's own claim** | ⭐ **no datum we do not already hold** | ✅ **entity — no regime re-entered** |

**this is the one surface where the reincorporation pattern becomes visible at entity grain.** the
hazard brief's honest accounting says the gap is *declined rather than closed* — and this narrows it
by a genuine increment, at zero grain cost, which is why it earns its own boundary.

⛔ **and it narrows it rather than closes it.** the mismatch appears only where the subject **claims a
tenure**. a reincorporated operator who leaves that field blank produces no mismatch, and a bare
`⬜` beside a four-month registration is exactly what an honest new business shows. **the control
depends on the subject's own volunteered claim, which the party it would catch has every reason to
withhold.**

## ⛔ .what we may render, and what we may NOT

the two facts are individually publishable, for **two different reasons** — and that is the load-
bearing part:

| what | may we render it | why |
|---|---|---|
| the registry date, sourced and dated | ✅ **yes** | a favorable-or-neutral presence-fact; `rule.require.asymmetric-disclosure` publishes it because it says what IS |
| the subject's tenure claim, labeled `claimed` | ✅ **yes** | `[observed]` **§ 230(f)(3)** puts content on whoever is *"responsible, in whole or in part, for the creation or development"* — this is **theirs**, and it stays theirs while it is attributed |
| ⛔ a computed **delta** — *"claims 28 years, registered 4 months ago"* | ⛔ **no** | ⭐ **the arithmetic is OURS.** a difference we compute and display is our assertion, and its plain meaning is *this subject may be lying* |
| ⛔ a **flag, badge, or rank penalty** on the mismatch | ⛔ **no** | a conclusion-word about the subject; `rule.require.asymmetric-disclosure` forbids an adverse assertion outright |
| ⛔ a **restatement** of their claim as ours — *"in business 28 years"* | ⛔ **no** | ⭐ the § 230 shield is lost the moment we drop the attribution. **the label is what keeps the statement theirs** |

> ⭐⭐ **render both, attribute both, compute no relation.** the reader does the subtraction; we do not.

⚠️ **and that is not a dodge.** the two rows sit adjacent with their provenance labels intact. a reader
who notices the gap has noticed a real thing about facts we lawfully published. a reader who does not
has lost no fact we were entitled to tell them.

## ⛔ .the innocent explanations — why the delta must never be labeled

`[inferred]` a registry/claim mismatch has ordinary causes, and they are common:

- a genuine **relocation** — the operator traded 20 years in another state and registered here in 2026
- a **merger, sale, or re-domestication** that produced a new registration for an unbroken practice
- a **conversion** — sole proprietor to LLC, which is a first registration for an old business
- an owner who counts their **apprenticeship or employment** years, honestly, as "in the trade since"

⛔ **so the mismatch is genuinely ambiguous, and a label would assert the adverse reading of an
ambiguity.** ⚠️ **that is the false-light shape rather than the defamation shape** — the components
are true and the juxtaposition carries the meaning — which is `G-false-light`, the cluster's highest-
priority un-swept question. **the control that narrows the reincorporation gap runs straight into the
gap the cluster has least authority on.**

## ⭐ .the parallel that generalizes — a lookup returns a STATUS, never a boolean

this row has the same shape as the licence row, and the shape is worth the name:

| row | looks like | actually is |
|---|---|---|
| licence | *found / not found* | ⛔ a **status** — *active clean · active disciplined · expired · suspended · revoked*, and only the first is assertable |
| established date | *found / not found* | ⛔ a **date with a referent** — true about the entity, and silent about the operator |

⭐ **both mistakes are the same mistake: a row collapsed one axis too far.** the collapse is invisible
because the collapsed value is still true — a revoked licence *was* found, and the registry date *is*
correct. **a row that returns a true answer to a question nobody asked is the hardest defect to see in
review.**

see `define.boundary.presence-fact-vs-absence-fact`, which owns the status correction in full.

## .the test

> **"is this statement about the ENTITY, about the OPERATOR, or about the gap between them?"**

- about the entity, from a registry, sourced and dated → ✅ publish
- about the operator, from the subject, labeled `claimed` → ✅ publish, **attributed**
- ⛔ about the **gap** — a delta, a flag, a rank, a sort, an order that surfaces it → **ours, and
  adverse**

## .enforcement

> ⭐ **these are DERIVED checks** — each says what to verify so the rules this brief composes are not
> tripped. the original mandates live in `rule.*` files.

- a computed **difference** between registry date and claimed tenure, rendered in any form = **blocker**
- a **flag, badge, warn-icon, or rank adjustment** keyed on the mismatch = **blocker**
- the subject's tenure claim rendered **without** its `claimed` attribution = **blocker** (the
  attribution is what keeps `[observed]` § 230(f)(3) on their side of the line)
- a tenure claim **restated in our own voice** — *"in business 28 years"* = **blocker**, same reason
- ⛔ the mismatch exposed through a **sort order, filter, export column, or API field** = **blocker**
  — the asymmetry is a property of the output, and a sort is output
- the registry date rendered **without** its source and as-of date = **blocker**
  (`hazard.stale-public-record-status`)
- ✅ **permitted:** both rows adjacent, each attributed, each dated, with no computed relation between
  them

## .publishability

☀️ **fullsun** (share) — the boundary is a composition of a public statute (§ 230(f)(3)) and a
structural observation about state registries. no client identity, no coverage data, no applied
mechanic.

⭐ **and this one is stronger for the publication, for the same reason the disclosure rule is.** a
directory that renders a tenure claim beside a registry date **without** a computed delta looks, to a
critic, like it missed the signal. **the restraint is only legible if the reason is published.** an
undisclosed restraint reads as an oversight.

⚠️ **what is held back:** whether an adopter *detects* the mismatch internally, at what threshold, and
