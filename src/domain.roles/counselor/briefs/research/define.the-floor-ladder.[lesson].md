# define.the-floor-ladder

## .what

⭐ **how a research pass becomes a floor: three rungs, each a projection of the one below.**

```
      the sources
           │  sweep ── twice: for what they DEMAND, and for what they GRANT
           ▼
  ①  floor.landmines     FACTS   — what defeats a careful reader
           │  the redline test: "cross it and are you defeated whatever else you do?"
           ▼
  ②  floor.redlines      LINES   — the imperatives, each traced to a fact by ID
           │  the must/can't × establish/eliminate projection
           ▼
  ③  floor.mustcants     TODOS   — the action and its done-condition, traced to a line by ID
```

⭐ **the label on each arrow is the rung's admission test, and it is the whole guarantee.** an item
enters a rung only where that test forces it — so the scrub is in the rule of admission, never in a
pass afterwards.

⛔ **each rung is a separate file, and the separation is the guarantee rather than tidiness.** bundled,
a fact and an imperative read alike, and invented caution becomes invisible to a reviewer.

⭐ **subject-agnostic.** any domain with a body of authority — a regulation, an API contract, a
protocol spec, a vendor agreement — climbs the same ladder.

> **not legal advice.** informational groundwork for counsel to validate.

> ⚠️ **a note on gerunds.** this file trips `rule.forbid.gerunds` on terms of art quoted from the
> worked example (`telemarketing`).

---

## ⭐ .why — each rung LOSES information, and that is the point

the instinct is that a distillate **adds** — insight, judgment, synthesis. ⛔ **this ladder does the
opposite, on purpose.** every rung is a strict filter over the one below:

| the step | what it drops | ⭐ what the drop buys |
|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| ① → ② | every fact that raises risk **without** defeat | ⭐ **appetite** — a preference among permitted options fails the redline test **by construction**, so it cannot enter |
| ② → ③ | ⛔ **the order** — the projection classifies and refuses to sequence | ⭐ **a plan is a spend decision**; a classification is not, so the artifact stays publishable |

⭐ **so the ladder's guarantee is negative and mechanical: no item enters a rung that its own admission
test does not force.** that is what makes each rung shippable — the scrub is in the rule of admission,
never in a pass afterwards.

⚠️ **and it is why you cannot skip a rung.** to go from sources straight to todos is to write a plan:
you carry judgment you never had to justify, and no reviewer can tell which sentences the sources force
from which you chose. ⛔ **the trace column at each rung is the audit trail, and a skipped rung has no
column to check.**

## the three rungs

| | ① **landmines** | ② **redlines** | ③ **mustcants** |
|---|---|---|---|
| holds | **facts**, with authority | **imperatives** | **todos**, with done-conditions |
| mood | indicative — *"the burden sits on the sender"* | negative — *"never send without…"* | imperative + verifiable — *"write it; done when you can produce it"* |
| ⭐ **admission test** | ⛔ **two questions, and you owe both**: *what does this demand of someone who has **never heard of it**?* (🪨 plain) · *did a source **contradict** a competent reader's belief?* (⚡ surprise) | **"cross it and are you defeated whatever else you do?"** | **is an action available, and does it make a fact true or stop a practice?** |
| prescribes | ⛔ **no action at all** | the lines to hold | the actions, ⛔ **in no order** |
| traces to | the source, verbatim | a landmine, by ID | a redline, by ID |
| ⛔ must refuse | to say *"so you should…"* | to rank permitted options | to sequence, to price, to certify |
| its own bound | what was **swept and met** | bounded by ① | bounded by ② |

### ① landmines — the facts

⛔ **a landmine is what a reader steps on — and readers arrive with different amounts of light.**

| the walker | what they hold | ⛔ what defeats them | the mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌞 **lightwalker** | a live model of the domain | the **surprise**: a false belief that survives inspection, or a duty they never met | ⚡ |
| 🌑 **nightwalker** | ⭐ **no model at all** — they may not know the instrument reaches them | the **plain rule**, stated in the instrument's most obvious words | 🪨 |

⭐ **rung ① owes both, and marks only the surprises.** a plain rule is **never omitted for obviousness**
— it is stated flatly and marked 🪨, so a lightwalker skims past in a glance. the surprises carry ⚡, so
a nightwalker knows which rows their intuition cannot carry them through.

### ⛔ the trap: a reader-relative test silently bounds a reader-independent one

⚠️ **the worked example shipped without its plain rules, and the cause was structural rather than
careless.** rung ①'s original single test was *"did a source contradict a competent reader's belief?"*
— which is **reader-relative**. rung ② is enforced to trace into rung ①. so ②'s **reader-independent**
test (*"defeated whatever else you do?"*) was narrowed by ①'s without a word said: ⛔ **a rule nobody
was wrong about could not enter, however large it was.**

⭐ **the failure mode generalizes past any one subject: a catalogue's blind spot is proportional to its
authors' expertise.** a research pass is run by people who have just read the instrument, so what is
obvious *to them* is invisible *to it* — and that is exactly what a newcomer walks into.

⚠️ **and the plain half is the one you cannot sweep for**, because the sweep it needs is *"what does
this demand of someone who has never heard of it?"* — a question an author cannot honestly ask after a
week with the text. ⭐ **the only real instrument is a genuinely uninitiated reader.** absent one, a
projection of the domain's own **trigger** material (*does this instrument reach me at all?*) is the
best available substitute, and it should be labelled as the substitute it is.

the catalogue is fed by **two sweeps of the same instrument**:

| sweep | returns | ⚠️ the cost of the one you skip |
|-------|---------|-------------------------------|
| for what the source **demands** | ways you lose | you miss duties, and a demand letter eventually tells you |
| ⭐ for what the source **grants** | exemptions, thresholds, safe harbours, cure windows | ⛔ **silent** — an unknown protection announces itself **never**, and you over-reserve forever |

⚠️ **the second sweep is the one a hazard pass loses by default**, because a sweep returns what it was
sent to find. it is the half that pays for the exercise.

⛔ **both sweeps land in rung ①, as groups of one catalogue — never as two files.** an earlier cut of
the worked example gave the grants half its own file, on the claim that a protection is a fact you
**have** rather than a condition you must **prove**. ⚠️ **that claim was false on its own entries** —
every protection carried conditions the reader has to establish. the entries were folded back as a
group, because a false belief about *escape* is the same species of fact as a false belief about
*exposure*, and the rung's admission test does not distinguish them.

### ② redlines — the lines

one question admits a line, and no other:

> **if you cross this line, are you defeated *whatever else you do*?**

| answer | verdict |
|--------|---------|
| yes — **the text forbids it** | ⛔ a **hard** redline |
| yes — **your defense becomes unprovable** | ⛔ a **proof** redline |
| yes — **the claim itself is false**, so what is built on it inherits the error | ⛔ a **claim** redline |
| no — it raises risk, or it is one of several permitted options | ✅ **not a redline.** it is a judgment |

⭐ **the fourth row is what makes the file publishable.** wherever the sources permit a choice, the
redlines must **stay silent** — and that silence is the guarantee, not a gap.

### ③ mustcants — the todos

two axes, four cells, four different done-conditions. ⛔ **`CAN'T` means *unable*, never *forbidden*** —
under the forbidden reading the matrix collapses. see `define.the-mustcants-matrix` for the full
treatment, the degeneracy proof, and the procedure.

## ⭐ what each rung answers, and who reads it

⚠️ **the rungs are not drafts of one another. each answers a different question, and a reader who
holds only one lacks what the others carry.**

| the reader's question | the rung | ⛔ what they lose if they read only it |
|-----------------------|----------|--------------------------------------|
| 🌑 *"is this even about me?"* | ① landmines, the 🪨 rows | ⭐ **the orientation every other row presumes** — without it a correction has no belief to correct |
| *"what would I have gotten wrong?"* | ① landmines, the ⚡ rows | no idea which errors are **fatal** vs merely costly |
| *"what defeats me outright?"* | ② redlines | ⛔ a clean sheet reads as a **certificate**, which it is not |
| *"what do I do on monday?"* | ③ mustcants | the **why** behind each todo, and the authority under it |

⭐ **and one pathology is specific to a partial read of ①.** a hazard catalogue read without its
protection mirror leads a reader to **over-reserve** against rules that never reached them. the
catalogue's own header must say so, because the reader cannot see the omission from inside.

## ⭐ the trace chain, and what it proves

```
  a todo  ──cites──▶  a line  ──cites──▶  a fact  ──quotes──▶  the source
```

⛔ **a break anywhere in that chain is a specific, nameable defect:**

| the break | what it means | severity |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| a fact with no source quote | an assertion from recall | ⛔ **blocker** |
| a line with no fact behind it | ⭐ **invented caution** — our preference in a floor's clothes | ⛔ **blocker** |
| a todo with no line behind it | ⭐ **invented work** — the same defect one rung up | ⛔ **blocker** |
| a fact that produced no line, or a line that produced no todo | ✅ **fine, and expected** — the filters are lossy by design | ✅ none |

⭐ **note the asymmetry, because it is the whole shape of the ladder.** the chain must be complete
**upward** and is expected to be incomplete **downward**. a rung is a filter, not a translation.

---

## ⚠️ the ladder's bound, stated once and inherited by every rung

**each rung is only as good as the rung below it, and rung ① is only as good as what was swept.**

⛔ **an absence anywhere on the ladder is not a clearance.** in the worked example this is measured
rather than hypothetical: a **second** sweep of paragraphs the first pass never asked for added **10
facts**, moved the line count from **29 to 43**, and **contradicted a hard line the file had already
published**.

⭐ **so the sweep is not a one-time step.** its yield is a function of **what remains unread**, which
the first sweep does not measure — and a method that presents it as done-once will systematically
under-run it.

## key takeaways

| question | answer |
|----------|--------|
| ⭐ what is the ladder in one line? | **facts → lines → todos**, each rung a lossy projection of the one below |
| ⭐ what does each step **lose**, and why is that good? | ①→② drops everything that is not fatal, which excludes **appetite** by construction; ②→③ drops the **order**, which keeps it from becoming a plan |
| ⛔ can you skip a rung? | **no.** sources → todos is a plan: judgment you never justified, with no column a reviewer can check |
| why separate files rather than one? | bundled, a fact and an imperative read alike, and **invented caution becomes invisible** |
| ⭐ which direction must the trace chain be complete? | **upward, always.** downward gaps are expected — a rung is a filter |
| what feeds rung ①? | ⭐ **two sweeps** of each instrument — for what it demands, and for what it grants |
| which sweep is silently expensive to skip? | the **grants** sweep: an unknown duty eventually announces itself; an unknown protection never does |
| ⭐ who is rung ① written for? | 🌞 **and** 🌑 — both walkers. every mine is enumerated; ⛔ **the plain ones are never omitted for obviousness**, and only the surprises carry ⚡ |
| ⛔ why does a catalogue lose its plain rules? | ⭐ **its blind spot is proportional to its authors' expertise.** what is obvious to someone who just read the instrument is invisible to the artifact they write |
| ⚠️ which admission test bounds which? | rung ①'s is **reader-relative** and rung ②'s is **reader-independent** — and because ② traces into ①, ①'s narrows ②'s **silently**. that is why the plain half must be an explicit half of ①'s test |
| ⛔ what does a complete ladder get you? | ⚠️ **not defeated** — never **compliant** |

## the honest gap

- ⬜ **the ladder has been climbed once, on one subject.** three rungs, 63 facts, 52 lines, 61 todos.
  one run is not evidence the rung boundaries are right for a domain with a different shape.
- ⛔ **the plain half of rung ① cannot be swept for, and no substitute is as good as the thing.** the
  sweep it needs is *"what does this demand of someone who has never heard of it?"*, which an author
  cannot honestly ask after a week with the instrument. ⚠️ **the worked example's 🪨 rows were
  assembled by a projection of its own trigger brief, not by a sweep** — a real one needs an
  uninitiated reader, and none was available. ⭐ **treat any plain-rule set as a floor, never a
  peak**, and prefer a genuinely new reader over any procedure proposed here.
- ⚠️ **rung ③ was added after rungs ① and ②, and the ladder was written after all three.** ⛔ **this
  brief is therefore a description of a path already walked, not a design validated by a second walk** —
  and a retrospective method reads more inevitable than it was.
- ⚠️ **the redline test has a soft edge**, inherited here: "defeated whatever else you do" is crisp for a
  forbidden act and softer for a proof failure, where the honest answer is often "defeated *if the
  question is ever litigated*."
- ⬜ **no method finds a rung-① fact nobody stepped on.** by construction the wrong-belief half surfaces
  only on contradiction; a blind second reader is the closest substitute and it is a different technique.
- ⬜ **the ladder says how to climb, never when to stop.** how many sweeps is enough is a spend decision,
  and it is left open on the same grounds every other order question is.

|------------------|-----------|---------|
| names a client, campaign, volume, vendor, schema, or config? | **no** — the worked example is a public-law subject and names no party | ✅ clean |
| discloses a choice the sources do not force? | ⚠️ **yes, and that is what a method brief IS** — the three rungs and their admission tests are **ours**. ⭐ they are choices about **how to write a document**, not about what any reader should do about their own risk | ✅ clean |
| discloses risk appetite? | ⛔ **no** — it prescribes **tests**, and expressly forbids every artifact it produces from any rank, sequence, or price | ✅ clean |

⭐ **the asymmetry that makes this publishable:** a method whose entire purpose is the **exclusion** of
appetite from a document is itself appetite-free.
