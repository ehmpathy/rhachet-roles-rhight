# define.the-mustcants-matrix

## .what

⭐ **the projection that turns a set of redlines into a set of todos without an order.**

a redline is a negative — *"never let a registry copy age past 31 days"*. a reader needs an action.
but the obvious move from lines to actions is a **plan**, and a plan ranks, sequences, and costs —
which is appetite, and appetite is what makes an artifact unpublishable.

the matrix is the way through. it sorts every line onto **two axes** and stops:

|  | **ESTABLISH** — make it true | **ELIMINATE** — make it not happen |
|---|---|---|
| **MUST** — you owe an action | 🔨 **`ME`** — build it, and be able to produce it | 🛑 **`MX`** — stop it |
| **CAN'T** — ⭐ **no action reaches it** | ⏰ **`CE`** — too late | 🧱 **`CX`** — no exit |

⭐ **a classification is not a sequence.** a reader gets four piles with four different done-conditions
and decides their own order — which is exactly the decision that was never ours to make.

⭐ **subject-agnostic.** the axes are about the *shape of an action*, not about law. any domain with a
body of authority — a regulation, an API contract, a protocol spec, a vendor agreement — projects onto
them the same way.

> **not legal advice.** informational groundwork for counsel to validate.

> ⚠️ **a note on gerunds.** this file trips `rule.forbid.gerunds` on terms of art quoted from the
> worked example (`telemarketing`, `advertising`).

## ⛔ `CAN'T` means **cannot**, never **may not**

⚠️ **this is settled first because the word earns a misread and the misread destroys the matrix.**
*"we can't run a program that spams people"* is a natural sentence, and it uses `CAN'T` in the
**forbidden** sense. that is **not** this row.

⭐ **the two senses were tested against the grid rather than chosen by taste:**

| under `CAN'T` = **may not** (forbidden) | the result |
|----------------------------------------|-----------|
| ⏰ *can't establish* a prohibited program | ⛔ **identical to** 🛑 `MX` — "never build it" and "stop running it" are one duty at two moments, not two buckets |
| 🧱 *can't eliminate* … | ⛔ **meaningless** — "you are forbidden to stop" names no duty any source imposes |

| under `CAN'T` = **cannot** (impossible) | the result |
|-----------------------------------------|-----------|
| ⏰ *can't establish* the record for an act already past | ✅ distinct, and a class no other rung of the ladder holds |
| 🧱 *can't eliminate* a liability the source assigns to you | ✅ distinct, and the class readers spend money against |

⛔ **the prohibition reading degenerates a 2×2 into a 1×2; the impossibility reading keeps four live
cells.** so `CAN'T` is glossed as *unable*, and 🛑 `MX` is where every "you may not" lands.

⚠️ **and "establish" is overloaded too, in a way that matters.** a source that says *"established and
implemented"* means **build**. litigation that says "establish an element" means **prove**. ⭐ a 🔨 `ME`
todo is not done until **both** senses are satisfied — which is why every `ME` done-condition is
written as a **production**, never as a build.

## the four cells

| cell | the action | ⭐ what "done" means | what it costs you to misfile |
|------|-----------|--------------------|------------------------------|
| 🔨 **`ME`** | build an artifact, and hold it | you can **produce** it on demand | you build the thing and cannot show it |
| 🛑 **`MX`** | cease a practice | the practice is **not able to run** | you layer a control on top instead of removal, and the practice survives |
| ⏰ **`CE`** | ⭐ **act before a moment, or never** | ⛔ **"done" is unavailable afterward** | you plan to do it later, and later does not exist |
| 🧱 **`CX`** | ⭐ **bound it and budget it** | ⛔ **never done** — a residual, not a task | you spend on a fix that cannot work, and stay exposed |

⭐ **the two `CAN'T` cells are why the matrix is worth running.** the `MUST` cells restate the redlines
in a different mood — useful, not new. the `CAN'T` cells name a class the redlines do not isolate:
**false beliefs about escape**. in the worked example that is precisely where the landmine catalogue
clusters hardest, and it had no home until the projection was run.

### ⚠️ two kinds of `CE`, and the file must not blur them

| kind | why it cannot be established | what is still open |
|------|-----------------------------|--------------------|
| **a moment passed** | the act is in the past and the artifact was contemporaneous | the 🔨 `ME` that precedes it, for future acts |
| ⬜ **the research has a hole** | the standard is unconstrued, or a term's boundary was never located | ⬜ **research** — it may be perfectly establishable by a reader who reads what you did not |

⛔ **to file the second as the first tells a reader a door is shut when it is merely unopened.**

## how to run it

### step 1 — take one line, ask the modality

> **is there an action available that reaches this?**

- yes → **MUST** row
- no → **CAN'T** row

### step 2 — ask the verb

> **does the action make something true, or make something not happen?**

- true → **ESTABLISH** column
- not happen → **ELIMINATE** column

### step 3 — write the done-condition, and make it verifiable

⛔ **this is the step that makes the artifact tactical rather than a restatement.** a bucket without a
done-condition is a mood, not a todo. the condition must be checkable by someone who was not there:

| cell | the shape the condition takes |
|------|------------------------------|
| 🔨 `ME` | **a production** — "for any single X you can produce the artifact that…" |
| 🛑 `MX` | **an incapacity** — "no path can reach…" |
| ⏰ `CE` | **the moment that closed**, plus the `ME` that is still open |
| 🧱 `CX` | ⛔ **an explicit statement that there is no done** |

### step 4 — trace by ID, exactly as the redlines trace to the catalogue

⛔ **a todo with no line behind it is invented work**, and it is the same defect as a redline with no
landmine behind it. the trace column is what makes it visible at a glance.

### step 5 — collect the silences

wherever the sources permit more than one design, the matrix must **stay silent** — and say so
explicitly, in a table of open questions. ⭐ **the silences table is the audit trail** that the
admission rule was applied. a matrix with no silences has been padded with preference.

⛔ **build order is itself a silence.** a sequence is a spend decision, and a spend decision is
appetite. ⏰ `CE` is the only bucket with a clock, and that clock is in the **source** rather than in
your preference — which is exactly why it is admissible.

## ⭐ the columns are not independent, and the overlap is a lifecycle

`ME1` and `CE1` in the worked example are **the same object at two moments**: build the record
(greenfield), and you cannot build it after the act (brownfield).

⚠️ **that is not a defect to normalize away — it is the matrix's most useful property**, because it
gives two readers two different entry points:

| the reader | reads first | why |
|------------|-------------|-----|
| on a **new** system | the **MUST** column | every window is still open; the whole sheet is available |
| with **work already done** | ⭐ the **CAN'T** column | it names the repairs that no longer exist, **before** they spend on one |

⛔ **a plan cannot serve both readers, because a plan has one order.** a classification serves both,
because each reader picks their own entry.

---

## what the matrix does **not** do

1. ⛔ **it does not rank.** no cell outranks another, and no row within a cell is first.
2. ⛔ **it does not price.** how much to reserve against a 🧱 `CX` is appetite and stays a silence.
3. ⛔ **it does not add.** every todo is a projection of a line you already held — the matrix changes
   the **mood**, never the **content**. a todo with an empty trace column is a defect.
4. ⚠️ **it does not certify.** a full sheet means **not defeated**, never **compliant** — the same
   caveat the redlines carry, inherited unchanged.

## key takeaways

| question | answer |
|----------|--------|
| ⭐ what problem does the matrix solve? | a reader needs actions; the obvious move to actions is a **plan**, and a plan ranks. the matrix gives actions **without** an order |
| ⭐ what are the two axes? | **must/can't** (is an action available?) × **establish/eliminate** (make it true, or make it not happen) |
| ⛔ what does `CAN'T` mean? | **unable** — never *forbidden*. under the forbidden reading ⏰ `CE` duplicates 🛑 `MX` and 🧱 `CX` is meaningless |
| where do prohibitions go? | 🛑 `MX`. "never build it" and "stop running it" are one duty at two moments |
| ⭐ what makes a bucket a **todo** rather than a mood? | the **done-condition**, and its verifiability by someone who was not there |
| ⛔ what is the done-condition for 🔨 `ME`? | a **production** — never a build. a program you cannot evidence is the classic failure |
| ⛔ what is the done-condition for 🧱 `CX`? | there is none. it is a residual; the moves are bound and budget |
| why is the greenfield/brownfield overlap a feature? | it gives two readers two entry points, which a single-order plan cannot |
| what is the proof the admission rule was applied? | ⭐ **the silences table** — a matrix with no silences has been padded |

## the honest gap

- ⬜ **this projection has been run once**, on one subject, over one set of 52 lines. the four cells
  were populated and none came out empty, which is weak evidence they are the right four — a set of
  lines that left a cell empty would be the interesting test, and it has not been met.
- ⚠️ **the `CAN'T` gloss is a repair, not a clean name.** the honest statement is that the word earns
  its misread and the gloss must be read before the buckets are used. a label that needs a paragraph of
  defense is a label with a defect; it was kept because ⏰ `CE` and 🧱 `CX` read cleanly at the cell
  level and no better row-name was found.
- ⬜ **the modality question in step 1 has a soft edge.** "is an action available?" is crisp for a past
  act and softer where an action exists but is disproportionate. the matrix has no test for that case,
  and would file it under **MUST** — which may overstate what is genuinely open.
- ⚠️ **the two kinds of ⏰ `CE` are distinguished by judgment, not by a rule.** "the moment passed"
  and "the research has a hole" are separated on inspection; no test is proposed.
- ⬜ **no test distinguishes a 🧱 `CX` from a 🔨 `ME` nobody has thought of yet.** a residual is
  recorded because no control **we know of** reaches it, which is an argument from absence — the same
  bound the catalogue underneath it carries.

| discloses a choice the sources do not force? | ⚠️ **yes, and that is what a method brief IS** — the two axes and the done-condition discipline are **ours**. ⭐ but they are choices about **how to write a document**, not about what any reader should do about their own risk | ✅ clean |
| discloses risk appetite? | ⛔ **no, and this is the check the matrix exists to pass.** it prescribes a **classification** and expressly forbids the artifact it produces from any rank, sequence, or price | ✅ clean |

document is itself appetite-free. the moment this brief said "and do the 🔨 `ME` rows first", it would
fail its own third row.
