# howto.protect-when-hire-contractor

## .what

complete protection stack for homeowners when they hire contractors — from verification before hire through final payment structure.

## .why

- most homeowners check license and stop there
- each mechanism protects against a different failure mode
- layered protection means no single point of failure
- the order matters: verify before hire, gate payments to milestones
- one absent layer can cost $5,000-100,000+

---

## the protection stack

| # | mechanism | protects against | when to verify |
|---|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| 1 | licensed | incompetence, no recourse | before hire |
| 2 | bonded | abandonment, theft | before hire |
| 3 | insured (GL) | property damage, third-party injury | before hire |
| 4 | insured (workers comp) | worker injury lawsuits against you | before hire |
| 5 | additional insured | defense costs hit your policy | before work starts |
| 6 | written contract | scope disputes, "he said / she said" | before work starts |
| 7 | permit-gate | pay before paperwork, contractor vanishes | before first payment |
| 8 | inspection-gate | pay for work that fails code | while work proceeds |
| 9 | lien waivers | sub liens your house after you paid GC | with each payment |
| 10 | final holdback | no leverage for punchlist | at project end |

## layer 1: licensed

**what it enables**:

| benefit | why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| board complaint | leverage to force completion or refund |
| bond access | direct recovery without lawsuit |
| competence signal | passed exam, met requirements |
| permit eligibility | can pull permits legally |
| insurance requirement | most jurisdictions require insurance for license |

**how to verify**:

| jurisdiction | where to check |
|--------------|----------------|
| Indianapolis (electrical) | [Indy.gov Contractor Licenses](https://www.indy.gov/activity/contractor-licenses) |
| Indiana (general) | [Indiana Professional License Agency](https://www.in.gov/pla/) |
| other states | state contractor license board website |

**what to check**:

| item | red flag |
|------|----------|
| license status | expired, suspended, revoked |
| license type | wrong trade for your work |
| disciplinary history | prior complaints, actions |
| insurance on file | expired or none |

---

## layer 2: bonded

**what it enables**:

| benefit | why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| direct recovery | surety pays you, not contractor |
| no lawsuit needed | file claim, get paid |
| no collectability risk | surety company is solvent |
| faster than court | 30-60 days vs 90-180+ |

**typical bond amounts**:

| contractor type | typical bond |
|-----------------|--------------|
| electrical | $5,000-15,000 |
| plumber | $5,000-15,000 |
| HVAC | $5,000-15,000 |
| general contractor | $10,000-25,000 |

**how to verify**: ask contractor for bond certificate, or call license office.

**limitation**: bond may not cover full loss on large projects.

---

## layer 3: insured (general liability)

**what it covers**:

| scenario | GL pays |
|----------|---------|
| contractor damages your property | yes |
| contractor damages neighbor's property | yes |
| third party injured by contractor's work | yes |
| your visitor injured by completed work (later) | yes (completed operations) |

**minimum to require**:

| coverage | minimum |
|----------|---------|
| per occurrence | $1,000,000 |
| aggregate | $2,000,000 |
| completed operations | included |

**how to verify**: request certificate of insurance (COI) — not just a statement that they're insured.

---

## layer 4: insured (workers compensation)

**what it covers**:

| scenario | workers comp pays |
|----------|-------------------|
| contractor's employee injured on your property | yes |
| subcontractor's employee injured | yes (if sub has coverage) |

**why you need this**:

| if contractor lacks workers comp | consequence |
|---------------------------------|-------------|
| worker injured on your property | worker may sue YOU |
| your homeowners policy | may not cover (business activity exclusion) |
| personal liability | your assets at risk |

**how to verify**: request workers comp certificate of insurance.

**exception**: sole proprietors may be exempt from workers comp requirement — but this shifts risk to you.

---

## layer 5: additional insured

**what it does**:

| without additional insured | with additional insured |
|---------------------------|------------------------|
| lawsuit names you + contractor | same |
| your homeowners policy defends you | contractor's GL defends you |
| defense costs hit YOUR policy | defense costs hit THEIR policy |
| YOUR premiums rise | their premiums rise |
| claim on YOUR record | claim on their record |

**defense costs (even if you win)**:

| dispute value | defense cost |
|---------------|--------------|
| $250K | $90-150K |
| $500K | $150-250K |
| $1M+ | $200-500K+ |

**how to get it**:

| step | action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | include clause in contract (see below) |
| 2 | request certificate that shows you as additional insured |
| 3 | verify your property address is listed |
| 4 | confirm "primary and non-contributory" language |

**contract clause**:

> "Contractor shall name Owner as additional insured on Contractor's commercial general liability policy. Contractor's insurance shall be primary and non-contributory. Contractor shall provide certificate of insurance prior to start of work."

---

## layer 6: written contract

**what it proves**:

| element | protects against |
|---------|------------------|
| scope of work | "that wasn't included" disputes |
| price | price creep, "I said $X not $Y" |
| timeline | endless delays |
| payment schedule | disputes over what's owed when |
| change order process | unauthorized additions |
| warranty | "not my problem" after completion |

**minimum contract elements**:

| section | content |
|---------|---------|
| parties | your name, contractor's legal name + license # |
| scope | detailed description of work |
| price | total price, what's included, what's extra |
| payment schedule | tied to milestones (see below) |
| timeline | start date, completion date, delay penalties |
| permits | who pulls, who pays |
| insurance | requirements + additional insured |
| change orders | must be written and signed |
| warranty | duration and what's covered |
| dispute resolution | mediation before litigation |
| termination | conditions and process |

---

## layer 7: permit-gate

**the rule**: no payment until permit is filed.

| without permit-gate | with permit-gate |
|--------------------|------------------|
| pay deposit | contractor vanishes | pay deposit | contractor files permit |
| no paper trail | | permit = commitment |
| hard to prove work existed | | public record of project |

**why it works**:

| factor | effect |
|--------|--------|
| permit costs contractor time | shows commitment |
| permit is public record | proves project started |
| permit ties contractor to work | can't deny involvement |
| permit required for inspection | forces completion path |

**implementation**:

| contract language | effect |
|-------------------|--------|
| "deposit due upon permit issuance" | gates first payment |
| "contractor shall provide permit number within 5 business days of deposit" | deadline |

---

## layer 8: inspection-gate

**the rule**: progress payments tied to passed inspections.

| inspection | payment released |
|------------|------------------|
| permit issued | 10% (deposit) |
| rough-in inspection passed | 30% |
| final inspection passed | 50% |
| punchlist complete | 10% |

**why it works**:

| factor | effect |
|--------|--------|
| city inspector verifies work | independent third party |
| contractor can't fake progress | inspection is pass/fail |
| you never pay for failed work | inspection gates payment |
| contractor motivated to pass | money tied to outcome |

**what inspections cover**:

| trade | rough-in inspection | final inspection |
|-------|---------------------|------------------|
| electrical | wires before drywall | fixtures, panel, function |
| plumber | pipes before drywall | fixtures, pressure test |
| HVAC | ducts before drywall | equipment, function |
| structural | frame before drywall | completion |

## layer 9: lien waivers

**the problem**:

```
YOU ──pay $20K──▶ GENERAL CONTRACTOR
                        │
                        ▼
                 GC doesn't pay ──▶ ELECTRICIAN ($5K)
                                          │
                                          ▼
                              ELECTRICIAN LIENS YOUR HOUSE
                                          │
                                          ▼
                              YOU PAY AGAIN OR FIGHT IN COURT
```

**the fix**: lien waivers with each payment.

| payment | lien waiver required |
|---------|---------------------|
| deposit | conditional waiver from GC |
| progress payment | unconditional waiver for prior payment + conditional for current |
| final payment | unconditional waiver from GC + all subs |

**lien waiver types**:

| type | when used | effect |
|------|-----------|--------|
| conditional | with payment | waives lien rights IF check clears |
| unconditional | after payment clears | waives lien rights permanently |

**who signs**:

| party | why |
|-------|-----|
| general contractor | for their work |
| major subcontractors | electrical, plumber, HVAC |
| material suppliers | lumber yard, supply house |

**how to implement**:

| step | action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | contract requires lien waivers with each payment |
| 2 | ask GC for list of subs and suppliers |
| 3 | require waivers from all parties over $500 |
| 4 | no payment without signed waivers |

---

## layer 10: final holdback

**the rule**: retain 10% until punchlist complete.

**why it works**:

| without holdback | with holdback |
|------------------|---------------|
| contractor "done," leaves scratches, absent trim | contractor returns to finish |
| you have no leverage | 10% = motivation |
| small claims for $500 of fixes | "finish punchlist, get final check" |

**punchlist process**:

| step | action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | walkthrough with contractor |
| 2 | written list of deficiencies |
| 3 | contractor fixes items |
| 4 | re-walkthrough |
| 5 | sign-off and final payment |

## the complete payment flow

```
BEFORE HIRE
   │
   ├─ verify license ✓
   ├─ verify bond ✓
   ├─ verify GL insurance ✓
   ├─ verify workers comp ✓
   └─ request additional insured ✓
         │
         ▼
CONTRACT SIGNED
   │
   ├─ scope, price, timeline ✓
   ├─ payment schedule ✓
   ├─ insurance requirements ✓
   └─ lien waiver requirements ✓
         │
         ▼
PERMIT FILED ─────────────────▶ pay 10% deposit
   │                            + conditional lien waiver
   │
   ▼
ROUGH-IN INSPECTION PASSES ───▶ pay 30%
   │                            + unconditional waiver (prior)
   │                            + conditional waiver (current)
   │
   ▼
FINAL INSPECTION PASSES ──────▶ pay 50%
   │                            + unconditional waiver (prior)
   │                            + conditional waiver (current)
   │
   ▼
PUNCHLIST COMPLETE ───────────▶ pay 10% final
                                + unconditional waivers (all parties)
```

## what each layer protects against

| failure mode | layer that protects |
|--------------|---------------------|
| contractor incompetent | 1 (license) |
| contractor abandons job | 2 (bond), 7-8 (payment gates) |
| contractor damages property | 3 (GL insurance) |
| worker injured, sues you | 4 (workers comp) |
| third party injured, sues you | 3 (GL), 5 (additional insured) |
| defense costs hit your policy | 5 (additional insured) |
| scope disputes | 6 (contract) |
| contractor vanishes before start | 7 (permit-gate) |
| work fails inspection after you paid | 8 (inspection-gate) |
| sub liens your house | 9 (lien waivers) |
| contractor won't finish punchlist | 10 (final holdback) |

---

## pre-hire checklist

before you sign or pay:

- [ ] license verified (active, correct trade, no disciplinary actions)
- [ ] bond verified (certificate received, amount adequate)
- [ ] GL insurance verified (certificate received, $1M+ per occurrence)
- [ ] workers comp verified (certificate received, or sole proprietor acknowledgment)
- [ ] additional insured requested (your name + property address)
- [ ] written contract reviewed (scope, price, timeline, payment schedule)
- [ ] payment tied to permit + inspections
- [ ] lien waiver requirement in contract
- [ ] 10% final holdback in contract

---

## red flags: walk away

| red flag | what it signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| won't provide license number | unlicensed or suspended |
| no insurance certificate | uninsured |
| won't add you as additional insured | coverage problem |
| wants large deposit (>10%) | cash flow problem |
| wants payment before permit | may vanish |
| no written contract | disputes guaranteed |
| won't agree to inspection-tied payments | quality concern |
| refuses lien waivers | may not pay subs |
| pressure to decide today | concealed problem |
