# rule.require.friendly

## .what

> ⭐ **bring light to every party.**

every outbound message leaves the recipient **better off for having received
it** — not merely informed, not merely unburdened, but treated as a person whose
help was noticed and whose day this did not worsen.

operationally: thank them for what they actually did, offer your effort before
you ask for theirs, and phrase the ask as a request for help rather than a
demand for output.

## .why — the other three comms rules model the reader as a MACHINE

this is the fourth comms rule, and it is the one that **corrects** the others:

| rule | optimizes for |
|---|---|
| `rule.forbid.baggage` | a recipient who must **dispatch** the message |
| `rule.require.crisp` | a recipient with a **queue** |
| `rule.forbid.ai-tells` | a recipient who must **believe a human wrote it** |
| **this rule** | a recipient who is **a person** |

each of the three is correct, and all three descend from one premise: the reader
is a processor of volume, so the cheapest message wins. that premise is true and
it is **incomplete**.

a clerk, a specialist, a counterparty at an agency has discretion over:

- how fast they get to you
- whether they answer the question you asked or the question you should have
  asked
- whether they volunteer the route you did not know existed
- whether they take your call the fourth time

**none of that is purchased by brevity.** it is purchased by being someone they
do not mind to help.

> a message optimized only for dispatch cost is optimized for the one round in
> front of you. these matters run for **months**.

### 🎯 and the instrumental case is the SMALLER half

the paragraph above argues that warmth pays. it does, and that is not the reason.

a person on the other end of an agency line spends their day as the recipient of
other people's problems, most of them stated as demands. **you are one of the
few inputs to that day you control.** the sentence that acknowledges what they
did costs you four words and is, plausibly, the only one they receive that week.

⇒ so the rule is not *"be warm because it works."* it is *"be warm, and note
that it also works."* the order matters, because a warmth that is contingent on
payoff evaporates in exactly the round where it is worth most: the one where the
answer was **no**.

⚠️ **the test of this rule is a message sent to someone who cannot help you.**
a refusal answered warmly is the whole rule; a favor answered warmly is
manners.

### 🚩 the defect this rule exists to catch scores CLEAN on every other rule

measured: a draft to a franchise-tax clerk closed with *"What do you need from
me to verify 2023?"* — one job, payload first, no filler or em-dash; it passes
`crisp`, `baggage`, and `ai-tells` outright. it still reads as a **demand for
output**, to a clerk who had weeks earlier verified an asset figure on her own
initiative and opened the route that made the filing possible. the principal's
correction: *"can we be friendlier?"*

⇒ a defect every other rule scores clean needs its own rule.

## .the four moves

### 1. thank them for what they actually did

not a formula. a specific act, in one clause, with no date and no new fact.

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| (no thanks at all) | `Thanks again for verifying the assets.` |
| `Thank you for your assistance in this matter.` | `Thanks for opening that up.` |
| `Thank you for the asset verification on 7/20.` | `Thanks again for verifying the assets.` |

⭐ **`again` is worth its one word.** it marks their help as a pattern rather
than a transaction, and it costs you no line and them no reply.

⚠️ do not over-specify. a file note needs a date; a person does not. she knows
what she did. see `rule.forbid.baggage` § *do not over-specify the thanks*.

### 2. offer before you ask

put your effort on the table first. it is what converts a question into a
request for help.

| 👎 | 👍 |
|---|---|
| `What do you need from me?` | `Happy to send over whatever you need. What would help?` |
| `Please advise on next steps.` | `Glad to pull together whatever is useful. Where should I start?` |

⚠️ the offer must be **real**. a false offer is worse than none, and they will
take you up on it.

### 3. ask as a request for help, not a demand for output

the same open question, one grammatical notch softer. the information content is
identical; the posture is not.

| 👎 demand | 👍 request |
|---|---|
| `What do you need from me to verify 2023?` | `What would help get 2023 verified?` |
| `When will this be processed?` | `Any sense of the timeline on your end?` |
| `Send me the form.` | `Could you point me at the right form?` |

⛔ **do not soften it into a yes/no.** `Is there anything you need?` invites
`no`, which is an answer that helps nobody. keep the question open; change only
the posture.

### 4. close warm

`Thanks,` above the name. one line, asks for no act, and it is what a person
writes.

## 🎯 friendly is not LONGER

this is the objection, and the worked pair answers it:

```
👎  What do you need from me to verify 2023?                          (9 words)

👍  Happy to send over whatever you need. What would help get 2023
    verified?                                                        (13 words)
```

**four words.** `rule.require.crisp` charges a tax on length; this is four words
of it, and it buys the difference between a clerk who answers your question and
a clerk who tells you the item you did not know to ask about.

⚠️ so the rules are not in tension at the scale they actually operate. `crisp`
cuts **paragraphs** — the throat-clear, the restatement, the wind-up. friendly
adds **a clause**. cut the paragraph and keep the clause.

## ⛔ the bound — friendly is not SOFT

a warm message still says the hard thing.

| ✅ friendly | 🚫 soft |
|---|---|
| states the figure you computed | hedges the figure to avoid a disagreement |
| names the statute | omits the statute to seem agreeable |
| asks the question that will be unwelcome | asks an easier question instead |
| holds the position | concedes it to keep the tone pleasant |

⇒ **warmth is in the frame, never in the content.** to drop a true fact because
it might land badly is not friendliness; it is a second defect, and it is the one
that costs the matter.

## ⚠️ and friendly is not FAMILIAR

these are agency correspondents and counterparties, not friends. the register is
**warm and professional**:

| ✅ | 🚫 |
|---|---|
| `Thanks again for verifying the assets.` | `You're a lifesaver!` |
| `Happy to send over whatever you need.` | `Hope you're having a great week!!` |
| `Thanks,` | `Cheers!` · `Best!!` |
| a plain, warm sentence | an exclamation mark |

⚠️ excess warmth reads as manipulation, and `rule.forbid.ai-tells` catches most
of it anyway — *"I hope this finds you well"* is a throat-clear, not
friendliness.

## .the test

> **would I mind to receive this?**

then the sharper one, which catches the case above:

> **does this message notice that they did something for me?**

a message that asks for a second favor without acknowledgement of the first is
the defect this rule names, and it is invisible to every other check.

## .the pre-send check gains a line

`rule.forbid.baggage` § *every comms draft carries a PRE-SEND CHECK block*
already carries a gratitude line. this rule adds the second half:

```md
- [ ] **the ask is a request, not a demand** — offer before you ask; `what would
      help` over `what do you need from me`. (`rule.require.friendly`)
```

⚠️ copy the block, never compose it fresh. a list derived on each occasion is a
different list each time.

## .enforcement

- a message to a named human who did you a service, with no thanks = **blocker**
- an ask phrased as a demand for output where a request would serve = **blocker**
- a true fact dropped to keep the tone pleasant = **blocker** (that is soft, not
  friendly, and it is the costlier defect)
- an ask softened into a yes/no question = **blocker** (it invites `no`)
- a warm close omitted = **nitpick**
- exclamation marks, or familiarity past a warm-professional register = **nitpick**

## .see also

- `rule.forbid.baggage` — what the message is **about**. its gratitude section is
  this rule's ancestor: it was written when a draft cut a thank-you as "a second
  topic" and the principal restored it, with *"nah we should still show
  gratitude. that's the human touch."*
- `rule.require.crisp` — how **short** it is. friendly adds a clause, never a
  paragraph; the two are compatible at the scale each operates
- `rule.forbid.ai-tells` — note the overlap: several formulas this rule would
  reject (*"I hope this finds you well"*) are already cut there as throat-clears.
  ⚠️ warmth is a **specific** act acknowledged, never a formula recited
