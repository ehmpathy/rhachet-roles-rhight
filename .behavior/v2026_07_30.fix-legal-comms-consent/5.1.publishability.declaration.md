## ⚠️ the leak vector this engagement is most exposed to — **test 4, voice**

this is the finding that most changes how phase 1 must be authored, and the vision did not have it.

the vision is written **end to end in advisory voice.** its assumption **A5** fixes the audience as
*"a company that sends"*; the wish itself says *"you as the corp"*. the result is exactly the shape
`rule.require.fullsun-facts-not-tactics` → `.the voice test` flags:

- **one repeat protagonist** — "the sender", present in every section
- **that protagonist always lands safe** — every edge case ends on what the sender should do
- **the briefs cross-reference into one design** — the proposed tree was explicitly a system

> the rule's own test: *"strip the citations from the whole set — can a reader still name the design
> the author recommends?"* applied to the vision's proposed tree, **yes** — a reader recovers *"capture
> written consent per number, honor revocation inside ten days, retain past the limitations period,
> and buy coverage outside your CGL."* that is an integrated design.

**so the author mandate for every phase-1 brief:**

| do | do not |
|----|--------|
| state **where the law draws the line** | state **what a sender should do** |
| use a **generic or conditional subject** — "a caller who…", "a message that…" | carry "the sender" as a standing protagonist |
| describe **both sides symmetrically** — the permitted side and the prohibited side get equal treatment | land every example on the safe side |
| keep `.see also` **navigational** — "related law lives here" | stitch the briefs into one recommended build |
| present the safe harbors as **law that exists** | present them as the target to build toward (that is `O-4`) |

⚠️ note the tension this creates with the wish. the wisher asked *"how to prove consent?"* — a
question posed in advisory voice, which invites an advisory answer. **the fullsun set answers the
terrain half; the runbook half is `O-1`/`O-3` and does not ship in daylight.** the vision already
named this as `.what is awkward` #2; this declaration makes it a hard boundary rather than a
discomfort.

## how phase 2 will falsify this

hand a fresh reader **only** the `☀️ fullsun` briefs — no route file, no wish, no vision — and ask:

1. what business does this company run, and what is it about to do?
2. **what did the author decide to do about the law?** — the applied design, the chosen structure
3. name the record schema, the retention period, and the channel design the author has in mind

| the red-team recovers… | verdict |
|------------------------|---------|
| only `F-1`–`F-8` — the statutes, the boundaries, the coverage gap | ✅ the scrub held |
| any of `O-1`–`O-4` — a schema, a runbook, the synthesis, a safe-harbor pick | ⛔ **LEAK — blocker.** redact per `howto.redact-obscure-to-legal-facts`, re-run |
| the **integrated design** — "a sender lands safe by X, Y, Z" — with no single tactic sentence | ⛔ **LEAK — blocker.** the set is in advisory voice; rewrite to neutral, re-run |
| any of `P-1`–`P-3` | ⛔ **LEAK — blocker**, and a signal that **Q3** was answered wrong |

**one pass is rarely enough.** each leak found is a re-scrub and a re-run.
