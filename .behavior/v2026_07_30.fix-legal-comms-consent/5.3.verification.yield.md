## coverage — the gate reaches every brief the peer rubrics could not

`briefs.integrity` exists because the automated peer rubrics glob `**/*.{ts,sh}`, so no rubric ever
reads a markdown brief. run over the whole roles tree (the wired gate): **216 briefs reached, 0

0 blockers, 10 nitpicks** — all the same check, `source-anchor-unreferenced`.

### the 10 nitpicks — anchor-extractor artifacts, not pads

`asSourceAnchor` derives a citation anchor from each source title, then looks for it in a body
sentence. all 10 hits trace to **two** case titles whose anchor the extractor mis-derives:

| source title | anchor emitted | why it misfires |
|--------------|----------------|-----------------|
| `Facebook, Inc. v. Duguid` | `Inc. v.` | the regex stops at the comma and takes the corporate suffix — bodies cite this as **"Duguid"** |
| `Insurance Marketing Coalition Ltd. v. FCC` | `Insurance Marketing Coalition Ltd. v.` | the full four-word firm name; bodies cite it as **"the 2025 vacatur"** / **"IMC"** |

verified in situ: `hazard.unprovable-consent-record.[hazard].md:249-250` lists both as sources 6 and 7,
and both are discussed in the body under their short forms. **not citation pads** — the case is cited,
the extractor's anchor is a weak guess. nitpick-severity by design; the wired gate enforces blockers
only, so these do not gate the build.

⇒ **caught as a follow-up, not fixed here:** to tighten `asSourceAnchor` so it skips a corporate-suffix
anchor (`Inc. v.`, `Ltd. v.`) is a checker-heuristic change that needs its own clamp and shifts
anchors elsewhere — not a blocker, and better done as a deliberate checker improvement than churned
mid-buttonup.

## ⭐ the reader-walkthrough — the wish's critical path, scored (the r10-deferred gate)

r10 deferred `has-critical-paths-frictionless` / `has-ergonomics-validated` to this stone. the
walkthrough: the vision's headline usecase is a founder about to launch an SMS campaign who needs
*is it lawful · can I prove it · what does it cost · am I covered*. the path is **open the front door
→ find the axis → read the redline → check the citation.**

| step | the reader does | what they hit | friction? |
|------|-----------------|---------------|-----------|
| **1. open** | opens `_.start-here.[summary].md` (the registered front door) | leads with *"⭐ the point to carry away first"* — benefit-first, no preamble | ✅ none — the answer is above the fold |
| **2. find the axis** | looks for *"can I contact this person?"* | a four-row table — `§ 227(b)` / `§ 227(c)` / the multiplier / the cost — each with "what defeats it" **and** "what does NOT" | ✅ none — the axis is a table, not prose; the ⚠️ on row 2 pre-empts the single most common misread |
| **3. read the redline** | wants the minimum to not get sued | *"the floor we found — six layers"*, each with its **authority** column and a **full detail** pointer | ✅ none — each layer is one row, cites its statute, links its deep brief; the ⛔ "a floor, not a guarantee" sets honest expectation |
| **4. check the citation** | wants to confirm layer 1 | row 1 cites `47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(f)(9)` → `ref.tcpa-consent-elements.[ref].md`; the see-also target **opens** (machine-verified: `see-also-target-absent` = 0 blockers tree-wide); the brief embeds the official eCFR URL + effective-date stamp | ✅ none — the pointer resolves, the source is official and dated |
