# case=10 — a review that skipped or crashed fails closed (C11 · sharp · critipath)

## narrative

a clone runs `git commit` the morning after a tool upgrade. the commit is refused: `🦅 the review could not run`. the cause line reads `no logs: line — the review skipped, or crashed before it wrote logs`, and the tail under it shows the crash: the brain SDK had no key. the hint below names the keyring the review uses and the command to fill it: `rhx keyrack set --owner ehmpath --key ANTHROPIC_API_KEY --env prep`. the clone asks its human to fill the key; the next commit's review runs, writes its json, and lands. a review that printed `0 blockers` and nothing else was never trusted as clean. (seen live on this branch, 2026-10-27.)
