# 🌙 dream: a brain account out of credit is named as such, not as an absent key

- kind: defect
- owner: rhight / transcriber pii gate
- caught 2026-10-27

## .the cue that fired
the done commit of fix-pii-verdict-reads-json ran the gate on a key whose account had no credit. `rhx review` crashed before it wrote logs with `BadRequestError: 400 … Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API`. the gate classed it `key` and printed "most often the review brain has no key … unlock prep … keyrack set", a fix that cannot help.

## .the gap, precisely
`get_verdict_of_review` sends every no-logs crash to the `key` kind (`RETRY key`). the evidence tail already holds the provider's own words, but no kind reads them.

## .the shape of the fix
- a `credit` kind: a no-logs crash whose tail matches `credit balance is too low|insufficient_quota|payment required` is FIXED (a retry cannot help), with the hint "the brain account has no credit; top it up, or point the gate at a funded key"
- a clamp: a fake `rhx` that dies with that 400 text before logs; assert the `credit` hint, and that no `keyrack unlock` line prints

## .why it is not done in this round
the route is complete and the commit is in flight; a new cause kind is a contract change to the malfunction report, with its own snapshots.
